# Visual Strategy — fiber-patient-education.html
## williamriveromd.com | Dr. W. G. M. Rivero, MD

### Guide URL
https://www.williamriveromd.com/guides/fiber-patient-education

### Audience
Filipino patients (general public), ages 30–70, many with diabetes, hypertension,
or CKD. Mid-health-literacy. Reads on mobile. Culturally anchored to rice-based diet.

### Visual Goals
1. **Interest** — Arrest the scroll. Hero must feel cinematic and personal.
2. **Comprehension** — Biology diagrams must reduce cognitive load, not increase it.
3. **Retention** — Food matrix and daily-target builder are reference images patients return to.

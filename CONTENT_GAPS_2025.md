## How Topics Were Scored

Each topic was cross-referenced against three independent sources:
- **Clinical agent** — peer-reviewed evidence, guidelines, FDA approvals 2024–2026
- **Gap agent** — systematic audit of all 107 existing guides for depth & coverage
- **Social agent** — Filipino patient communities, Facebook groups, TikTok, news

A topic appearing in **2+ sources** = Tier 1 (build now). Single-source high-impact = Tier 2.

## TIER 1 — Build Now (Zero or near-zero coverage, confirmed from 2+ sources)

### 1. PhilHealth 156-Session Hemodialysis Benefit — The Practical Navigation Guide
**Suggested slug:** `philhealth-hd-156-guide.html`
- PhilHealth expanded to 156 sessions/year at ₱6,350/session effective 2024–2025, but zero patient-facing Filipino guide explains the Dialysis Patient Database (PDD) enrollment process, what happens at the 156-session cap, remaining out-of-pocket costs, or how to appeal denials. Confirmed as the #1 most-asked question in the 83,000-member Dialysis PH Support Group.
- **Gap source:** Social media agent #1 | **Evidence source:** PhilHealth Circular 2024

### 2. Conservative Kidney Management — The Right to Choose No Dialysis
**Suggested slug:** `conservative-kidney-management.html`
- Globally 20–30% of ESRD patients die after refusing or discontinuing dialysis; Filipino patients and families regularly ask nephrologists about "hindi na mag-dialysis." This topic is completely absent from the site and treated near-taboo in Filipino patient spaces. A guide on conservative management (symptom palliation, hospice, what to expect), aligned with advance-care-planning-dialysis.html, addresses a real and under-served patient need.
- **Gap source:** Social media agent #9 | No existing content on site

### 3. OFW and Kidney Disease Abroad
**Suggested slug:** `ofw-kidney-disease.html`
- ~1.8M deployed OFWs; being diagnosed with CKD or ESRD while working in the Middle East, Hong Kong, Taiwan, or Japan is a distinct clinical and bureaucratic crisis entirely different from tourist travel dialysis (covered in travel-dialysis-ckd.html). Gaps: PhilHealth contributions abroad, OWWA repatriation medical benefit, dialysis costs in OFW destination countries, pre-employment medical exam (PEME) kidney findings, and how to transition home. No equivalent content exists anywhere in Filipino online health content.
- **Gap source:** Content gap #7 + Social media #11

### 4. FSGS (Focal Segmental Glomerulosclerosis) — Patient Guide
**Suggested slug:** `fsgs-guide.html`
- Largest driver of nephrotic-range proteinuria → ESRD in young Filipino adults. The generic glomerulonephritis.html has 1 paragraph on FSGS. No guide explains the disease course, steroid resistance, rituximab/voclosporin/sparsentan options, genetic testing considerations, or relapse management. Patients diagnosed with FSGS have nowhere to go on this site.
- **Gap source:** Content gap #1 | **Evidence source:** KDIGO 2021 GN guideline, sparsentan FDA approval

### 5. Mefenamic Acid and OTC Nephrotoxins — The Hidden Kidney Killers
**Suggested slug:** `otc-nephrotoxins-ckd.html`
- Mefenamic acid (Ponstan) is among the top-purchased OTCs in Philippine pharmacies, used freely for dysmenorrhea and pain by millions with silent early-stage CKD. The existing nsaid-kidney-injury.html is clinical in framing; nothing addresses the Filipino OTC culture pattern. Facebook doctors post warnings but no comprehensive patient guide exists. High-volume preventable harm.
- **Gap source:** Social media #3 | Extends nsaid-kidney-injury.html scope

### 6. Fistula vs. Graft vs. Catheter — The Access Decision Guide
**Suggested slug:** `dialysis-access-decision.html`
- dialysis-access-care.html and avf-aneurysm-and-changes.html cover care of existing access; nothing helps a pre-dialysis patient understand the AVF vs AVG decision: vessel mapping, maturation time (AVF 6–8 weeks vs catheter same-day), infection/patency trade-offs, upper arm vs forearm options. Result: patients arrive at ESRD needing a catheter because no one explained they needed fistula surgery 3 months earlier.
- **Gap source:** Content gap #10 | Clinical urgency: very high

### 7. Membranous Nephropathy — PLA2R Antibodies and Rituximab
**Suggested slug:** `membranous-nephropathy.html`
- One of the most common causes of nephrotic syndrome in Filipino adults over 40. Anti-PLA2R antibody testing (diagnostic and monitoring cornerstone) gets one table row in the GN guide. Rituximab as first-line per KDIGO 2021 is absent from patient-facing content. Patients undergoing rituximab infusion cycles have no Filipino guide explaining what to expect.
- **Gap source:** Content gap #2 | Evidence: KDIGO GN 2021, MENTOR trial

### 8. PWD Card for CKD Patients — Who Qualifies and How to Apply
**Suggested slug:** `pwd-card-ckd.html`
- HB 05136 (19th Congress) establishes PWD privileges for CKD patients; Stage 5 patients entitled to 20% discount on dialysis. Facebook groups repeatedly surface this question but no step-by-step LGU application guide exists in Filipino. Short guide, high immediate utility.
- **Gap source:** Social media #4

### 9. Malasakit Center — Step-by-Step Dialysis Claims Guide
**Suggested slug:** `malasakit-center-guide.html`
- ckd-financial-stress.html and philhealth-z-packages.html mention Malasakit Centers in bullet points. Neither provides the actual document checklist (PhilHealth card, hospital bills, medical abstract, DTI drug price quote), simultaneous DSWD-DOH-PhilMedical Fund routing, processing timelines, or what to do when one window denies. Failed Malasakit claims almost always result from missing documents, not ineligibility.
- **Gap source:** Social media #5 + Content gap #15

## TIER 2 — Build Next (Single source, high clinical or Filipino-specific impact)

### 10. ANCA Vasculitis and Rapidly Progressive GN
**Suggested slug:** `anca-vasculitis-ckd.html`
- GPA/MPA/EGPA can destroy kidney function in weeks. The GN guide has one paragraph. Pulse cyclophosphamide or rituximab induction, azathioprine/MMF maintenance, PR3 vs MPO subtype behavior, ENT/lung co-involvement — all absent. Patients arrive at nephrology after rheumatology/ENT completely confused.
- **Gap source:** Content gap #11

### 11. Immunosuppression Patient Guide
**Suggested slug:** `immunosuppression-kidney-guide.html`
- No consolidated guide for CKD patients on steroids, tacrolimus, MMF, cyclosporine, or rituximab. Calcineurin inhibitor toxicities (gingival hyperplasia, diabetes, tremors), infection risk, live vaccine contraindications, herbal drug interactions, tacrolimus nephrotoxicity as a cause of allograft failure — all scattered across lupus/GN/transplant guides. The highest-risk patient group is the least-guided.
- **Gap source:** Content gap #3

### 12. Contraception and Family Planning in CKD
**Suggested slug:** `contraception-ckd.html`
- ckd-and-pregnancy.html and sexual-health-intimacy-ckd.html cover fertility but neither addresses safe contraceptive methods. Estrogen-containing OCP raises VTE risk in proteinuric/nephrotic patients. An unplanned pregnancy in CKD Stage 4–5 carries serious maternal/fetal risk. Fills the gap between the pregnancy and sexual health guides.
- **Gap source:** Content gap #4

### 13. Pregnancy on Dialysis — Intensive HD and What It Means
**Suggested slug:** `pregnancy-on-dialysis.html`
- ckd-and-pregnancy.html has 2 paragraphs on Stage 5D pregnancy. Intensive daily HD (≥36 hr/week), BUN targets, fluid management, EPO dosing changes, suppressed HPG axis, fetal survival rates (40–80% with intensive HD vs 30–40% standard) — all absent. Filipino dialysis patients who become pregnant have nowhere to turn on this site.
- **Gap source:** Content gap #12 + Social media #8 (IJSMS 2025 paper on this topic)

### 14. Phosphate Binder Choice — Patient Comparison Guide
**Suggested slug:** `phosphate-binder-comparison.html`
- phosphorus-ckd.html has a thin binder table. Missing: vascular calcification risk with calcium binders, GI tolerability comparison, pill burden, Philippine pharmacy costs, ferric citrate (iron-based binders), and the new tenapanor (Xphozah, FDA 2023 — blocks intestinal phosphate absorption via NHE3 inhibition, different mechanism entirely). KDIGO 2024 now favors non-calcium binders; many Filipino patients still on calcium carbonate.
- **Gap source:** Content gap #9 + Clinical dev #11 (tenapanor + KDIGO 2024 MBD update)

### 15. Dental Care for Kidney and Dialysis Patients
**Suggested slug:** `dental-care-ckd.html`
- AVF/graft patients need antibiotic prophylaxis before dental procedures (bacteremia seeding access → hospitalization). Post-extraction pain management without NSAIDs, bleeding risk in anticoagulated patients, oral manifestations of CKD (uremic fetor, xerostomia, stomatitis). Filipino patients frequently attend dentists without disclosing kidney disease. Mentioned in one sentence in dialysis-access-infection.html.
- **Gap source:** Content gap #6

### 16. Sleep Apnea in CKD — Full Clinical Guide
**Suggested slug:** `sleep-apnea-ckd.html`
- Affects 40–80% of dialysis patients; drives nocturnal hypertension, cardiovascular events, and fluid shifts. ckd-mental-health-sleep.html shares 3 paragraphs with 5 other topics. CPAP titration complexity in fluid-overloaded patients, nocturnal HD reducing OSA severity — none of this depth exists. Patients attribute daytime fatigue to dialysis rather than recognizing treatable OSA.
- **Gap source:** Content gap #8

### 17. Pediatric CKD — Guide for Filipino Parents
**Suggested slug:** `pediatric-ckd-parents.html`
- NKTI 2024 data: pediatric CKD cases jumped from 144 (9.3%) to 301 (12.1%) — a 108% increase in one year. ckd-children-young-adults.html exists but targets patients; nothing targets parents of children with CKD: congenital anomalies explained simply, school management, PhilHealth expanded pediatric PD benefit (effective Jan 2025), emotional support for families.
- **Gap source:** Social media #12 + Clinical dev #15 (PSN/DOH response)

### 18. Barangay to Nephrologist Referral Pathway
**Suggested slug:** `nephrology-referral-pathway.html`
- No guide explains: BHC → RHU → district hospital → tertiary center chain, what lab values trigger a nephrology referral, how to use PhilHealth primary care benefit for workup, DOH kidney disease program. Many CKD Stage 3 patients identified at RHU level never reach a nephrologist. Late referral is the primary driver of preventable dialysis-dependence.
- **Gap source:** Content gap #5

### 19. Dialysis in the Provinces — Finding Centers and What to Do When There Is None
**Suggested slug:** `provincial-dialysis-access.html`
- HB 02647 (20th Congress — Regional Dialysis Center Act) is pending because provincial access is a documented crisis. Patients from Eastern Visayas, Mindanao, MIMAROPA travel 3–5 hours per session. No guide covers: DOH-accredited center finder, transient dialysis arrangements when traveling to Manila, emergency dialysis in dialysis-sparse areas.
- **Gap source:** Social media #10

### 20. Kidney Transplant Pathway — Realistic Philippines Guide
**Suggested slug:** `transplant-pathway-philippines.html`
- Only 2% of Filipino ESRD patients receive transplants. kidney-transplant.html exists but doesn't cover: NKTI waiting list process, living-related vs deceased donor practicalities, realistic total cost, post-transplant immunosuppression and PhilHealth Z coverage, opt-out donation law reform. transplant-allograft-failure.html covers failure — but the journey to transplant is undocumented.
- **Gap source:** Social media #13 + 2025 PMC paper on opt-out policy

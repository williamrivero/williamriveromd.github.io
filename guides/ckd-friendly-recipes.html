If your doctor has prescribed a phosphate binder (calcium carbonate, sevelamer), it must be taken at the START of every meal — not before or after. The binder works by trapping phosphorus from the food you are currently eating. Skipping it even once allows excess phosphorus to be absorbed.Kung ang inyong doktor ay nag-reseta ng phosphate binder (calcium carbonate, sevelamer), dapat itong inumin sa SIMULA ng bawat pagkain — hindi bago o pagkatapos. Ang paglaktaw nito kahit minsan ay nagpapahintulot sa labis na phosphorus na masipsip.Kung ang inyong doktor nagrekomenda og phosphate binder (calcium carbonate, sevelamer), kinahanglan kini inumun sa PAGSUGOD sa matag pagkaon — dili sa wala pa o human. Kitchen Techniques

CKD Cooking Tips That Make a Real Difference

The leaching method — reduce potassium by up to 50%

Peel and cut vegetable into small pieces → soak in large amount of warm water for 2 hours (change water once) → drain → boil in fresh water → drain again and discard water. Works for kamote, potato, pechay, kangkong, sitaw. Never use the boiling water as sabaw.

Replace patis with calamansi + pepper

1 tbsp patis = ~1,500 mg sodium. Calamansi juice provides similar umami brightness with zero sodium. For dishes needing depth, use a combination of calamansi + garlic + black pepper + a drop of sesame oil (for flavor, not cooking).

Grilling, steaming, and poaching over frying

Grilling and steaming require no breading (which adds phosphate additives) and no sauces (which add sodium). They also reduce fat content — important for CKD patients with concurrent dyslipidemia. Reserve frying for small amounts of garlic bits and onion for topping.

Avoid phosphate-laden processed foods entirely

Hotdog, tocino, longganisa, processed cheese, instant noodles, and canned goods all contain inorganic phosphate preservatives absorbed at 90–100%. One piece of hotdog can contain 400 mg phosphorus — nearly half the daily limit. Cook fresh every day when possible.

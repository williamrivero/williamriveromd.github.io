PhilHealth Claims Toolkit

Six working tools for the Renal Care Academy Billing & Claims Officer track. They do arithmetic, not decisions: the governing circular, your PRO’s written guidance, and your facility’s approved settings decide. Use case tokens, never patient names. Nothing you enter leaves this page.

Policy values used here. Hemodialysis package under PhilHealth Circular 2024-0023: 156 sessions per calendar year at 6,350 pesos per session (6,000 facility and 350 professional fee), effective October 9, 2024; claims filed within 60 calendar days; professional fees released within one month of receipt. Each tool lets you change these values. Confirm the rules in force on the date of service before you rely on a result.

1. Filing deadline and internal target

Counts 60 calendar days from the day after your approved filing trigger, sets an earlier internal target, and shows how many days are left. Weekends and holidays count unless an issuance says otherwise (Module 6).

2. Session ledger and allocation projection

Numbers each session against the 156-session allocation, starting from the PRO-confirmed count, keeps pending entries visibly separate, flags same-date rows, and projects the last covered session for the patient’s schedule (Modules 2 and 4).

3. Remittance and professional-fee split

Checks a remittance against the sessions allowed, splits it into facility and professional shares, shows any unexplained difference, and gives the professional-fee release date (Module 8).

4. Claims performance rates

Return rate, denial rate, and first-pass acceptance for one cohort with one start point. Refuses a cohort whose parts add up to more than the whole (Module 8).

5. Bill checker

Flags covered, indicated items charged to the patient and premium items without a signed agreement before the statement goes out (Module 3).

6. Capstone score sheet

Totals the practicum tasks (Form A or B) and applies the critical-error gate before the score: pass is 80 of 100 or more with zero critical errors (Module 11).

7. PhilHealth–HMO–patient split

Splits one session’s charges with PhilHealth first, then the HMO (capped by the contract, the plan, and the letter of authorization), then the patient, and tracks the member’s remaining maximum benefit limit (Modules 12 and 13).

8. HMO aging by payer

Ages unpaid HMO claims from proof of receipt, by payer, against each payer’s own payment term, and ranks the payers to follow up first (Module 14).

Important: These tools do arithmetic, not decisions. They do not replace the governing PhilHealth issuances, your PRO’s written guidance, or your facility’s approved adoption record. Clinical questions go to the attending nephrologist and nursing. Use case tokens, never real patient data. Reviewed November 24, 2026.

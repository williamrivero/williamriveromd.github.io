Nephrology Calculators & Clinical Tools

A standalone library of the calculators built into our kidney guides — gathered in one place, each on its own page with indications, the formula, the evidence, and references. Everything runs in your browser; no data is stored or transmitted.

Why this exists As a nephrologist, all these calculators, scoring tables, and formulae are genuinely hard to keep in memory — hence the need for one central repository: every tool in a single place, each with its indication, formula, evidence, and references, ready whenever the bedside decision can't wait.

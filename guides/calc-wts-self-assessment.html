About This Assessment

This is an educational self-check drawn from the Hemodialysis Water Treatment Systems reference guide, covering the regulatory framework, treatment-train design, source-water variants, capacity planning, facility layout (isolation bays, HDF bays, dual-pass RO), water quality standards, and troubleshooting. It is intended as a knowledge refresher and orientation aid for dialysis nurses and technicians who operate the water treatment system — it is not a substitute for your facility's formal competency validation, credentialing, or DOH-required training records, and a passing score here does not certify competency to independently manage a water treatment system.

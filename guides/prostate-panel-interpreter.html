What is PSA? Ano ang PSA? Unsa ang PSA? Nanu ing PSA?

PSA (Prostate-Specific Antigen) is a protein made only by prostate cells. A small amount normally leaks into the blood. The PSA test measures this amount in nanograms per milliliter (ng/mL). Higher levels can mean the prostate is enlarged, inflamed, or — less commonly — contains cancer.

PSA is a screening tool, not a diagnosis. A high PSA does not automatically mean cancer; most elevated PSA results are due to benign causes like BPH (enlarged prostate) or prostatitis.

PSA kinetics and clinical context: Total PSA represents combined free and complexed (PSA-ACT) fractions. In prostate cancer, reduced glandular architecture leads to greater PSA leakage and lower % free PSA (<10–15% high-risk). Benign prostatic hyperplasia produces proportionally more free PSA (>25% lower risk). PSA density (>0.15 ng/mL/mL) and PSA velocity (>0.75 ng/mL/yr) are independent predictors of significant prostate cancer on biopsy.

EAU/AUA guidance: PSA <1.0 at age 40 → resample at 8 years; PSA 1.0–2.0 at age 40 → resample at 2 years; PSA >2.0 at any age → annual monitoring. Biopsy threshold typically >4.0 ng/mL effective PSA, or lower if density, velocity, or free PSA fraction are unfavorable.

Some medications significantly lower PSA levels, which can mask an underlying problem if your doctor does not know about them. The most important class is 5-alpha reductase inhibitors (5-ARIs), used to shrink an enlarged prostate.

Ang ilang mga gamot ay nagpapababa ng antas ng PSA, na maaaring magtago ng isang problema kung ang iyong doktor ay hindi alam ang tungkol sa mga ito. Ang pinakamahalaga ay 5-alpha reductase inhibitors (5-ARIs), na ginagamit upang palamitin ang isang pinalaking prostate.

Ang uban nga mga tambal makadapat og kamahinungdanon sa pagpaubos sa mga lebel sa PSA, nga mahimong magtagong usa ka problema kung ang imong doktor wala mahibalo bahin kanila. Ang pinaka-importante mao ang 5-alpha reductase inhibitors (5-ARIs), gigamit aron mapagamay ang usa ka nagdako nga prostate.

Ing ilang mga gamut kamahinulagang nagpapababa ning antas ning PSA, at maaaring magtaguan ning problema nung ali alam ning inyung doktor. Ing pinakamahalaga ay 5-alpha reductase inhibitors (5-ARIs), ginagamit anting paliitin ing lumakie na prostate.

5-ARI pharmacology: Finasteride (type II 5-AR inhibitor) reduces DHT by ~70%, causing ~50% PSA suppression at steady state (6 months). Dutasteride (dual type I+II) reduces DHT by ~90%, causing ~60% suppression. The AUA recommends multiplying the observed PSA by 2 (finasteride) or 2.5 (dutasteride) to estimate the "true" PSA for risk stratification. Free PSA % is preserved on 5-ARIs; any rise in PSA while on 5-ARI should be investigated regardless of absolute level.

Alpha-1 blockers and PDE5i: No clinically meaningful PSA effect. Statins may lower PSA slightly in some studies but no accepted correction factor exists.

Pre-analytic variables: DRE transiently raises PSA (mainly complexed fraction) — historically avoid DRE before PSA draw; modern literature suggests the effect is small but recommendation to draw PSA first persists. Prostate biopsy causes PSA to peak at 24h and may remain elevated for up to 6 weeks. UTI/prostatitis elevates PSA through disruption of the basement membrane and para-epithelial tissue; always document active infections, re-test after treatment.

Ejaculation raises PSA transiently due to secretion into the bloodstream; the 48-hour abstinence recommendation is conservative but reasonable for borderline values.

    Matapos ang mga koreksyon ng gamot, ang iyong PSA ay binibigyang-kahulugan sa mga antas. Bawat antas ay nagmumungkahi ng iba't ibang kurso ng aksyon.

    Human sa mga koreksyon sa droga, ang imong PSA gihubad sa mga tier. Ang matag tier nagsugyot og lain-laing kurso sa aksyon.

    Kasunud ning mga koreksyon ning gamut, ing inyung PSA binabalintataw king mga antas. Ing bawat antas nagmumungkahi ning iba't ibang kurso ning aksyon.

    EAU 2024 PSA thresholds: PSA 4–10 ng/mL ("grey zone") — cancer detected in only 25% of biopsies; use % free PSA (<15% = higher risk), density (>0.15 = biopsy), and mpMRI (PI-RADS ≥3 = biopsy). PSA >10 — cancer probability ~60%; refer. Consider PSAD over threshold absolute PSA in men with large prostates (volume >60 mL).

    PSA velocity (PSAV): >0.75 ng/mL/yr with PSA in 4–10 range is an independent predictor of significant cancer on biopsy. >2.0 ng/mL/yr predicts aggressive disease. Requires minimum 2 measurements ≥18 months apart for reliable calculation.

    Free PSA caveat: % free PSA is affected by storage/handling (free PSA degrades faster than total PSA) — interpret cautiously if sample was not processed promptly. Not reliable after prostatitis or biopsy.

    How often you need PSA monitoring depends on your current level, age, and risk factors. The table below gives general guidance.

    PSA Monitoring Intervals
    SituationRecommended Interval
    PSA <1.0, age <60Every 2–4 years
    PSA 1.0–3.0, no risk factorsEvery 1–2 years
    PSA 3.0–4.0 or rising trendEvery 6–12 months
    PSA 4.0–10.0, pending biopsy decisionEvery 3–6 months until evaluated
    On active surveillance (cancer confirmed)Every 3–6 months per urologist
    After radical prostatectomyEvery 3–6 months × 2 years, then annually
    After radiation therapyEvery 3–6 months × 5 years, then annually

    Kung unsa kasagad ang imong kinahanglan og pagmonitor sa PSA nagdepende sa imong kasamtangang lebel, edad, ug mga hinungdan sa risgo.

    Ing dalas ning pangangailangan mong pagmatyag ning PSA depende king inyung kasalukuyang antas, edad, at mga salik ning panganib.

    Post-treatment surveillance: After radical prostatectomy, PSA should fall to undetectable (<0.1 ng/mL) within 4–8 weeks. Any detectable PSA thereafter is biochemical recurrence (BCR). After EBRT/brachytherapy, PSA nadir takes 18–36 months; Phoenix criterion (nadir + 2.0 ng/mL) defines BCR. After ADT, PSA castration-resistant threshold is PSA progression despite testosterone <50 ng/dL.

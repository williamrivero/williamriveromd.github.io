③ Potassium — check carefully
235mg per serving. Whole container: 588mg — nearly 30% of a dialysis patient's daily limit (2,000mg). Always listed in mg now on Philippine labels.
④ Phosphorus — often missing
Not required on Philippine labels. You must check the ingredients list for phosphate additives instead — this is where hidden phosphate is buried.

%DV is based on a 2,000 kcal healthy adult diet — not CKD limits. A "low sodium" claim (20% DV) still contains 470mg — significant for a CKD patient. Ignore %DV; focus on milligrams.

%DV on Philippine nutrition labels is calculated based on a 2,000 kcal diet for a healthy adult. The reference values used: sodium 2,300mg, potassium 4,700mg. But CKD Stage 4–5 patients need sodium <2,000mg and potassium <2,000mg — different reference points. A product showing "20% DV sodium" (460mg) is actually 23% of your CKD sodium limit.

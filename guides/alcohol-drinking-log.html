Why honesty matters here. Your nephrologist cannot adjust your medications, BP plan, or dialysis schedule without knowing what you actually drink. Estimates are useless — write down what you actually consumed each day, not what you wish you had. This log is for you first, your doctor second. It will not be filed in any system you don't control.

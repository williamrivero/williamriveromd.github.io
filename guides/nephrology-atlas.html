E1Pronephros — Week 3–4

First kidney analog arising from cervical intermediate mesoderm (somites 7–14). Seven to ten paired non-functional nephrotomes. Pronephric duct extends caudally — this persists as the mesonephric (Wolffian) duct. Completely regresses by week 4. Functional in fish; vestigial in mammals. Phylogenetic comparison panel.

Cervical SomitesNon-FunctionalWolffian Duct Origin

E2Mesonephros — Week 4–6

Interim functional kidney at thoracolumbar level. Mesonephric (Wolffian) duct drains ~40 tubules with primitive glomeruli into cloaca. Temporarily produces urine weeks 6–10. Persistent structures: males → epididymis, vas deferens, seminal vesicle; females → regresses (Gartner's duct remnant only). Ureteric bud will arise from this duct.

E3Ureteric Bud Emergence & MM Induction — Week 5–6

Ureteric bud (UB) sprouts from caudal Wolffian duct at week 5. GDNF from metanephric mesenchyme (MM) activates RET receptor on UB tip → induces invasion. Reciprocal induction begins. Key signals: GDNF→RET (primary), Wnt11 (UB maintains GDNF), PAX2/PAX8/WT1 (MM competence). GDNF/RET failure → renal agenesis. Single vs dual UB → normal vs duplex collecting system.

E4Branching Morphogenesis — Week 6–10

~15 rounds of dichotomous UB branching: generations 1–5 (renal pelvis + major calyces), 6–11 (minor calyces), 12–15 (collecting ducts). Each ampulla tip induces cap mesenchyme condensation — future nephron progenitor population. ~1 million terminal collecting ducts generated, each inducing one nephron. 3D collecting duct tree inset. Reduced branching → oligonephropathy.

E5Nephrogenesis — MET Sequence, Week 7–36

Seven-stage MET sequence: cap mesenchyme → pretubular aggregate (Wnt9b→Wnt4) → renal vesicle → comma-shaped body → S-shaped body (three segments: future glomerulus/PCT/DCT) → capillary loop (endothelial invasion, podocyte differentiation) → mature nephron. Nephrogenesis ends at 36 weeks. Prematurity/IUGR → permanent nephron deficit → Brenner hypothesis.

Wnt9b / Wnt4S-Shaped Body36 Week EndpointBrenner Hypothesis
⊕
E6Renal Ascent & Rotation — Week 6–9

Three ghost kidney positions: sacral (S1–S2, hilum anterior, week 5–6) → iliac (45° rotation, week 7) → final lumbar L1–L2 (90° medial rotation complete, week 9). Sequential vascular acquisition: accessory arteries = persistent embryonic vessels (15–30% adults). Failure points: pelvic ectopia, horseshoe (IMA arrest L3–L4), malrotation (hilum faces anteriorly), crossed fused ectopia.

90° Medial RotationIMA ArrestEctopic KidneyAccessory Arteries

E7Mature Fetal Kidney — Week 10 through Birth

Fetal lobulation (surface grooves between lobes — normal, benign in adults). Urine production begins week 10; primary amniotic fluid source from week 16. Fetal urine 10 mL/day at 20 weeks → 500–700 mL/day at term. Nephrogenesis timeline bar (week 7→36). Oligohydramnios → Potter sequence. Antenatal hydronephrosis: 1–5% of pregnancies, most resolve.

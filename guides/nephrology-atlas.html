07Renal Artery Stenosis — Anatomy, Pathophysiology & Clinical Correlates

Four-panel infographic: (1) Normal renal artery with correct arterial wall layers (intima/media/adventitia). (2) Atherosclerotic RAS — eccentric calcified plaque at ostium, crescent-shaped residual lumen, small atrophic kidney, tardus-parvus Doppler, angiographic appearance. (3) FMD — string-of-beads mid-distal artery, no plaque, young women. (4) RAAS cascade → efferent constriction → ACEi/ARB AKI risk.

12BPH — Prostate Enlargement, Bladder Outlet Obstruction & Upstream Cascade

(2) Bladder — detrusor hypertrophy, trabeculation (coarse muscular ridges), multiple diverticula, elevated PVR. (3) Bilateral dilated hydroureters. (4) Bilateral hydronephrosis with cortical thinning. Connecting arrows show pressure cascade. Treatment summary: IPSS scoring, alpha-blockers, 5-ARIs, TURP.

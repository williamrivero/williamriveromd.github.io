P13CRRT — Continuous Renal Replacement Therapy CircuitHybrid

CVVHDF (continuous veno-venous hemodiafiltration) combines diffusion (dialysate counter-current) and convection (replacement fluid + ultrafiltration) for maximal solute clearance. Blood pump 100–200 mL/min; effluent rate 20–25 mL/kg/hr target dose. Regional citrate anticoagulation chelates ionized calcium at filter; calcium infused post-filter to restore systemic levels — avoids systemic bleeding risk of heparin. Machine alarms: filter clotting (rising TMP), air detection, blood leak.

  1. 1Large-bore dual-lumen catheter (IJ or femoral); blood pump 100–200 mL/min
  2. 2Citrate pre-filter → chelates Ca²⁺ at hemofilter; iCa target <0.4 mmol/L
  3. 3Effluent = ultrafiltrate + dialysate waste; target 20–25 mL/kg/hr
  4. 4CaCl₂ post-filter restores systemic iCa; monitor systemic iCa 1.1–1.3 mmol/L

P14Therapeutic Plasma Exchange — Centrifugal & Membrane TPEHybrid

TPE removes large-molecular-weight pathogenic substances (antibodies, immune complexes, paraproteins) unachievable by standard dialysis. Centrifugal separation (blood spinning) vs membrane filtration (0.2–0.6μm pore). Replacement fluid: 5% albumin (standard) or FFP (for TTP — provides ADAMTS13). ASFA Category I indications include TTP, anti-GBM disease, Guillain-Barré, myasthenia crisis, and FSGS recurrence post-transplant. One plasma volume (40–60 mL/kg) exchanged per session.

  1. 1Central or large peripheral access; anticoagulation with citrate or heparin
  2. 21.0–1.5× plasma volume exchanged per session (40–60 mL/kg)
  3. 3Replacement: albumin 5% (most) or FFP (TTP — replaces ADAMTS13)
  4. 4Monitor: hypocalcemia (citrate), allergic reactions (FFP), hypotension

P17TURBT — Transurethral Resection of Bladder TumorHybrid

Cystoscopic resection using monopolar or bipolar resectoscope loop. En-bloc or piecemeal resection must include detrusor muscle (muscularis propria) for accurate T-staging — absence of muscle in specimen is indication for re-TURBT. Lateral wall tumors risk obturator nerve reflex causing leg adduction and bladder perforation — managed with neuromuscular blockade or obturator nerve block. Intravesical mitomycin-C within 6h post-TURBT for NMIBC reduces recurrence 35%.

E3Ureteric Bud Emergence & MM Induction — Week 5–62D

Ureteric bud (UB) sprouts from caudal Wolffian duct at week 5. GDNF from metanephric mesenchyme (MM) activates RET receptor on UB tip → induces invasion. Reciprocal induction begins. Key signals: GDNF→RET (primary), Wnt11 (UB maintains GDNF), PAX2/PAX8/WT1 (MM competence). GDNF/RET failure → renal agenesis. Single vs dual UB → normal vs duplex collecting system.

E4Branching Morphogenesis — Week 6–102D

~15 rounds of dichotomous UB branching: generations 1–5 (renal pelvis + major calyces), 6–11 (minor calyces), 12–15 (collecting ducts). Each ampulla tip induces cap mesenchyme condensation — future nephron progenitor population. ~1 million terminal collecting ducts generated, each inducing one nephron. 3D collecting duct tree inset. Reduced branching → oligonephropathy.

E5Nephrogenesis — MET Sequence, Week 7–362D

Seven-stage MET sequence: cap mesenchyme → pretubular aggregate (Wnt9b→Wnt4) → renal vesicle → comma-shaped body → S-shaped body (three segments: future glomerulus/PCT/DCT) → capillary loop (endothelial invasion, podocyte differentiation) → mature nephron. Nephrogenesis ends at 36 weeks. Prematurity/IUGR → permanent nephron deficit → Brenner hypothesis.

Wnt9b / Wnt4S-Shaped Body36 Week EndpointBrenner Hypothesis
E6Renal Ascent & Rotation — Week 6–92D

Three ghost kidney positions: sacral (S1–S2, hilum anterior, week 5–6) → iliac (45° rotation, week 7) → final lumbar L1–L2 (90° medial rotation complete, week 9). Sequential vascular acquisition: accessory arteries = persistent embryonic vessels (15–30% adults). Failure points: pelvic ectopia, horseshoe (IMA arrest L3–L4), malrotation (hilum faces anteriorly), crossed fused ectopia.

E7Mature Fetal Kidney — Week 10 through BirthHybrid

Fetal lobulation (surface grooves between lobes — normal, benign in adults). Urine production begins week 10; primary amniotic fluid source from week 16. Fetal urine 10 mL/day at 20 weeks → 500–700 mL/day at term. Nephrogenesis timeline bar (week 7→36). Oligohydramnios → Potter sequence. Antenatal hydronephrosis: 1–5% of pregnancies, most resolve.

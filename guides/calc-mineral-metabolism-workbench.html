Instructions
  1. Phosphate handling: enter paired serum and urine phosphate and creatinine with a unit for each, then the specimen context. Phosphate pairs and creatinine pairs may use different units; the tool normalizes them.
  2. Trend dashboard: add each result with its date, analyte, unit, assay or laboratory name and that laboratory’s limits. PTH is standardized only when the assay ULN is entered.
  3. Calcium ledger: list each calcium-containing product with its elemental calcium per unit from the verified label.
  4. Review aids: the IV iron and antiresorptive tools document and prompt; they do not score or decide.

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing is stored, sent to analytics or placed in the address bar. Do not enter names, hospital numbers or other identifiers.

When to Use — and What These Tools Do Not Do

Use with Novel Regulators and Functions in Mineral Metabolism for adults with CKD G3a–G5D and for hypophosphatemia work-up. Arithmetic is versioned in MM-CORE v1.0.0; interpretive notes come from a separate, sourced rule set (MM-RULES v0.1.0-draft) that is marked as a draft pending clinical sign-off.

⚠️

Deliberately excluded

No FGF23/Klotho score or ratio, no activin A fracture predictor, no T50 inferred from calcium and phosphate, no “mineral stress index,” no automatic drug selection or dose change, no bone-turnover diagnosis, no “MBD controlled” badge, and no individual probability derived from trial or cohort rates. Calcium is never auto-adjusted for albumin, and total and ionized calcium are never converted into each other.

Interactive Tool · 1 of 6

Renal Phosphate Handling — FEPO₄, TRP, TmP/GFR

Fractional excretion of phosphate (FEPO₄), tubular reabsorption of phosphate (TRP) and the tubular maximum for phosphate reabsorption per glomerular filtration rate (TmP/GFR) by the Kenny–Glen algorithm (Payne, 1998; Barth et al., 2000). Designed for the question “is the kidney conserving phosphate appropriately when serum phosphate is low?”

Paired sample

Urine creatinine is often reported in mmol/L.

Specimen and patient context (drives applicability limits, not the arithmetic)

⚕ FEPO₄ (%) = 100 × (UP × SCr) ÷ (SP × UCr); TRP = 1 − FEPO₄/100; TmP/GFR = TRP × SP if TRP ≤ 0.86, else SP × 0.3 × TRP ÷ (1 − 0.8 × TRP). TmP/GFR keeps the serum phosphate unit. A low TmP/GFR does not identify the responsible hormone.

Interactive Tool · 2 of 6

Assay-Aware CKD-MBD Trend Dashboard

Small-multiple charts for total calcium, ionized calcium, phosphate, intact PTH (iPTH), alkaline phosphatase (ALP) and 25-hydroxyvitamin D. Lines break wherever the assay or unit changes; change is shown only against the prior comparable sample. PTH is plotted as a multiple of the assay’s upper limit of normal (ULN) when every PTH row has one.

Treatment events (drawn as markers; never used to compute anything)

⚕ PTH ratio = PTH ÷ assay ULN (no ratio when the ULN is missing or ≤ 0). The approximately 2–9 × ULN band (KDIGO 2017, 4.2.3, grade 2C) is drawn only for CKD G5D and does not imply appropriate bone turnover.

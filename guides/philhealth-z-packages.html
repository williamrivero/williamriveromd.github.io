Background

What Is the PhilHealth Z Benefit Package?

The PhilHealth Z Benefit Package is a special coverage program for catastrophic illnesses — conditions that are life-threatening and financially devastating without government support. Kidney failure (ESKD) qualifies for two Z packages: the Peritoneal Dialysis (PD) Z Package and the Kidney Transplantation (KT) Z Package. These provide substantial benefit payments specifically for patients who cannot afford hemodialysis costs or transplant surgery.

The Z Package was designed to address the catastrophic financial impact of ESKD. PD and KT receive dedicated Z benefit envelopes — separate from and supplemental to regular PhilHealth inpatient/outpatient benefits.

⚠

Benefit amounts change — always verify with PhilHealth directly

PhilHealth periodically updates Z benefit amounts, accredited facilities, and document requirements through circulars. The figures in this guide reflect available 2024 rates. Before applying, always confirm current amounts at philhealth.gov.ph or call the PhilHealth hotline at 1-800-10-PHILHEALTH (744-5432) or visit your nearest PhilHealth branch.

Accredited facility required:✓ PhilHealth-accredited PD center only

Why PD instead of HD for this package

The PD Z Package was created because PD is generally more cost-effective than hemodialysis when fully costed, allows home-based treatment, and reduces the burden on dialysis center infrastructure. PhilHealth specifically designed the package to encourage PD as a viable alternative — particularly for patients in areas with limited hemodialysis access.

What the package does NOT cover

Medications outside the package (erythropoietin, phosphate binders, antihypertensives — these are separate claims). Hospitalization for complications (billed as separate inpatient claims). Transport to the PD center. Non-accredited facility charges. Amounts exceeding the ₱120,000 annual cap — patient shoulders the excess.

PD Qualifications

Who Qualifies for the PD Z Package

📋 Medical eligibility requirements

  • Diagnosed with End-Stage Kidney Disease (ESKD) — GFR <15 mL/min or already on dialysis
  • Medically suitable for peritoneal dialysis (no absolute contraindications: prior extensive abdominal surgery, active inflammatory bowel disease, abdominal hernias — must be assessed by nephrologist)
  • Patient and at least one family member willing and able to be trained on PD technique
  • Home environment suitable for sterile PD exchanges (clean room, running water)
  • Nephrologist certification that PD is the appropriate modality

📋 PhilHealth membership requirements

  • Active PhilHealth member OR qualified dependent of an active member
  • Minimum of 3 months premium contributions in the 6 months immediately preceding the claim period
  • Member must not be in arrears (delinquent) on contributions
  • Indigent patients enrolled under the Sponsored Program (barangay indigent certification) also qualify
  • Senior citizens under the Lifetime Member category are automatically covered

📋 Facility requirements

  • Must be treated at a PhilHealth-accredited PD training center
  • List of accredited centers available at philhealth.gov.ph under "Accredited Health Care Institutions"
  • Major accredited PD centers in Metro Manila include NKTI, St. Luke's, Philippine General Hospital, V. Luna Medical Center, and selected private hospitals
  • Provincial accredited centers are listed per region on the PhilHealth website
PD Application Process

How to Apply for the PD Z Package — Step by Step

1

Confirm PD eligibility with your nephrologist

Your nephrologist must assess you for PD suitability and write a formal referral/endorsement stating that PD is the recommended modality. This is the medical foundation of your application.

2

Enroll at a PhilHealth-accredited PD training center

The center's social worker or PD coordinator will guide you through the PhilHealth Z benefit enrollment. The facility must initiate and co-sign the application — patients cannot apply independently.

3

Prepare documentary requirements

PhilHealth MDR (Member Data Record) · Valid ID of member and patient · Nephrologist's certification of ESKD · Latest creatinine and eGFR results · Completed PhilHealth Z benefit claim forms (CF1, CF2, CF4) · Hospital Certificate of Accreditation for PD

4

Undergo PD training

You and a family member must complete the PD training program at the center (typically 5–7 days). Training is covered under the Z package. The center will certify your competency before you begin home PD.

5

Benefit activation and supply delivery

Once approved, PhilHealth pays the accredited facility directly. PD supplies are arranged through the center — typically delivered to your home monthly by Baxter or Fresenius. Your responsibility is the monthly monitoring visit and any amounts exceeding the annual cap.

6

Annual renewal

The Z package must be renewed every year. Your nephrologist provides a renewal certification, your PhilHealth membership is re-verified, and the center resubmits your enrollment. Do not miss renewal — a lapse means out-of-pocket expenses until reinstatement.

💡

The KT Z Package is the single most important PhilHealth benefit for ESKD patients

Kidney transplantation costs ₱800,000–₱1,500,000 or more without coverage. The ₱660,000 Z benefit — combined with regular PhilHealth inpatient benefits — makes transplantation financially accessible for many Filipino patients who would otherwise never be able to afford it. Ask your nephrologist specifically about KT Z eligibility even if you are currently on dialysis.

KT Qualifications

Who Qualifies for the Kidney Transplant Z Package

📋 Medical eligibility

  • Diagnosed ESKD — on dialysis or GFR <20 mL/min with rapid decline
  • No absolute contraindications to transplantation (active malignancy within 2 years, uncontrolled infection, severe cardiovascular disease, non-adherence to treatment)
  • Age typically 18–65 years — some centers accept selected patients outside this range
  • Available living donor (related or emotionally related) OR listed on the deceased donor waiting list at a PhilHealth-accredited transplant center
  • Completed pre-transplant medical evaluation clearances: cardiac, pulmonary, infectious disease, dental, ophthalmology
  • Negative cross-match with donor (immunologic compatibility)

📋 PhilHealth membership and facility requirements

  • Active PhilHealth membership (same premium requirements as PD package)
  • Must be performed at a PhilHealth-accredited transplant center
  • Accredited KT centers in the Philippines: NKTI (National Kidney and Transplant Institute), St. Luke's Medical Center (QC and BGC), Asian Hospital, Makati Medical Center, The Medical City, selected regional hospitals
  • NKTI is the primary government center — highest volume, lowest out-of-pocket costs for indigent patients

Living donor requirements

The donor must be: a blood relative (parent, sibling, child, cousin) OR a non-related donor with documented emotional relationship and NKTI Voluntary Organ Donation Certificate. Age 18–55 (some centers to 60). Normal kidney function. No hypertension, diabetes, or significant medical conditions. Must pass psychological evaluation. Donor surgery is covered under the Z package.

Philippine organ donation law (RA 7170)

Organ sale is illegal in the Philippines. All donors must be voluntary — financial compensation is prohibited. The National Kidney Transplant Institute (NKTI) maintains the national waitlist for deceased donor organs (PDOS — Philippine Deceased Organ Sharing). Waiting time varies from months to years depending on blood type and medical urgency.

KT Application Process

How to Apply for the KT Z Package

1

Referral to an accredited transplant center

Your nephrologist writes a referral to a PhilHealth-accredited transplant center. NKTI accepts direct referrals for indigent patients; private centers require specialist endorsement. The transplant workup typically takes 3–6 months.

2

Complete pre-transplant evaluation

Full medical, cardiac (ECG, echo, stress test), pulmonary, GI clearances, infectious disease panel (TB, hepatitis, HIV, CMV, EBV), dental clearance, psychological assessment. Both recipient AND living donor must be evaluated. This process is covered under the Z package.

3

Transplant center files the Z benefit claim

The accredited center's PhilHealth coordinator files the Z claim with the supporting clinical documents. Required forms: PhilHealth Claim Form 1 (CF1), physician's certification, transplant coordinator's report, donor evaluation documents, cross-match results.

4

PhilHealth approval and benefit activation

PhilHealth reviews and approves the claim. Processing time varies — follow up regularly with the center's social worker. Once approved, the center can schedule the surgery with the Z benefit activated.

5

Post-transplant benefit management

The Z package covers immunosuppressants and follow-up for the first year post-transplant. After Year 1, transition to regular PhilHealth outpatient benefits. NKTI has a dedicated indigent patient program (MALASAKIT Center) for additional financial assistance.

Practical Guidance

Practical Tips and Common Questions

Get a Medical Social Worker involved early

Every PhilHealth-accredited hospital has a Medical Social Worker (MSW) whose job includes assisting with Z benefit applications. Approach the MSW at your target center as early as possible — they know the current documentary requirements, can expedite applications, and can connect you with additional assistance programs (PCSO, MALASAKIT, DSWD, local government medical assistance).

Combine with other assistance programs

PhilHealth Z benefit is not the only source of assistance. PCSO Medical Assistance Program — apply online for additional drug and hospitalization support. MALASAKIT Center (in government hospitals) — coordinates multiple government benefits in one window. Local Government Medical Assistance — barangay and municipal health offices often have separate assistance funds. DSWD Sustainable Livelihood Program — for family income support.

Common QuestionAnswer
Can I use the Z package at a private hospital?Yes — if the hospital is PhilHealth-accredited for PD or KT. Private centers generally have out-of-pocket gaps above the Z benefit amount; NKTI has the smallest gaps for KT.
Can I transfer from HD to PD to use the Z package?Yes — patients on HD can transition to PD and qualify for the PD Z package. Discuss with your nephrologist whether you are a suitable PD candidate.
What if my PhilHealth contributions lapsed?You must settle arrears first. Self-employed and informal sector workers can pay contributions retroactively at any PhilHealth branch. Alternatively, indigent patients can be enrolled in the Sponsored Program through the barangay.
Can a senior citizen with no contributions use this?Yes — all Filipinos aged 60+ are Lifetime PhilHealth members regardless of contribution history (RA 10645). Senior citizens can use Z benefits without current contributions.
What happens if the transplant fails (rejection)?The patient returns to dialysis covered under standard CDMP/Z-HD benefits. The one-time KT Z package has already been used but standard dialysis coverage continues.
Is there an income means test for Z benefits?No income test for Z benefits — any active PhilHealth member qualifies regardless of income. Indigent patients additionally qualify through the Sponsored Program.

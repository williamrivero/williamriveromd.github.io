Background

What Is the PhilHealth Z Benefit Package?

The PhilHealth Z Benefit Package covers catastrophic illnesses — conditions that are life-threatening and financially devastating without government support. Kidney failure (CKD Stage 5 / ESKD) qualifies under two Z packages: the Peritoneal Dialysis (PD) Z Package and the Kidney Transplantation (KT) Z Package. Both are separate from — and supplemental to — regular PhilHealth inpatient and outpatient benefits. Both received their first major update since 2012 effective January 1, 2025.

2025 PD update (PC 2024-0036)

PD benefits now vary by age group and modality. Adult CAPD increased from ₱270,000 to up to ₱510,140/year. Pediatric APD now covered for the first time at up to ₱1,270,000/year — a 370% increase.

2025 KT update (PC 2024-0035)

KT benefits now differentiated by donor type and technique. Living donor laparoscopic: ₱993,000. Deceased donor with rATG + machine perfusion: ₱2,146,000. Up from a flat ₱600,000 — a 258% increase for the best-covered category.

New Post-KT Package — July 5, 2025

PC 2025-0011 (pediatric) and 2025-0012 (adult) now cover maintenance immunosuppressants, drug level monitoring, labs, and graft Doppler — addressing the lifelong medication gap that previously deterred many patients from pursuing transplantation.

Always verify with PhilHealth directly

Benefits are updated via PhilHealth Circulars. Confirm current rates at philhealth.gov.ph or call 24/7: (02) 866-225-88 · 0998-857-2957 · 0968-865-4670 · 0917-127-5987.

No co-payment for essential services

Contracted PD providers are strictly prohibited from charging co-payments for essential health services covered by the package. They may charge for non-covered amenities or upgraded services — but must disclose these clearly in advance.

PD Z Package — Eligibility

Who Qualifies for the PD Z Package?

Must meet ALL of these:

  • Diagnosed with CKD Stage 5 (ESKD)
  • Active PhilHealth member or qualified dependent
  • Registered in the PhilHealth Dialysis Database
  • Choosing PD as primary renal replacement therapy
  • Medically fit for peritoneal dialysis
  • Treated at a PhilHealth-accredited PD center (51 nationwide)

Patient obligations:

  • Adhere to prescribed treatment plan
  • Attend all required follow-up visits
  • Never share, sell, or distribute PD solutions received under the package
  • Maintain active PhilHealth membership contributions
  • Re-register and re-apply annually
💡

PD is now promoted as first-line RRT

PhilHealth explicitly encourages PD as a first-line modality for CKD Stage 5 — not just an alternative to hemodialysis. Choosing PD provides greater autonomy, flexibility, and mobility, particularly for working adults and children.

PD Z Package — Application

How to Apply for the PD Z Package

1

Confirm CKD Stage 5 diagnosis with your nephrologist

Your nephrologist certifies ESKD and that PD is the appropriate modality for you. A formal nephrology referral letter and medical records are required.

2

Enroll at a PhilHealth-accredited PD training center

The center's social worker or PD coordinator initiates Z benefit enrollment on your behalf — the facility must co-sign the application. You cannot apply independently.

3

Complete PD training

You and a family member must complete the PD training program (typically 5–7 days). Training is covered under the package. The center certifies your competency before home PD begins.

4

Submit required documents to PhilHealth

Member Data Record (MDR) or PhilHealth ID · PhilHealth Claim Form 1 · Medical records confirming ESKD · Nephrology referral letter · Any additional forms required by the accredited center. Confirm the exact list directly with the center.

5

Begin treatment and renew annually

Once approved, PD supplies, training, and monitoring services are covered at the accredited center. Annual re-application is mandatory — missing renewal causes coverage to lapse.

What the KT package covers

Covers the entire pre-transplant evaluation, transplantation surgery (both recipient and donor), induction immunosuppression, and initial post-operative hospitalization. Maintenance immunosuppressants beyond the initial period are now covered by the new Post-KT packages — see below.

KT Z Package — Eligibility

Who Qualifies for the KT Z Package?

Standard eligibility criteria:

  • CKD Stage 5 (ESKD) — on dialysis or pre-emptive (CrCl <20 mL/min in diabetic patients)
  • Age 10–70 years (individual centers may vary)
  • At least one HLA-DR match with donor
  • Panel Reactive Antibody (PRA) <20%
  • No donor-specific antibodies (DSA)
  • Active PhilHealth member or qualified dependent
  • Treated at a PhilHealth-accredited transplant center

Standard exclusion criteria:

  • Previous kidney transplant
  • Active or recent malignancy
  • Active Hepatitis B or C infection
  • HIV positivity
  • CMV R−/D+ mismatch
  • Uncontrolled congestive heart failure
  • Liver cirrhosis

Individual accredited hospitals may have additional eligibility criteria.

⚠

Pre-authorization required — valid for 60 days

The accredited transplant center must submit a pre-authorization request to PhilHealth before the procedure. Approval is valid for 60 days. Transplants performed without prior authorization may not be covered. Plan ahead — workups and donor evaluation take time.

KT Z Package — Application

How to Apply for the KT Z Package

1

Get referred to an accredited KT center

Your nephrologist refers you to a PhilHealth-accredited kidney transplant center. NKTI and select government and private hospitals are accredited nationwide.

2

Complete pre-transplant workup

Comprehensive evaluation of recipient AND donor: labs, imaging, cardiac clearance, infectious disease screening (TB, hepatitis, HIV, CMV), HLA typing, cross-match, psychosocial assessment. Covered under the Z package.

3

Hospital submits pre-authorization to PhilHealth

The transplant center's Z Benefit Coordinator files the pre-authorization request on your behalf. Approval is valid for 60 days — the transplant must occur within this window.

4

Proceed with transplantation

Once pre-authorized, the transplant is scheduled. The benefit amount is deducted directly from your hospital bill — you pay only the remaining balance, if any, for non-covered items.

5

Apply for Post-KT package (PC 2025-0012)

After a successful transplant, apply for the new Post-KT Z Benefit Package covering maintenance immunosuppressants, drug monitoring, labs, and graft Doppler. See next section.

Post-KT Z Package PC 2025-0011 & 2025-0012

New: Post-Kidney Transplant Z Benefit Effective July 5, 2025

The biggest barrier to kidney transplantation in the Philippines has been the lifelong cost of anti-rejection medications — not the surgery itself. PhilHealth Circulars 2025-0011 (pediatric) and 2025-0012 (adult), launched June 19, 2025 at NKTI, now provide a front-to-end continuum of care, reducing out-of-pocket costs during the highest-risk period for graft rejection.

Practical Guidance

Practical Tips for Patients and Families

Before you apply

  • Ensure your PhilHealth membership is active and contributions are up to date. Coverage lapses if contributions are delinquent.
  • Identify your nearest accredited PD center (51 nationwide) or accredited KT center. Full lists on philhealth.gov.ph.
  • Bring your PhilHealth ID or MDR, a valid government ID, and latest medical records to every consultation.
  • Ask your nephrologist about your PhilHealth Z benefit eligibility — many patients are eligible but unaware.

Supplementary assistance — do not overlook

  • Malasakit Center — in government hospitals; coordinates DSWD, DOH, and PhilMedical Fund assistance to cover remaining balances
  • PCSO Medical Assistance — for medications and hospital balances not covered by PhilHealth
  • DSWD Assistance — social protection for qualified indigent patients
  • Senior Citizen Discount (RA 9994) — 20% discount on medications for patients 60 years and older
  • PWD Discount — 20% on medications and selected services for registered Persons with Disability
🚫

Know your rights — no co-payment for essential services

Under Section 9 of the Universal Health Care Act and PC 2024-0035 and 2024-0036, accredited Z-benefit providers cannot charge co-payments for essential health services included in the package. If a facility demands payment for a covered service, request an itemized statement and contact PhilHealth at (02) 866-225-88. You may also file a formal complaint at your nearest PhilHealth branch.

Many patients with kidney disease are already using popular supplements such as MX3, paragis, and spirulina. This guide does not tell you whether to take them or not. Its purpose is to give you honest, evidence-graded information so that you and your doctor can make a well-informed decision together. 🟣 MX3 — Mangosteen Xanthone
Source: Rind (pericarp) of the mangosteen fruit (Garcinia mangostana L.)

Active compounds: Alpha-, beta-, and gamma-mangostin — polyphenols called xanthones. Each capsule contains 500 mg of standardized pericarp extract.

Philippine FDA (Food and Drug Administration) status: Registered food supplement (FR-4000009668333). No therapeutic claim permitted.

How consumed: 1 capsule twice daily; excipients include magnesium stearate and gelatin capsule.Pinagmulan: Balat (pericarp) ng bungang mangosteen (Garcinia mangostana L.)

Mga aktibong sangkap: Alpha-, beta-, at gamma-mangostin — mga polyphenol na tinatawag na xanthones. Bawat kapsula ay naglalaman ng 500 mg ng standardized pericarp extract.

Katayuan sa Philippine FDA (Food and Drug Administration): Nakarehistrong food supplement (FR-4000009668333). Walang pinapayagang therapeutic claim.

Paano iniinom: 1 kapsula dalawang beses araw-araw; kabilang sa excipient ang magnesium stearate at gelatin capsule.Gigikanan: Panit (pericarp) sa bunga sa mangosteen (Garcinia mangostana L.)

Mga aktibong sangkap: Alpha-, beta-, ug gamma-mangostin — mga polyphenol nga gitawag og xanthones. Ang matag kapsula adunay 500 mg sa standardized pericarp extract.

Kahimtang sa Philippine FDA (Food and Drug Administration): Rehistradong food supplement (FR-4000009668333). Walay gitugotan nga therapeutic claim.

Giunsa pag-inom: 1 kapsula kaduha kada adlaw; ang mga excipient naglakip sa magnesium stearate ug gelatin capsule.Penibatan: Balat (pericarp) ning bungang mangosteen (Garcinia mangostana L.)

🔵 Spirulina

MX3 — Mangosteen Xanthones

Alpha-mangostin activates the Nrf2 pathway — a cellular master switch for antioxidant and anti-inflammatory enzyme production (HO-1, NQO1, SOD, GPx). In preclinical AKI (acute kidney injury) models, it significantly reduced serum creatinine, BUN (blood urea nitrogen), MDA, and ROS. Network pharmacology identified 10 hub targets including TNF, AKT1, NF-κB1, HIF-1A, and IL-6. One human RCT (randomized controlled trial) in healthy adults showed CRP (C-reactive protein) reduction over 30 days; kidney function tests were unchanged. No human trial in CKD patients exists.[1,2,3]Ang alpha-mangostin ay nagpapagana sa Nrf2 pathway — isang cellular master switch para sa produksyon ng antioxidant at anti-inflammatory na enzyme (HO-1, NQO1, SOD, GPx). Sa mga preclinical AKI (acute kidney injury) na modelo, makabuluhan nitong ibinaba ang serum creatinine, BUN (blood urea nitrogen), MDA, at ROS. Natukoy ng network pharmacology ang 10 hub target kabilang ang TNF, AKT1, NF-κB1, HIF-1A, at IL-6. Isang human RCT (randomized controlled trial) sa malulusog na adulto ang nagpakita ng pagbaba ng CRP (C-reactive protein) sa loob ng 30 araw; hindi nagbago ang mga kidney function test. Walang human trial sa mga pasyenteng may CKD na umiiral.[1,2,3]Ang alpha-mangostin nagpalihok sa Nrf2 pathway — usa ka cellular master switch alang sa produksyon sa antioxidant ug anti-inflammatory nga enzyme (HO-1, NQO1, SOD, GPx). Paragis — Eleusine indica

Vitexin and isovitexin are flavone C-glycosides with anti-inflammatory and antioxidant activity. Ethanolic extract increased urine output and electrolyte excretion (diuretic effect) and dissolved calcium oxalate crystals in animal models. No clinical trial in humans with any kidney disease has been completed. The Philippine Institute of Traditional and Alternative Health Care (PITAHC) formally states paragis cannot be claimed as a definitive treatment for any disease.[4,5,6]Ang vitexin at isovitexin ay mga flavone C-glycoside na may anti-inflammatory at antioxidant na aktibidad. Walang natapos na clinical trial sa tao na may anumang sakit sa bato.

Ang Iyong Puso, Bato, at Metabolismo — Isang Magkakaugnay na Krisis

Ang Imong Kasingkasing, Kidney, ug Metabolismo — Usa ka Magkadugtong nga Krisis

Ing Pusu, Bato, at Metabolismo Mu — Metung a Mikakaugne a Krisis

CKM stands for Cardiovascular-Kidney-Metabolic Syndrome — formally defined by the American Heart Association (AHA) and the American Society of Nephrology (ASN) in 2023. It links four conditions that share the same biological roots: heart disease, kidney disease, type 2 diabetes / metabolic dysfunction, and obesity.

Ang CKM ay nangangahulugang Cardiovascular-Kidney-Metabolic Syndrome — pormal na binigyang-kahulugan ng American Heart Association (AHA) at American Society of Nephrology (ASN) noong 2023. Pinag-uugnay nito ang apat na kondisyong may parehong biolohikong ugat: sakit sa puso, sakit sa bato, type 2 diabetes / metabolic dysfunction, at obesity.

Ang CKM nagpasabot ug Cardiovascular-Kidney-Metabolic Syndrome — pormal nga gihubit sa American Heart Association (AHA) ug American Society of Nephrology (ASN) niadtong 2023. Gisumpay niini ang upat ka kondisyon nga managsama ug biolohikanhong gamot: sakit sa kasingkasing, sakit sa kidney, type 2 diabetes / metabolic dysfunction, ug obesity.

Ing CKM kabaldugan na Cardiovascular-Kidney-Metabolic Syndrome — pormal a pikalbug ning American Heart Association (AHA) at American Society of Nephrology (ASN) anyang 2023. Pisusumpung na karing apat a kondisyon a misanmetung ya biolohiku a ugat: sakit king pusu, sakit king bato, type 2 diabetes / metabolic dysfunction, at obesity.

CKM matters because these conditions are not separate problems — they feed each other in biological feedback loops. High blood sugar damages kidney filters; failing kidneys raise blood pressure; high blood pressure strains the heart; and extra weight inflames everything at once. Each condition speeds up the others.

Mahalaga ang CKM dahil ang mga kondisyong ito ay hindi magkakahiwalay na problema — pinapalala nila ang isa't isa sa pamamagitan ng biolohikong feedback loops. Sinisira ng mataas na asukal sa dugo ang mga salaan ng bato; ang nasisirang bato ay nagpapataas ng presyon ng dugo; ang mataas na presyon ay nagpapahirap sa puso; at ang sobrang timbang ay nagpapamaga sa lahat nang sabay-sabay. Bawat kondisyon ay nagpapabilis sa iba.

Importante ang CKM kay kini nga mga kondisyon dili managlahi nga problema — nagpasamot sila sa usag-usa pinaagi sa biolohikanhong feedback loops. Ang taas nga asukal sa dugo modaot sa mga salaan sa kidney; ang naluya nga kidney mopataas sa presyon sa dugo; ang taas nga presyon mopabug-at sa kasingkasing; ug ang sobra nga timbang mohubag sa tanan dungan. Ang matag kondisyon mopadali sa uban.

Mahalaga ya ing CKM uli deng kondisyon a reni ali la mirakal a problema — pisusumpung da la reng metung at metung king biolohiku a feedung loops. Ing matas a asukal king daya seseran na reng salaan ning bato; ing maluya a bato pepatas na ing presyun ning daya; ing matas a presyun pepasakit na ing pusu; at ing sobra a timbang pepamaga na ngan a dake. Balang kondisyon pepabilis na reng aliwa.

This is why treating only the kidneys, or only the heart, often fails. Siloed care misses the shared pathways. A nephrologist who ignores the heart, or a cardiologist who ignores the kidneys, treats only half of one disease.

Ito ang dahilan kung bakit madalas nabibigo ang paggamot lamang sa bato, o sa puso lamang. Ang hiwa-hiwalay na pangangalaga ay hindi napapansin ang magkakatuwang na daluyan. Ang nephrologist na hindi pinapansin ang puso, o ang cardiologist na hindi pinapansin ang bato, ay ginagamot lamang ang kalahati ng isang sakit.

Mao kini ang hinungdan nganong kanunay mapakyas ang pagtambal lamang sa kidney, o sa kasingkasing lamang. Ang nagkabulag nga pag-atiman dili makakita sa kaubang dalan. Ang nephrologist nga wala magtagad sa kasingkasing, o ang cardiologist nga wala magtagad sa kidney, motambal lamang sa katunga sa usa ka sakit.

Iti ya ing sangkan nung baket pauli mapapali ing panlulu king bato mu, o king pusu mu. Ing mirakal a pamaglage ali na akakit deng misasaup a dalan. Ing nephrologist a ali na pepansinan ing pusu, o ing cardiologist a ali na pepansinan ing bato, mamanlulu mu king kapitna ning metung a sakit.

1. Sugar Overworks the Filter1. Pinapagod ng Asukal ang Salaan1. Gipakapoy sa Asukal ang Salaan1. Pepapagal ning Asukal ing Salaan

Insulin resistance forces the kidneys to filter too hard (hyperfiltration) — like a car engine revving too fast and running too hot. Over time the strained filters start leaking protein (proteinuria), and that damage becomes CKD.

Pinipilit ng insulin resistance ang mga bato na magsala nang sobra (hyperfiltration) — tulad ng makina ng kotse na umaandar nang sobrang bilis at sobrang init. Sa paglipas ng panahon, ang napapagod na salaan ay nagsisimulang tumagas ng protina (proteinuria), at ang pinsalang iyon ay nagiging CKD.

Gipugos sa insulin resistance ang mga kidney nga mosala ug sobra (hyperfiltration) — sama sa makina sa sakyanan nga modagan ug sobra ka paspas ug sobra ka init. Sa paglabay sa panahon, ang gikapoy nga salaan magsugod ug pagtulo ug protina (proteinuria), ug kanang kadaot mahimong CKD.

Pepilit ning insulin resistance deng bato a manyala lang sobra (hyperfiltration) — anti ning makina ning kotse a kakaras a sobra kasiplas at sobra kapali. Kabang lumalabas ing panaun, ing mepagal a salaan magumpisa yang tutulu protina (proteinuria), at ing pinsala iyan magi yang CKD.

2. Sick Kidneys Strain the Heart2. Pinapahirapan ng May-Sakit na Bato ang Puso2. Gipalisud sa Sakit nga Kidney ang Kasingkasing2. Pepasakit ning Masakit a Bato ing Pusu

Damaged kidneys hold on to fluid and let toxins build up. The extra fluid raises blood pressure, which forces the heart to pump against resistance. The heart muscle thickens, then weakens — leading to heart failure.

Ang nasirang bato ay nagtatabi ng tubig at hinahayaang mag-ipon ang mga lason. Ang sobrang tubig ay nagpapataas ng presyon ng dugo, na pumipilit sa puso na bumomba laban sa paghihigpit. Lumalapot ang kalamnan ng puso, pagkatapos ay humihina — na nagdudulot ng heart failure.

Ang nadaot nga kidney mogunit sa tubig ug motugot sa mga hilo nga motipon. Ang sobra nga tubig mopataas sa presyon sa dugo, nga mopugos sa kasingkasing nga mobomba batok sa pagsukol. Modako ang unod sa kasingkasing, dayon moluya — nga moresulta sa heart failure.

Ing mepinsala a bato itatabi na ing danum at paburen na reng lason a mititipun. Ing sobra a danum pepatas na ing presyun ning daya, a pepilit king pusu a bumomba salang king pamanlaban. Mangapal ya ing laman ning pusu, kaibat maluya — a magdala king heart failure.

3. Belly Fat Inflames Everything3. Pinapamaga ng Taba sa Tiyan ang Lahat3. Gipahubag sa Tambok sa Tiyan ang Tanan3. Pepamaga ning Taba King Atyan ing Anggang

Visceral fat — the deep "taba sa tiyan" — is not just storage. It releases inflammation chemicals into the blood that damage the kidney filters AND the heart's arteries at the same time.

Ang visceral fat — ang malalim na "taba sa tiyan" — ay hindi lamang imbakan. Naglalabas ito ng mga kemikal ng pamamaga sa dugo na sumisira sa mga salaan ng bato AT sa mga ugat ng puso nang sabay.

Ang visceral fat — ang lawom nga "tambok sa tiyan" — dili lang tipiganan. Nagpagawas kini ug mga kemikal sa hubag ngadto sa dugo nga modaot sa mga salaan sa kidney UG sa mga ugat sa kasingkasing dungan.

Ing visceral fat — ing malalam a "taba king atyan" — ali ya mu imbakan. Maglalual yang kemikal ning pamamaga king daya a seseran na reng salaan ning bato AT deng ugat ning pusu a dake.

How Excess Weight Damages the Kidneys

Paano Sinisira ng Sobrang Timbang ang Bato

Giunsa Pagdaot sa Sobra nga Timbang ang Kidney

Makananu Seseran ning Sobra a Timbang ing Bato

Excess weight causes a condition called obesity-related glomerulopathy. Fat that builds up around and inside the kidney physically compresses it, while the body forces the kidney to over-filter (hyperfiltration) to handle the larger body size. Both forces wear out the delicate filters and cause protein to leak into the urine.

Ang sobrang timbang ay nagdudulot ng kondisyon na tinatawag na obesity-related glomerulopathy. Ang tabang naiipon sa paligid at loob ng bato ay pisikal na pumipiga rito, habang pinipilit ng katawan ang bato na mag-sobra-sala (hyperfiltration) upang masapatan ang mas malaking laki ng katawan. Parehong puwersa ang nakakasira sa marupok na salaan at nagiging sanhi ng pagtagas ng protina sa ihi.

Ang sobra nga timbang maoy hinungdan sa kondisyon nga gitawag ug obesity-related glomerulopathy. Ang tambok nga motipon palibot ug sulod sa kidney pisikal nga mopit-os niini, samtang gipugos sa lawas ang kidney nga mosobra-sala (hyperfiltration) aron makaya ang mas dako nga kadako sa lawas. Ang duha ka pwersa modaot sa pino nga mga salaan ug maoy hinungdan sa pagtulo sa protina ngadto sa ihi.

Ing sobra a timbang magdala ya kondisyon a aus na obesity-related glomerulopathy. Ing taba a mititipun king paligid at lub ning bato pisikal nang pipiga, kabang pepilit ning katawan ing bato a manyala lang sobra (hyperfiltration) bang maganan ya ing mas maragul a laki ning katawan. Adua reng pwersa seseran da reng marupuk a salaan at magdala king pamantulu ning protina king ihi.

The good news: weight loss reverses some of this damage. Even a modest 5–10% loss of body weight reduces protein in the urine and slows kidney decline. The FLOW trial confirmed that GLP-1 medicines, which produce weight loss, protect the kidneys in people with type 2 diabetes and CKD.

Ang magandang balita: binabaligtad ng pagbaba ng timbang ang ilan sa pinsalang ito. Kahit ang katamtamang 5–10% na pagbaba ng timbang ay nagpapababa ng protina sa ihi at nagpapabagal sa paghina ng bato. Pinatunayan ng FLOW trial na ang mga gamot na GLP-1, na nagdudulot ng pagbaba ng timbang, ay nagpoprotekta sa bato ng mga taong may type 2 diabetes at CKD.

Ang maayong balita: ang pagkunhod sa timbang mobalik sa pipila niini nga kadaot. Bisan ang gamay nga 5–10% nga pagkunhod sa timbang mopakunhod sa protina sa ihi ug mopahinay sa pagluya sa kidney. Gipamatud-an sa FLOW trial nga ang mga tambal nga GLP-1, nga nagpakunhod sa timbang, manalipod sa kidney sa mga tawo nga adunay type 2 diabetes ug CKD.

Ing mayap a balita: ing pamananaba na ning timbang baliktaran na na ing mapilan kareng pinsala a reni. Maski ing kaunti a 5–10% a pamananaba na ning timbang pepababa na ing protina king ihi at pepabagal na ing pamaluya ning bato. Pepatunayan ning FLOW trial a deng gamut a GLP-1, a magdala king pamananaba ning timbang, peprotektan da reng bato karing taung atin type 2 diabetes at CKD.

Limitasyon sa hawak (DOH Pilipinas): Lalaki ≥90 cm ug Babaye ≥80 cm timailhan sa taas nga cardiorenal risk — bisan pa ug "normal" ang makita sa timbangan.

AHA-ASN Definition

AHA-ASN Staging Criteria

The 2023 AHA Presidential Advisory reframes cardiovascular, kidney, and metabolic disease as a single interconnected entity — Cardiovascular-Kidney-Metabolic (CKM) syndrome — rather than as discrete comorbidities managed in parallel. Critically, estimated glomerular filtration rate (eGFR) and urine albumin-to-creatinine ratio (UACR) are elevated to first-class staging inputs: kidney damage is not a downstream complication but a defining axis of the syndrome, and it is formally integrated into the AHA PREVENT risk equations that drive treatment decisions.

The conceptual shift: eGFR and UACR are no longer afterthoughts appended to a cardiometabolic risk profile. They are constitutive staging variables — a patient with preserved eGFR but A2/A3 albuminuria is already CKM Stage 2, and albuminuria independently escalates predicted CVD risk within the PREVENT framework regardless of glycemia or lipids.

CKM staging vs. Ronco cardiorenal syndrome

CKM staging is a longitudinal risk-stratification framework describing the chronic, progressive interplay of adiposity, metabolic dysfunction, and CKD across a lifetime. It should not be conflated with the Ronco cardiorenal syndrome (CRS) classification, which is a pathophysiologic/temporal taxonomy of acute and chronic bidirectional organ injury:

CRS Types 1 & 2

Cardiorenal — Type 1: acute heart failure → AKI; Type 2: chronic HF → progressive CKD.

CRS Types 3 & 4

Renocardiac — Type 3: AKI → acute cardiac injury/arrhythmia; Type 4: CKD → LVH, cardiomyopathy, accelerated ASCVD.

CRS Type 5

Secondary — systemic process (sepsis, amyloidosis, lupus) driving simultaneous cardiac and renal dysfunction.

In practice: use CRS to describe the mechanism and direction of an acute or chronic cardiorenal event at the bedside; use CKM staging to prognosticate and select preventive/disease-modifying therapy across the disease continuum.

Pathophysiology

Shared Pathophysiology

CKM syndrome is unified by a small set of overlapping mechanistic threads in which dysfunctional adiposity seeds neurohormonal, inflammatory, and metabolic-toxin cascades that injure the heart and kidney in tandem. Recognizing these shared drivers is what justifies the syndrome-level therapeutic approach — single agents (SGLT2 inhibitors, finerenone, GLP-1 RAs) interrupt several threads at once.

RAAS activation loop

Adiposity- and CKD-driven RAAS upregulation raises angiotensin II and aldosterone, producing efferent arteriolar vasoconstriction, glomerular hypertension, sodium retention, fibrosis, and adverse cardiac remodeling — a self-reinforcing loop that albuminuria both reflects and amplifies.

Adipokine profile

Dysfunctional visceral fat yields leptin resistance (sympathetic overactivity, sodium retention) and adiponectin deficiency (loss of insulin-sensitizing, anti-inflammatory, and anti-fibrotic tone), tilting the milieu toward insulin resistance and endothelial dysfunction.

FGF-23 / Klotho axis

Early CKD raises FGF-23 and depletes soluble Klotho. FGF-23 independently drives left ventricular hypertrophy and is a potent cardiovascular risk marker; Klotho deficiency removes a vasculoprotective, anti-fibrotic, anti-aging factor — coupling mineral-bone disorder to cardiac injury.

Uremic toxin cardiovascular toxicity

Protein-bound uremic retention solutes — indoxyl sulfate and p-cresyl sulfate (gut-derived, poorly dialyzable) — promote endothelial dysfunction, vascular calcification, oxidative stress, and cardiac fibrosis. Gut-microbiome-derived TMAO independently predicts atherosclerotic and heart-failure events. These solutes mechanistically explain why cardiovascular risk in CKD far exceeds what traditional risk factors predict.

⚡

Neurohormonal activation

  • Sympathetic nervous system: renal afferent signaling and leptin drive sustained sympathetic overactivity → hypertension, tachycardia, and arrhythmogenesis.
  • Aldosterone: mineralocorticoid receptor overactivation (beyond volume effects) drives cardiac and renal inflammation and fibrosis — the rationale for non-steroidal MRA (finerenone).
  • Endothelin: the ET-1 system promotes vasoconstriction, podocyte injury, and albuminuria, and is an emerging therapeutic target in CKM-associated CKD.
PREVENT Equation

The PREVENT Risk Calculator

The AHA PREVENT (Predicting Risk of cardiovascular disease EVENTs) equations supersede the 2013 ACC/AHA Pooled Cohort Equations (PCE) for primary-prevention risk estimation in CKM syndrome. PREVENT was derived in a large, contemporary, racially diverse US cohort, removes race as an input, and — central to CKM — incorporates eGFR as a core variable, with optional enhancement using UACR and HbA1c and a social-deprivation index. It estimates both 10-year and 30-year risk of total CVD (ASCVD plus heart failure). The calculator is freely available through the ACC/AHA online tool.

Because PREVENT folds kidney function and albuminuria directly into the risk estimate, it operationalizes the CKM thesis: a patient's renal axis now shifts the predicted CVD risk that triggers statin and (in appropriate patients) SGLT2-inhibitor and risk-factor intensification — rather than being scored separately.

🔢

Practical thresholds

Use PREVENT-estimated risk (with eGFR/UACR entered) to set CKM-adjusted thresholds for statin initiation and to identify patients in whom kidney/metabolic-protective agents — SGLT2 inhibitors in CKD/HF/diabetes and finerenone in diabetic CKD with albuminuria — are indicated independent of, and additive to, lipid-lowering decisions. Document the 30-year estimate when counseling younger CKM patients, in whom 10-year risk understates lifetime burden.

Treatment Algorithm

Stage-Based Treatment Algorithm

Therapy is layered by CKM stage. Each higher stage retains the interventions of the lower stages and adds disease-modifying, organ-protective agents. The goal is early, simultaneous interruption of the shared pathophysiologic threads rather than sequential single-organ management.

StageCore interventions
0–1Structured lifestyle therapy (Mediterranean-style diet, physical activity, weight management); screening for HTN, dysglycemia, dyslipidemia, and albuminuria. Establish baseline eGFR + UACR.
2RAAS blockade (ACEi or ARB) titrated to max tolerated dose + SGLT2 inhibitor for CKD/diabetes/HF indications. Add finerenone in type 2 diabetes with albuminuric CKD and acceptable K+. Statin per PREVENT risk.
3Add GLP-1 receptor agonist for obesity/diabetes and cardiovascular/renal benefit; intensify CVD risk reduction (moderate-to-high-intensity statin, BP to target, antiplatelet where indicated).
4Full guideline-directed medical therapy for established HF (ARNI/ACEi-ARB, beta-blocker, MRA, SGLT2i) and/or ASCVD, with renal-adjusted dosing and coordinated cardiology–nephrology co-management. In 4b, dialysis-modality and transplant-candidacy planning.
⚠️

Expected eGFR dip on SGLT2 inhibitor initiation

A modest acute eGFR decline (typically up to ~30%) in the first weeks after starting an SGLT2 inhibitor is an expected hemodynamic effect — reduced intraglomerular pressure from afferent vasoconstriction via restored tubuloglomerular feedback. It is reversible, renoprotective long-term, and not a reason to discontinue. Do not stop the drug for an anticipated dip; recheck and confirm stabilization. The same hemodynamic dip is expected after RAAS blockade and finerenone initiation.

Drug-initiation monitoring

  • SGLT2 inhibitor: check eGFR at ~2–4 weeks then q3 months; anticipate and accept the initial eGFR dip; counsel on euglycemic DKA and genital mycotic infections; hold during acute illness/volume depletion (sick-day rules).
  • Finerenone: baseline K+ and eGFR; recheck K+ at ~4 weeks (and within ~2 weeks after dose adjustment), then periodically. Do not start if K+ >5.0 mmol/L; adjust/withhold per K+ algorithm.
  • ACEi/ARB: check eGFR and K+ within 1–2 weeks of initiation or up-titration; tolerate a ≤30% creatinine rise.
  • GLP-1 RA: titrate slowly for GI tolerability; review for retinopathy, pancreatitis history, and renal dose considerations.
  • Combined therapy: when RAAS blockade + finerenone are co-prescribed, monitor K+ more intensively and counsel against high-potassium salt substitutes.
Philippine Context

The Philippine Context

Translating the CKM framework into Philippine practice requires accounting for fragmented primary care, out-of-pocket medication costs, and uneven specialist access. As of ~2025, the Philippine Society of Nephrology (PSN) and the Philippine Society of Cardiology (PSC) have moved toward a joint position on integrated cardiorenal-metabolic care, mirroring the AHA-ASN direction; clinicians should anchor management to formal CKM staging while adapting drug selection to local availability and PhilHealth coverage.

NKTI screening

The National Kidney and Transplant Institute supports CKM-relevant screening initiatives and serves as a tertiary referral and KRT hub; leverage it for complex co-management and transplant evaluation.

Referral triggers

Refer for nephrology–cardiology co-management with: eGFR <30, rapidly declining eGFR, A3 albuminuria, refractory hypertension or hyperkalemia, HF with CKD, or any CKM Stage 4 patient.

Formulary navigation

Where SGLT2i/finerenone/GLP-1 RA are unaffordable, maximize accessible therapy (max-dose RAAS blockade, statin, BP and glycemic control) and document the cost barrier for stepped escalation.

🏥

Integrated risk scoring at the RHU / barangay level

CKM screening can be operationalized with low-cost tools already feasible at the Rural Health Unit and barangay level: urinalysis (dipstick proteinuria) + fasting blood sugar + waist circumference + blood pressure. This four-item bundle stages most patients into CKM 0–2, identifies albuminuria early, and triggers timely RAAS blockade and statin therapy — high-yield, sustainable interventions that do not depend on access to the costlier drug classes.

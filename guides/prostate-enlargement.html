The prostate gland surrounds the urethra just below the bladder. As men age, the prostate naturally enlarges — a process called benign prostatic hyperplasia (BPH). The enlarging gland squeezes the urethra, making it harder to urinate. By age 60, over 50% of Filipino men have BPH; by age 85, over 90% are affected. It is not cancer — but untreated, it can damage the kidneys.Ang prostate gland ay nakapaligid sa urethra nang direkta sa ibaba ng pantog.

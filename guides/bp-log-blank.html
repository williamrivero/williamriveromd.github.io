How to use: Take BP at the same time each session. Sit quietly for 5 minutes before measuring. Record Systolic / Diastolic (e.g., 130/80) and pulse. Note medications taken (Y/N) and any symptoms.
When to call your doctor: SBP ≥ 180 or DBP ≥ 110 mmHg (hypertensive crisis) — seek care immediately.

Saturated Fat

Philippine Pick: Rice Bran OilPili sa Pilipinas: Rice Bran OilPinili sa Pilipinas: Rice Bran OilPili king Pilipinas: Rice Bran Oil

For most Filipino patients, rice bran oil (Doña Elena, King brand, ~₱150–200/L) offers the best combination of evidence quality, affordability, local availability, and functional cooking properties. Its unique γ-oryzanol compound blocks intestinal cholesterol absorption through the same mechanism as the drug ezetimibe — but as a food. High smoke point (232°C) is ideal for ginisa, stir-fry, and everyday Filipino cooking.[6]Para sa karamihan ng pasyenteng Pilipino, ang rice bran oil (Doña Elena, King brand, ~₱150–200/L) ay nag-aalok ng pinakamahusay na kumbinasyon ng kalidad ng ebidensya, abot-kaya, lokal na availability, at functional na katangian sa pagluluto. Ang natatangi nitong γ-oryzanol compound ay humaharang sa pagsipsip ng cholesterol sa bituka sa pamamagitan ng parehong mekanismo tulad ng gamot na ezetimibe — ngunit bilang pagkain.

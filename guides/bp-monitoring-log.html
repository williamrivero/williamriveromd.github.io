7-Day Blood Pressure Monitoring Log

Bring this to your next consultation · Para sa inyong doktor

How to use: Measure twice daily — morning (before medications, before eating) and evening. Take 2 readings per session with 1 minute between. Record both and the average auto-calculates. Bring this printed log to every clinic visit.

🚨 When to Go to the Emergency Room

  • BP > 180/120 mmHg — with or without symptoms
  • Sudden severe headache, blurred vision, or vision loss
  • Chest pain, shortness of breath, or leg weakness

CKD anemia: iron decisions
Adult clinical reference · Initiation and ongoing treatment are different decisions
Population / decisionKDIGO 2026 guidance
Anemia + hemodialysis (HD): initiate ironFerritin ≤500 ng/mL and TSAT ≤30%. IV iron is suggested; use a proactive, monitored approach.
Anemia + non-dialysis CKD or peritoneal dialysis (PD): initiate ironFerritin <100 ng/mL and TSAT <40%, or ferritin ≥100 and <300 ng/mL and TSAT <25%.
Route outside HDChoose oral or IV iron using severity, response, tolerance, preference, availability, and cost. Switch an optimal oral regimen to IV if ineffective after 1–3 months or poorly tolerated.
Withhold routine ironFerritin >700 ng/mL or TSAT ≥40%. An elevated ferritin alone does not diagnose iron overload.
Systemic infectionConsider temporarily suspending iron. Inflammation without infection needs clinical assessment rather than an automatic infection diagnosis.
Monitoring during iron treatmentHb, ferritin, TSAT every 3 months in non-dialysis CKD/PD; every 1–3 months in HD, often monthly. Test sooner after blood loss, hospitalization, ESA escalation, or rapidly changing iron indices.

Before acting

Confirm anemia and evaluate symptoms, bleeding, infection, iron exposure, and reversible causes. Ferritin can rise with inflammation. A high ferritin alone does not establish iron overload. Respect formulation-specific dose and infusion limits.

ESA review and monthly HD checklist
Adult clinical reference · Use clinical judgment and the locally approved product label
DecisionKDIGO 2026 guidance
Adult HD or PD: consider initiationAfter reversible causes are addressed, consider ESA when Hb is ≤9–10 g/dL. Select the threshold within that range using symptoms, risks, and patient preferences.
Non-dialysis CKD, including transplant recipientsIndividualize initiation using symptoms, potential benefit, transfusion avoidance, ESA risks, and preferences; Hb <10 alone is not an automatic start order.
Adult maintenance goalTarget Hb below 11.5 g/dL (115 g/L), individualized. A common working range is about 10 to <11.5 g/dL; this is not an anemia diagnostic range.
Dose changesUse the lowest effective dose. Generally avoid adjustments more often than every 4 weeks. If Hb rises >1 g/dL in 2–4 weeks, reduce by 25–50%. Prefer reduction to holding while Hb does not exceed 11.5, as clinically appropriate.
MonitoringHb every 2–4 weeks after initiation or adjustment; at least every 3 months during maintenance, usually more often in HD.
Stroke, thrombosis, and cancerConsider suspending ESA during hospitalization for acute stroke, vascular access thrombosis, or thromboembolism. Active or previous cancer calls for shared decision-making, especially when treatment aims at cure. Uncontrolled hypertension and product-specific contraindications require review.

Additional safeguards

Trend ERI; no universal KDIGO cutoff of 10 or 14. Do not combine ESA and HIF-PHI. HIF-PHI trials in hyporesponsiveness require discussion of limited evidence and discontinuation after 3–4 months without the desired response. For chronic CKD anemia, transfusion decisions are based on symptoms and signs rather than an arbitrary Hb threshold.

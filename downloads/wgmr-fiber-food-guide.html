3 Fiber Supplements
Psyllium husk (C-Lium)
3–5 g / serving
Best all-round; very kidney-friendly. Drink extra water.
Shirataki rice
~1 g / cup
Ultra kidney-friendly; near-zero K & P. Good rice substitute.
Chia seeds
10 g / 2 tbsp
Easy to add to lugaw or water. Omega-3 bonus.
Flaxseed (ground)
5–6 g / 2 tbsp
Omega-3 bonus. Use ground for absorption.
Oat bran
6–8 g / ½ cup
Beta-glucan lowers LDL. Good in porridge.
4 CKD & Dialysis Notes
Important for Kidney Patients
  • Psyllium and shirataki are the most kidney-friendly options — low potassium, low phosphorus.
  • CKD Stage 4–5 / dialysis: limit avocado, monggo, and guava due to high potassium and phosphorus — check amounts with your dietitian.
  • Prunes work well for constipation (7 g fiber + sorbitol effect) but are HIGH in potassium — limit to 2–3 pieces in CKD 4–5/dialysis.
  • Sinigang mix powder contains 0 g fiber. Use fresh sampaloc pulp (eaten, not strained) to get the fiber benefit from tamarind.
5 The Fiber Myth
Common Misconception
"A big green salad ≈ 2–3 g fiber.  Half cup monggo ≈ 8 g.   Fiber ≠ vegetables."

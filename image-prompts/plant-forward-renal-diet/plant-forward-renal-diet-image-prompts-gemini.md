## How this differs from the OpenAI version

Gemini's image generators respond best to:

- **Flowing natural language** rather than bulleted structural blocks — write like you're briefing a photographer or illustrator, not filling out a form.
- **Short, quoted text strings** that appear verbatim in the image. Long paragraphs of on-image text render unreliably. Keep any typeset copy to headline + subtitle + attribution.
- **Concrete style anchors** ("in the visual register of a *Kidney International* graphical abstract", "styled like a Bon Appétit food editorial") — they steer the aesthetic much harder than adjective lists.
- **Explicit aspect ratio hints** in the prompt (`16:9 landscape`, `1:1 square`, `2:3 portrait`). The API's native output sizes are 1024×1024, 1408×768, 1248×832, 832×1248, and 768×1408 — target dimensions below are the final crop/upscale sizes needed for the site.
- **Negatives woven into prose** ("no serifs, no dialysis equipment in frame, no hands, no dark backgrounds") rather than a separate negative list at the end.
- **Precise light/mood/color descriptors** ("bright natural daylight from upper-left", "soft off-white background #fafafa", "restrained clinical palette anchored on navy #0f1e2e and clinical teal #1a6b72").

House-style constants preserved on every panel: light backgrounds only; typography drawn from the four approved sans-serifs (Inter, Manrope, Nunito Sans, IBM Plex Sans); navy/teal/green/amber/red accent palette; `williamriveromd.com` attribution rendered small and semi-transparent in the bottom-right corner (bottom-center for portrait).

# Source register — Nephrology Nursing & Hemodialysis

Review date for every row: **2026-11-23**. Keys match `tools/nursing/content/common.py` and the `[[key]]` citations in the module files. `data/nursing-course/refs-used.json` lists which pages cite each key; `QUIZ-KEY-REGISTER.md` maps every scored item to its sources.

**How each source was checked**

- **A = opened and read** during this build (the relevant section was retrieved and read).
- **B = bibliographic details verified in PubMed** (authors, journal, year, volume, pages, DOI) on the review date; the clinical statements attributed to it are well-established guideline content but were **not re-read page by page**. A clinical reviewer should confirm the section or recommendation number before publication.
- **C = publisher catalog or summary page only** (the full text is paywalled or was not retrieved).

## Claims that depend on policy, orders, manufacturer instructions, or a standard edition

These are flagged per item in `QUIZ-KEY-REGISTER.md` and worded on the pages so that the local source governs:

- Oxygen titration, fluid bolus volumes, and rapid-response activation (Modules 1, 4, 9): hospital policy and physician order.
- Hyperkalemia emergency treatment (Module 4): physician order or approved protocol.
- Ultrafiltration-rate threshold of 13 mL/kg per hour (Module 6): observational; presented as a prompt to call the physician.
- Which treatment inputs are added to the fluid goal, and rinse-back volume (Module 6): unit procedure and machine type.
- Antiseptic agent, contact time, hub scrub time, and whether a contaminated line is replaced (Module 7): product instructions and unit procedure. No times are quoted.
- Hepatitis B isolation and machine assignment (Module 7): CDC 2001 and PSN 2024; the DOH assessment tool was not re-read.
- Water limits and action levels (Module 8): the facility's adopted standard and edition; values shown for orientation. Endotoxin testing frequency is not stated in the Philippine sources read.
- Machine and circuit checks (Module 8): manufacturer's manual.
- Emergency sequences, including whether blood is returned and positioning for air entry (Module 9): unit protocol; medical director review required.
- Empirical antibiotics for peritonitis, exit-site agent and frequency, number of observed exchanges (Module 10): PD unit protocol.
- Immunosuppressant missed-dose rules; pediatric circuit volume and prescriptions; dialysis intensity in pregnancy (Module 11): program or specialist team.
- PhilHealth entitlements (Modules 5, 10): deliberately not quantified; current circulars.

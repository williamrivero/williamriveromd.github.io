# Medical Records Officer track: image prompt pack

Stage 1 prompts for ChatGPT image generation. Heroes follow `williamriveromd-hero-vignette` v3 (2048×2048, circular vignette, no text, no watermark). Figures follow `williamriveromd-infographic-skill` v5 (light background, Inter, attribution `renalcarematters.com`), rendered at 1672×941 to match the other Academy figures. No image may show a real name, number, or legible identifier.

**Workflow.** Generate PNGs, drop them in `~/Documents/Medical Records Officer/Images/` for audit, then convert to webp and place under `images/` with the file names below (the generator reads the names in `tools/techcourses/mro/figs.py`; heroes as `<slug>-vignette-hero.png` plus a webp copy). Card images (`medical-records-officer-card.webp`, `medical-records-officer-NN-card.webp`, 640×360) are cut from the heroes with `tools/techcourses/prep_assets.py`.

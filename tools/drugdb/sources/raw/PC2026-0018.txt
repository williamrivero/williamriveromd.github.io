             I.        RATIONALE


                       Pursuant to Republic Act No. 11223 (Universal Health Care Act), Republic Act
                       No. 10767 (Comprehensive Tuberculosis Elimination Plan Act), and
                       PhilHealth Board Resolution No. 3161 s. 2026, the Philippine Health
                       Insurance Corporation (PhilHealth) hereby integrates the pharmaceutical
                       component of Drug-Susceptible Tuberculosis (DS-TB) management into the
                       PhilHealth Guaranteed and Accessible Medications for Outpatient Treatment
                       (GAMOT) Program.

                      While the Outpatient Anti-Tuberculosis Directly Observed Treatment
                      Short-Course (TB-DOTS) Package under PhilHealth Circular No. 2014-0014
                      remains the primary mechanism for comprehensive TB care, this Circular
                      establishes a supplemental mechanism enabling accredited Primary Care
                      Clinics (PC Clinics) and accredited Primary Care Physicians to prescribe
                      first-line anti-TB drugs for dispensing through accredited GAMOT Facilities,
                      facilitating increased access to quality-assured medications at the point of
                       care.



        H.             OBJECTIVES


                       This Circular aims to outline guidelines for PhilHealth accredited PC Clinics,
                      TB-DOTS Facilities, and GAMOT Facilities.

    III.   SCOPE


           This Circular encompasses the rules governing access to outpatient first-line
           anti-TB drugs for eligible PhilHealth members and dependents diagnosed
           with DS-TB through the PhilHealth GAMOT Program.

    IV.    DEFINITION OF TERMS


           For the purpose of this Circular, the operational definitions of terms used in
           this issuance are the following:

           A. Authorized Physician - accredited physician with a PhilHealth GAMOT
              Application account.

           B. Continuation Phase - the second phase of tuberculosis treatment
              focused on eliminating residual bacilli, typically lasting 2 to 10 months
              using a reduced combination of anti-TB drugs.

           C. First-line anti-TB drugs - a standard fixed-dose combination (FDC) or
              single-drug formulation used for the primary treatment of
              drug-susceptible tuberculosis as defined in the Department of Health
              National TB Control Program (NTP) Manual of Procedures (MOP) 6th
              edition.


           D. GAMOT Application (GAMOT App) - a software that can be
              interoperable with existing operating systems, used to prescribe
              medications, validate prescriptions, record dispensing, and monitor
              performance of GAMOT Facilities, as applicable.

           E. GAMOT Facility - a drug dispensing facility that is engaged by
              PhilHealth to provide the contents of the outpatient drug benefit package.

           F. Intensive Phase - the initial phase of tuberculosis treatment aimed at
              rapidly killing active bacilli, typically lasting 2 months using a four-drug
              combination regimen.

         V.   POLICY STATEMENTS

              A. General Guidelines


                 1. This Circular shall serve as a supportive measure to promote treatment
                    initiation and prevent treatment interruption for members and
                    dependents diagnosed with DS-TB of first-line anti-TB drugs hy
                    incorporating these medications into PhilHealth GAMOT.

                 2. Accredited TB-DOTS Facilities with adequate and sustainable supplies
                    of first-line anti-TB drugs shall continue providing comprehensive
                    care—including directly dispensing anti-TB medications—and filing
                    their full claims under the standard Outpatient TB-DOTS Package.

                 3. PhilHealth Circular (PC) No. 2014-0014 (Revised Guidelines for the
                    PhilHealth Outpatient Anti-Tuberculosis Directly Observed Treatment
                    Short-Course [DOTS] Benefit Package) remains the primary policy
                    guidelines for the overall conduct, accreditation, and filing of claims
                    under the Outpatient TB-DOTS Package.

                 4. PhilHealth Circular Nos. 2025-0013 (Nationwide Implementation of
                    PhilHealth Guaranteed and Accessible Medications for Outpatient
                    Treatment), 2026-0002 (Supplemental Guidelines for the Nationwide
                    Implementation of PhilHealth Guaranteed and Accessible Medications
                    for Outpatient Treatment [GAMOT]), and 2026-0007 (Clinical and
                    Pharmaceutical Standards for PhilHealth Yaman ng Kalusugan
                    Program [YAKAP]) remains the governing policies for the general
                    conduct, accreditation, quality standards, and filing of claims under the
                    PhilHealth GAMOT Program.

               2. Dispensing of First-Line Anti-TB Drugs by GAMOT Facilities

                    a. The GAMOT Facility shall dispense in full the prescribed first-line
                       anti-TB drugs within two (2) days from the date of GAMOT
                       prescription issuance. Beyond the claiming period, the medications
                       can still be dispensed by the pharmacy but paid out of pocket. The
                       GAMOT prescription shall be updated by the authorized physician if
                       the medications remain medically necessary.

                    b. A maximum       of one month’s worth       of medications     shall be
                       dispensed (Annex C: PhilHealth GAMOT First-Line Anti-TB Drugs
                       Dispensing Limits).

               3. Payment Mechanism

                    a. First-line anti-TB drugs dispensed under PhilHealth GAMOT shall
                       not be deductible from the member's Annual Benefit Limit (ABL).


                    b. Accredited   GAMOT     Facilities   shall be reimbursed directly by
                       PhilHealth based on the fixed fee set for first-line anti-TB drugs
                       (Annex A.2: GAMOT Fixed Fee Schedule).

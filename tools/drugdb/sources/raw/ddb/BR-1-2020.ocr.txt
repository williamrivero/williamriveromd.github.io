OFFICE OF THE PRESIDENT
DANGEROUS DRUGS BOARD
BOARD REGULATION NO. 1
Series of 2020

SUBJECT: IMMEDIATE ADOPTION OF CHANGES IN THE SCOPE OF CONTROL OF

SUBSTANCES PURSUANT TO THE THREE (3) UNITED NATIONS

INTERNATIONAL DRUG CONVENTIONS

WHEREAS, Republic Act No. 9165 or the Comprehensive Dangerous Drugs Act
of 2002, as amended (the “Act”), provides that the Dangerous Drugs Board shall be the
policy — making and strategy — formulating body in the planning of policies and programs
on drug prevention and control;

WHEREAS, the Act also mandates that the Board shall promulgate such rules and
regulations as may be necessary to carry out the purposes of the Act;

WHEREAS, the Philippines is a party to three (3) United Nations International Drug
Conventions: the 1961 Single Convention on Narcotic Drugs, The 1971 Convention on
Psychotropic Substances, and the 1988 Convention Against Illicit Traffic in Narcotic
Drugs and Psychotropic Substances;

WHEREAS, the Conventions aim to promote cooperation among party — states in
addressing the various aspects of illicit trafficking of narcotic drugs and psychotropic
substances;

WHEREAS, the Conventions provide that the Commission on Narcotic Drugs
(“CND") shall have the power to change the scope of control of substances through
amendment of the Schedules annexed to each Convention, and party — states shall take
such measures to give effect and carry out the regulatory mechanisms provided for
substances listed in the Schedules of the Conventions;

WHEREAS, Article 3 (7) (Changes in the Scope of Control) of the 1961 Single
Convention on Narcotic Drugs states:

“Any decision of the Commission (on Narcotic Drugs) taken pursuant to
this article shall be communicated by the Secretary General to all State
Members of the United Nations, to non — member States Parties to this
Convention, to the World Health Organization and to the (International
Narcotics Control) Board. Such decision shall become effective with
respect to each Party on the date of its receipt of such communication,
and the Parties shall thereupon take such action as may be required
under this Convention”;

WHEREAS, Article 2 (7) (Scope of Control of Substances) of the 1971 Convention
on Psychotropic Substances provides:

“Any decision of the Commission (on Narcotic Drugs) taken pursuant
to this article shall be communicated by the Secretary General to all
States Members of the United Nations, to non-member States Parties
to this Convention, to the World Health Organization and to the
===== p-2.png =====
(International Narcotics Control) Board. Such decision shall become
fully effective with respect to each Party 180 days after the date of
such communication’;

WHEREAS, Article 12 (6) (Substances Frequently Used in the Illicit Manufacture
of Narcotic Drugs or Psychotropic Substances) of the 1988 Convention Against Illicit
Traffic in Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances states:

“Any decision of the Commission (on Narcotic Drugs) taken pursuant to
this article shall be communicated by the Secretary General to all States
and other entities which are, or which are entitled to become, Parties to
the Convention, and to the (International Narcotics Control) Board. Such
decision shall become fully effective with respect to each Party one
hundred and eighty days after the date of such communication’;

WHEREAS, the Conventions clearly indicate that there is a need to immediately
adopt actions undertaken by the Commission on Narcotic Drugs relative to changes in
scope of control of substances listed in the Schedules, subject to the periods stated in
each Convention.

THEREFORE, be it RESOLVED, as it is hereby RESOLVED, that pursuant to the
three (3) United Nations International Drug Conventions: the 1961 Single Convention on
Narcotic Drugs, The 1971 Convention on Psychotropic Substances, and the 1988
Convention Against Illicit Traffic in Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances, there
shall be immediate adoption of changes in the scope of control of substances as reflected
in the Schedules of the Convention;

RESOLVED FURTHER, that in order to implement such immediate adoption, the
Dangerous Drugs Board shall authorize the Chairman to issue a Regulation for such
purpose, with regulatory and penal mechanisms taking effect after registration thereof
with the Office of the National Administrative Register and publication in two (2)
newspapers of general circulation, Provided, that the Chairman and / or the Executive
Director shall inform the Board of the issuance of such Regulation before the schedule of
the next Regular Meeting of the Board.

APPROVED and ADOPTED, this 18" day of June in the year of our Lord, 2020 in
Quezon City.

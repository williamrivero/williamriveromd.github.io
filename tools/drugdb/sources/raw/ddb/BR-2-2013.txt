                                   BOARD REGULATION No. 2
                                                  Series of 2013

       SUBJECT: INCLUSION OF METHYLEPHEDRINE                                                  IN      THE       LIST       OF
                DANGEROUS DRUGS

       WHEREAS, pursuant to Section 93, Article XI of Republic Act No. 9165 or “The
Comprehensive Dangerous Drugs Act of 2002”, the Board is given the power to
reclassify, add to or remove from the list of dangerous drugs any dangerous drug;

       WHEREAS, Methylephedrine, a derivative of Ephedrine which is used in other
countries as a decongestant and bronchodilator, may induce increased blood pressure,
cardio-vascular stress and priapism but also has high potential for abuse as reported in
other countries and recognized scientific journals;

      WHEREAS, the Philippines have enough medications in the market tha can
produce the same therapeutic effects as that of Methylephedrine;

       WHEREAS, Methylephedrine is also a precursor of Ephedrine, a controlled
substance under the 1988 United Nations Convention against Illicit Traffic in Narcotic
Drugs and Psychotropic Substances, as evidenced by the presence of Methylephedrine
in clandestine drug laboratories during various operations by the Philippine National
Police and the Philippine Drug Enforcement Agency;

      WHEREAS, the World Anti-Doping Agency has included Methylephedrine in the
2013 Prohibited List, International Standard under item S6(b) as a prohibited stimulant;

      WHEREAS, during the public hearing last May 28, 2013 initiated by the DDB
Committee on Public Hearings, various stakeholders supported the proposed inclusion
of Methylephedrine in the list of dangerous drugs.

       WHEREFORE, be it RESOLVED, as it is hereby RESOLVED, to include
METHYLEPHEDRINE in the list of dangerous drugs and all compounds, mixtures and
preparations containing this substance, including its salts, isomers and salts of isomers,
are classified as dangerous drugs which shall be subject to all regulatory and control
measures provided for in the Comprehensive Dangerous Drugs Act of 2002 and rules
and regulations promulgated by the Board

     APPROVED and ADOPTED, this _12th day of September, in the year of
Our Lord, 2013 in Quezon City.

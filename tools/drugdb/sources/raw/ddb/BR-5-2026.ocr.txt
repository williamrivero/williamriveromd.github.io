OFFICE OF THE PRESIDENT
DANGEROUS DRUGS BOARD
BOARD REGULATION NO. 5
Series of 2026
SUBJECT: EXTENSION OF AUTHORIZATION FOR ORDINARY
PRESCRIPTIONS FOR DANGEROUS DRUGS FOR A PERIOD OF
ONE (1) YEAR

WHEREAS, the mandate and jurisdiction of the Dangerous Drugs Board are
enumerated in Republic Act No. 9165, as amended—otherwise known as the
“Comprehensive Dangerous Drugs Act of 2002”—which requires the use of special
prescription forms for the dispensation of dangerous drugs under normal
circumstances;

WHEREAS, the Dangerous Drugs Board, through Section 40(b) of Republic
Act No. 9165, as amended, may nevertheless authorize the use of ordinary
prescriptions in times of public emergency;

WHEREAS, the Board recently exercised this power through Board Regulation
No. 10, series of 2025, as a response to then global public health concerns;

WHEREAS, the authorization to use ordinary prescriptions under Board
Regulation No. 10 is set to expire on June 30, 2026;

WHEREAS, extending the use of ordinary prescription is a proportionate
regulatory response to prevent disruption in treatment, avoid undue burden on medical
practitioners and patients, and uphold the state’s obligation to balance drug control
with public health and patient welfare, until such time that the DOH resumes full
availability of the Special Prescription Forms;

WHEREFORE, the Board, by exercising its authority under Section 40(b) of
Republic Act No. 9165, as amended, hereby promulgates this Regulation:

1
Board Regulation No. 5, Series of 2026
===== p-2.png =====
Section 1. DECLARATION OF POLICY—The Board extends the authorization
to use ordinary prescriptions in order to allow the dispensing of medicines and
pharmaceutical products classified as dangerous drugs, thereby relieving
stakeholders of the transportation, logistics, and financial burdens of obtaining special
prescription forms from the Department of Health.

Section 2, COVERAGE—This Regulation applies to medical practitioners with
a valid and subsisting S2 license.

Section 3. IMPLEMENTATION AND DURATION—The guidelines and
protocols on the issuance of ordinary prescriptions for dangerous drugs under Board
Regulation No. 10, series of 2025, are extended until June 30, 2027.

Section 4. SUSPENSION AND REPEAL OF PREVIOUS REGULATIONS—
Section 9 of Board Regulation No. 10, series of 2025, is expressly repealed.

The contrary provisions of Board Regulation No. 1, series of 2014, as well as other
regulations, issuances, and rules inconsistent with this Regulation, are hereby
suspended during the effectivity of this Regulation.

Section 5. DISSEMINATION—Upon approval of this Regulation by the Board,

a copy thereof, as well as a soft copy of Board Regulation No. 10, series of 2025, shall
be publicized in all the digital platforms of the Dangerous Drugs Board and the
Philippine Drug Enforcement Agency.
Copies of the two Board Regulations, digital or otherwise, shall also be disseminated
by the Board, through the Secretariat, to its network of medical associations, reputable
newspapers and publications, and other key private sector stakeholders, to increase
awareness of the policy among pharmacists, physicians, and patients as soon as
possible.

Section 6. SEPARABILITY—If any provision or part of this Regulation is
declared unauthorized, unconstitutional, or invalid by any court of law, those provisions
not affected by such declaration shall remain valid and effective.

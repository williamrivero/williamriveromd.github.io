                                   BOARD REGULATION No. 1
                                                  Series of 2008

       SUBJECT:          INCLUSION OF ORIPAVINE                                  IN      THE         LIST        OF
                         DANGEROUS DRUGS

       WHEREAS, pursuant to Section 93, Article XI of Republic Act No. 9165,
otherwise known as the Comprehensive Dangerous Drugs Act of 2002, the Dangerous
Drugs Board (DDB), after due notice and public hearing, is given the power to
reclassify, add to or remove from the list of dangerous drugs any drug or substance;

       WHEREAS, under the same section of the said law, the Board, in the exercise
of this power, shall take into accord its obligations and commitments to international
treaties, conventions and agreements to which the Philippines is a signatory;

       WHEREAS, Oripavine which is an opiate and major metabolite of Thebaine and
easily convertible to Morphine, has a potential for abuse and is not clinically used due to
its severe toxicity effects;

      WHEREAS, the United Nations Commission on Narcotic Drugs (CND), through
CND Declaration 50/1, has classified Oripavine as a dangerous drug as defined in RA
9165;

       WHEREAS, during the public hearing held by the DDB Committee on Public
Hearings on May 6, 2008, after due notice, no opposition was raised to the proposed
inclusion of Oripavine in the list of dangerous drugs;

       WHEREFORE, be it RESOLVED, as it is hereby RESOLVED, to include
ORIPAVINE in the list of dangerous drugs and any material, compound, mixture or
preparation which contains any quantity of ORIPAVINE, including its salts, isomers, and
salts of isomers within the specific chemical designation, by whatever official, common,
unusual or brand name or designation, is classified as a dangerous drug which shall be
subject to all regulatory and control measures as provided under RA 9165 and the rules
and regulations of the Board, provided that this Regulation shall take effect fifteen (15)
days after its publication in two (2) newspapers of general circulation and after its
registration with the Office of the National Administrative Register (ONAR), UP Law
Center, Diliman, Quezon City.

      APPROVED and ADOPTED, this 14th day of May, in the year of Our Lord,
2008 in Quezon City.




Secretary ANSELMO S. AVENIDO, JR.

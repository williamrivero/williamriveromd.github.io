                               BOARD REGULATION No. 7
                                              Series of 2007

      SUBJECT:            AMENDING BOARD REGULATION NO. 3, SERIES OF
                          2003, ENTITLED “COMPREHENSIVE GUIDELINES ON
                          IMPORTATION,    DISTRIBUTION, MANUFACTURE,
                          PRESCRIPTION, DISPENSING AND SALE OF, AND
                          OTHER LAWFUL ACTS IN CONNECTION WITH, ANY
                          DANGEROUS DRUGS, CONTROLLED PRECURSORS
                          AND ESSENTIAL CHEMICALS AND OTHER SIMILAR OR
                          ANALOGOUS SUBSTANCES”

       WHEREAS, the Dangerous Drugs Board promulgated Board Regulation
No. 3, Series of 2003 providing for the           Comprehensive Guidelines on
importation, distribution, manufacture, prescription, dispensing and sale of, and
other lawful acts in connection with, any dangerous drugs, controlled precursors
and essential chemicals and other similar or analogous substances;

       WHEREAS, there is a need to amend the aforesaid guidelines to clarify
the regulatory fees for the import, export, transit or redirection of dangerous
drugs, controlled precursors and essential chemicals, or their preparations;

      WHEREFORE, be it RESOLVED, as it is hereby RESOLVED, to amend
Board Regulation No. 3, Series of 2003, as hereunder provided:


      Section 1. Sub-paragraph c(ii)(g) of Paragraph 8 of Section 6, Article III
of Board Regulation No, 3, Series of 2003, which reads as follows:

       “g. Controlled chemicals - P 0.01 per 1000 grams or a fraction thereof.”
is hereby deleted.


       Section 2. Section 10, Article III of Board Regulation No. 3, Series of
2003, is hereby amended by adding a paragraph which reads as follows:

       “3. A regulatory fee shall be paid for every application for permit for
import, export, transit or redirection of dangerous drugs, controlled precursors
and essential chemicals or their preparations, as follows:

      a. Five Hundred (P 500.00) Pesos when the quantity of the dangerous
         drugs, controlled precursors and essential chemicals, or their
         preparations is 1,000 kilograms or liters or less, as the case may be;

      b. One Thousand (P 1,000.00) Pesos when the quantity of the dangerous
         drugs, controlled precursors and essential chemicals, or their
         preparations is more than 1,000 kilograms or liters but less than 5,000
         kilograms or liters, as the case may be; and

      c. One Thousand Five Hundred (P 1,500.00) Pesos when the quantity of

       Section 3. This Regulation shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its
publication in two (2) newspapers of general circulation and after its registration

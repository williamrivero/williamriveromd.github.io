OFFICE OF THE PRESIDENT a
DANGEROUS DRUGS BOARD
BOARD REGULATION NO. 2
SERIES OF 2024
SUBJECT: EXTENSION OF THE PROVISIONAL REMOVAL OF LIANHUA QINGWEN
FROM THE LIST OF DANGEROUS DRUGS FOR ANOTHER PERIOD OF
ONE (1) YEAR

WHEREAS, the Dangerous Drugs Board (the "Board") under Section 81 of
Republic Act No. 9165 (RA 9165) or the Comprehensive Dangerous Drugs Act of 2002
is mandated to "formulate guidelines, in coordination with other government agencies,
relative to transactions involving dangerous drugs and controlled precursors and essential
chemicals, which may include the importation, distribution, sale, prescription, and
dispensing thereof";

WHEREAS, pursuant to the above mandate, the Board, in Resolution No. 1, Series
of 2020, interposed no objection to the product registration of Lianhua Qingwen capsules
with the Food and Drug Administration (FDA) of the Department of Health, provided that
should the FDA issue a Certificate of Product Registration, the importer thereof shall
secure the proper license from the Philippine Drug Enforcement Agency and comply with
all the regulatory requirements set forth in Board Regulation No. 1, Series of 2014;

WHEREAS, on 4 February 2021, the Board promulgated Regulation No. 1, Series
of 2021 approving the Provisional Removal of Lianhua Qingwen from the List of
Dangerous Drugs for a Period of One (1) Year pursuant to Section 93 of RA 9165 which
further provides that prior to the expiration of the one-year period, the Committee on
Reclassification shall evaluate and recommend to the Board whether to shorten or extend
the said period given the prevailing circumstances at such time;

WHEREAS, on 24 January 2022, the Board adopted Regulation No. 1, Series of
2022 extending the grant of the provisional removal of Lianhua Qingwen from the list of
Dangerous Drugs for another one (1) year;

WHEREAS, on 09 February 2023, the Board once again extended the grant of the
provisional removal of Lianhua Qingwen from the list of Dangerous Drugs for another one
(1) year through Regulation No. 1, Series of 2023;

WHEREAS, on 19 January 2024, the Philippine Archipelago International Trading
Corporation submitted a formal letter to the Board, requesting for an extension of the
provisional removal of Lianhua Qingwen from the List of Dangerous Drugs based on the
recognized efficacy of Lianhua Qingwen in managing symptoms associated with the
surge of COVID-19;

WHEREAS, Lianhua Qingwen capsules is duly registered with the FDA under
registration number THPR-50, valid until 7 August 2025, categorized under the
Pharmacologic classification of Traditionally-Used Herbal Products with the designated
use to assist in the alleviation of heat-toxin invasion symptoms affecting the lungs,
including but not limited to fever, aversion to cold, muscle soreness, and nasal
congestion;

WHEREAS, the Technical Working Group on Reclassification, Addition, or
Removal (TWG-RAR) of Drugs/Substances from the List of Dangerous Drugs/Controlled
Precursors and Essential Chemicals convened on 28 February 2024to discuss the
request;

WHEREAS, members of the TWG-RAR noted that since the introduction of
Lianhua Qingwen capsules in the Philippine market, there have been (a) no documented
cases of adverse reactions, instances of overdose, or drug abuse associated with
Lianhua Qingwen; and (b) no reported seizures nor incidents of diversion related to the
ephedra and ephedrine content in the said product;

WHEREAS, the TWG-RAR recommended to grant the request for the extension
of the provisional removal of Lianhua Qingwen from the list of dangerous drugs that was
presented to the Board for its approval;

WHEREFORE, be it RESOLVED, as it is hereby RESOLVED, to further extend
the provisional removal of Lianhua Qingwen from the List of Dangerous Drugs for another
period of one (1) year;

APPROVED and ADOPTED this 8th day of March, in the year of Our Lord, 2024,
in Quezon City, Philippines.

Secretary LINO S. CUY, CEO VI

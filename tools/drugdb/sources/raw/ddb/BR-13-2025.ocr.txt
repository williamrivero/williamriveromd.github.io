DANGEROUS DRUGS BOARD
BOARD REGULATION NO. 13
Series of 2025
SUBJECT: INCLUSION OF MDMB-4en-PINACA IN SCHEDULE II OF THE 1971 UN
CONVENTION ON PSYCHOTROPIC SUBSTANCES

WHEREAS, the Philippines is a signatory to the 1971 United Nations Convention
on Psychotropic Substances (“1971 UN Convention”) which seeks to promote
cooperation among states in order to address more effectively the various aspects of illicit
traffic of psychotropic substances;

WHEREAS, the Commission on Narcotic Drugs decided by 47 votes to none, with
no abstentions, to include Decision 64/3 of MDMB-4en-PINACA in Schedule II of the
Convention on Psychotropic Substances during its 6th meeting on 14 April 2021.

WHEREAS, MDMB-4en-PINACA (IUPAC Name: methyl 3,3-dimethyl-2-(1-
(pent-4-en-1-yl)-1H-indazole-3-carboxamido)butanoate) (CAS. No.2504100-70-1) is
a potent synthetic cannabinoid receptor agonist with a similar mechanism of action, and
similar effects to a number of other synthetic cannabinoids that are controlled under
Schedule II of the Convention on Psychotropic Substances of 1971.

WHEREAS, use of MDMB-4en-PINACA has been associated with severe adverse
effects, including fatal intoxications and cases of impaired driving. It has been identified
in seized material formulated for smoking, and found as white to yellow/brown powder.
This substance is clandestinely manufactured, poses a risk to public health and has no
recognized therapeutic use.

WHEREAS, the most likely routes of administration for MDMB-4en-PINACA in
humans are inhalation via smoking the chemical after it has been sprayed onto plant
material or vaping it after formulation in liquid.

WHEREAS, the 1971 UN Convention requires Parties thereof to implement control
measures on substances which have been classified under its Schedules;

WHEREAS, Board Regulation No. 1, Series of 2020, prescribed the immediate
adoption of changes in the scope of control of substances pursuant to the three (3) United
Nations International Drug Conventions and authorized the DDB Chairman to issue a
Regulation for such purpose, provided that the DDB Chairman and/or the DDB Executive
Director shall inform the Board of the issuance of such Regulation.

NOW THEREFORE, be it RESOLVED, as it is hereby RESOLVED, to adopt the
action of the UN-CND placing MDMB-4en-PINACA (IUPAC Name: methyl 3,3-
dimethyl-2-(1-(pent-4-en-1-yl)-1H-indazole-3-carboxamido)butanoate) and all
compounds, mixtures, and preparations containing this substance, including its salts,

                               BOARD REGULATION NO. 9
                                    Series of 2003


        SUBJECT:         ADOPTING AND ENFORCING ALL PREVIOUSLY ISSUED
                         DANGEROUS     DRUGS   BOARD     RESOLUTIONS/
                         REGULATIONS AND OTHER ISSUANCES THAT ARE
                         NOT INCONSISTENT WITH THE PROVISIONS OF RA
                         9165 OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE COMPREHENSIVE
                         DANGEROUS DRUGS ACT OF 2002


        WHEREAS, Republic Act No. 9165, otherwise known as the
Comprehensive Dangerous Drugs Act of 2002, has been enacted and became
effective July 4, 2002, after its publication in at least two (2) newspapers of
general circulation, thereby repealing Republic Act No. 6425, otherwise known as
the Dangerous Drugs Act of 1972;

       WHEREAS, under the new Act, the Dangerous Drugs Board that was
reconstituted by virtue thereof, was vested with the authority to issue and adopt
various regulations and resolutions that are consistent with the performance of its
mandated functions;

       WHEREAS, under the repealing clause of RA 9165, RA 6425 as amended
as well as all other laws, administrative orders, rules and regulations or parts
thereof which are found to be inconsistent with the provisions of the new Act,
have been repealed or modified accordingly;

      WHEREFORE, be it RESOLVED as it is hereby RESOLVED that all the
Board Regulations and other issuances of the old Dangerous Drugs Board that
are not inconsistent with the provisions of RA 9165 are hereby adopted as
enforceable and still in effect.

     ADOPTED and APPROVED this 24th day of October in the year of our
LORD, 2003 in Quezon City.

         SUBJECT:             Dissemination of the Electronic Version of the 14" Edition of the
                              2026 Interim Drug Price Reference Index (DPRI)



In line with the principles of transparency, efficiency, and value for money, the Department of
Health (DOH) hereby issues this Circular to announce the publication and implementation
guidelines of the 2026 Interim Drug Price Reference Index (DPRI), 14th Edition Electronic
Copy.

The latest edition of the DPRI may be accessed through the official DPRI website at:
(https://dpri.doh.gov.ph/index).

All end-users and procuring entities shall conduct market scoping activities pursuant to
Republic Act (RA) 12009, otherwise known as the New Government Procurement Act (NGPA),
as part of procurement planning and in determining the Approved Budget for the Contract
(ABC).    This requirement          shall apply to all procurement               methods      and arrangements,          including
competitive bidding, consignment agreements, framework agreements, and other analogous
procurement mechanisms, to validate prevailing market prices and ensure the reasonableness of
the ABC.

Market scoping activities shall include, whenever applicable:

                   a. The collection and validation of pricing information from manufacturers,
                   distributors, suppliers, government procurement data,and other reliable market
                   sources.
                   b. The use of the DPRI as one of the references pursuant to Rule II (Strategic
                   Procurement Planning), Section 10.2 (Market Scoping), paragraph (f) of the
                   NGPA, which allows the use of relevant data from Philippine Government
                   Electronic Procurement System (PHILGEPS) or agency websites as a guide or
                   reference in determining market prices.

  DOH Centers for Health Development (CHDs), specialty hospitals, and retained hospitals.
  End-users and procuring entities may adopt a unit cost up to the highest price indicated in the
  DPRI range, plus an additional 6.8%, representing the combined 3.6% average inflation rate
  for 2026 and 3.2% projected average inflation rate for 2027, based on data from the Bangko
  Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP). |

  Prices for drugs and medicines covered under the Maximum Wholesale Price (MWP), as
  stipulated under Executive Orders (EO) Nos. 104 and 155, are reflected accordingly.
  For guidance on the implementation of the DPRI Framework, reference may be made to
  Department Circular No. 2026-0303 dated 10 June 2026, entitled “Dissemination of the Interim
  Guidelines for the Implementation of the Drug Price Reference Index (DPRI) Framework,” and
  any subsequent issuances thereto.

  In the event of a failed bidding, any adjustment to the unit cost shall be made in accordance
  with the applicable provisions of the Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR) of the NGPA.
  For further information or clarification, please contact Mr. Achilles V. Aragona or Mr. John
  Lester Custodio of the Pharmaceutical Division through telephone number (02) 8651-7800 local
  8101 or via email at dpri@doh.gov.ph.

  This Circular shall take effect immediately.

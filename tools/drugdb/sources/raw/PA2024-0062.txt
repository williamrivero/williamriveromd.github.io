 Fixed Fee Schedule of Additional
Medicines for the PhilHealth GAMOT
PhilHealth Circular (PC) No. 2023-0029 provides for the implementation of the
Outpatient Drug Benefit Package, otherwise known as PhilHealth Guaranteed
Accessible Medications for Outpatient Treatment or PhilHealth GAMOT. Fixed fee
schedule or standard rates per specific drug preparation were set for the 154 covered
medications as reflected in Annex G of the PC.

Following the issuance of PC No. 2024-0024 entitled “Supplemental Guidelines for
the Health Care Provider Network (HCPN) Demonstration Program,” additional 13
drug preparations were included in the list for PhilHealth GAMOT.

This Advisory reflects the fixed fee schedule of the following:

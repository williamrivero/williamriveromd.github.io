       I.     RATIONALE


              The Universal Health Care Act (Republic Act No. 11223) mandates equitable
              access for all Filipinos to quality and cost-effective promotive, preventive,
              curative, rehabilitative, and palliative services delivered through a whole-of-
              system approach. It requires continuous quality improvement in health services
              and system performance, ensuring that care at all levels—includi ng primary
              care—is safe, evidence-based, and appropriate. Section 37 of the National
              Health Insurance Act of 2013 (Republic Act No. 7875, as amended) further
              mandates PhilHealth to implement a comprehensive Quality Assurance
              Program to ensure that services provided to members, including prescriptions,
              are necessary, appropriate, and consistent with accepted medical standards and
              ethics.


              PhilHealth expands its primary care through the Yaman ng Kalusugan Program
              (YAKAP), which integrates primary care services, the PhilHealth Guaranteed
              and Accessible Medications for Outpatient Treatment (PhilHealth GAMOT)
              benefit, and select cancer screening tests. Strengthening YAKAP through clear
              clinical standards supports effective implementation by promoting consistent,
              high-quality primary care, improved disease management, and timely referral
              across the health system.
       II.    OBJECTIVES


              This PhilHealth Circular shall establish clinical and pharmaceuticalstandards
              to ensure that primary and outpatient care services under YAKAP are of good
              quality and lead to better health outcomes.

        III.   SCOPE


               This PhilHealth Circular provides the clinical and pharmaceutical standards
               governing the delivery of primary and outpatient care services under the
               YAKAP. It outlines the required standards of care, prescribing and dispensing
               practices, and quality assurance mechanisms to ensure safe, effective, and
               coordinated primary care across the continuum of services. This PhilHealth
               Circular covers all stakeholders involved in the delivery of primary care
               services.



        IV.    DEFINITION OF TERMS


               For the purpose of this PhilHealth Circular, the following terms are defined:

               A.   Antimicrobial Stewardship - a multidisciplinary, multi-intervention,
                    and coordinated approach to improve the appropriate use of
                    antimicrobials by promoting the selection of the optimal antimicrobial
                    regimen to ensure the right drug, right administration, right time, and
                    right duration to minimize harm to the patients.

               B.   Authorized Physician - accredited physician with               a   PhilHealth
                    GAMOT Application account.

               C.   Empanelment - the establishment of a formal care relationship
                    between beneficiaries and their chosen YAKAP Clinics after completion of
                    the First Patient Encounter (FPE) and signing of the Mutual Care
                    Agreement (MCA) or YAKAP Empanelment Slip (YES), thereby creating
                    mutual accountability and ongoing clinical relationships. Once
                    empaneled, the health care team assumes responsibility for coordinating
                    comprehensive services for their assigned patients, ensuring continuity of
                    care with support from the broader care team.

               D.   First Patient Encounter (FPE) - the initial episode of patient contact
                    for the year, whereby a primary care clinic takes and/or updates the basic
                    health data of an eligible beneficiary to identify their health risks. The FPE
                    is not yet a consultation.

        V.   POLICY STATEMENTS


             A. Core Functions of Primary Care Providers

                  YAKAP providers shall perform the four core functions of primary care.
                  They shall serve as the first point of contact and provide comprehensive,
                  accessible, and continuous care to PhilHealth beneficiaries.

                  1. YAKAP Clinics shall deliver, at the minimum, the following services:

                     a. First Patient Encounter (FPE);
                     b. Empanelment;
                     c. Initial assessment, collection of basic health data, and risk profiling;
                     d. Primary care consultations, laboratory tests and medicines;
                     e. Referral to higher level facilities and other primary care services such
                        as tuberculosis treatment, maternity care, oral health, cancer
                        screening among others; and
                     f. Creation and maintenance of patient/client health records.

                  2. As first-contact providers, YAKAP Clinics shall serve as the initial and
                     continuous access point for clients seeking primary care services. They
                     shall also function as patient navigators, ensuring that PhilHealth
                     beneficiaries are referred to appropriate services based on their
                     healthcare needs.


                  3. The YAKAP Clinics shall provide comprehensive primary care including
                     promotive, preventive, and curative services that address the health
                     needs of their catchment population. They must be capable of managing
                     the most common health conditions across all life stages.

                  4. As providers of coordinated care, YAKAP Clinics shall ensure that
                     PhilHealth beneficiaries receive integrated, person-centered care across
                     different settings and levels of the health system,

                      a. YAKAP Clinics shall establish and maintain a care coordination,
                          patient navigation, and referral system with GAMOT providers,
                          cancer screening providers, and other health care providers within
                          their catchment area to facilitate timely access to appropriate
                          services and continuity of care,

                      b. Patient navigation and referrals for cancer screening tests shall be

                     facilities (such as maternity care clinics, TB-DOTS centers among
                      others) shall refer back their clients to YAKAP Clinics. These shall
                      be documented through a feedback report or its equivalent and shall
                      be integrated into the patients’ primary care records.

           B. YAKAP Clinics shall maintain and update clients’ records containing
              complete history, diagnoses, medications, laboratory and imaging results,
              medications, referrals, and follow-up plans. All members of the health care
              team shall document each client encounter accurately and promptly.
              Clinical diagnoses shall be properly translated into corresponding ICD-io
              code/s. In the future, YAKAP Clinics shall transition to a standardized
              electronic medical records (EMR) to streamline documentation, and ensure
              effective coordination and continuity of care.

           C. YAKAP physicians shall adhere to the current clinical practice guidelines,
              protocols, and standards of care including but not limited to the Omnibus
              Health Guidelines, National Antibiotic Guidelines, Philippine Package of
              Essential Non-communicable Disease Interventions, and PhilHealth-issued
              quality policies. When necessary, PhilHealth shall issue guidelines for
              specific conditions to ensure uniform implementation of this PhilHealth
              Circular.

           D. Medicines under PhilHealth GAMOT facilities shall be dispensed only by
              duly registered and licensed pharmacists, and upon presentation of a valid
              prescription issued by YAKAP physicians.

           E. Prescribing and Dispensing Standards and Rational Drug Use
              YAKAP providers shall uphold to the principles of rational drug use and
              antimicrobial stewardship.

              1. Primary care physicians shall prescribe medications in accordance with
                 all applicable laws and regulations, including the Generics Act of 1988
                 (Republic Act No. 6675) and the Comprehensive Dangerous Drugs Act
                 of 2002 (Republic Act No. 9165). They shall align the prescriptions on

coo              therapeutic indications (Annex A: Table of GAMOT Drugs and
<0               Indications).

              2. Primary care physicians shall ensure that patients receive medications
       8         only when clinically indicated, in appropriate doses, for an adequate

                    duration, at the lowest possible cost, and administered correctly by the
                    appropriate person.

                 3. In support of antimicrobial stewardship through rational antimicrobial
                    use and adherence to evidence-based clinical guidelines, prescribing
                    limits for oral antimicrobials shall NOT exceed fourteen (14) days per
                    prescription. Cases requiring extended therapy shall only be undertaken
                    following proper clinical assessment and evaluation by authorized
                    primary care physicians. The use and duration of other antimicrobial
                    agents shall be in accordance with applicable clinical guidelines.

                 4. Medications prescribed and claimed under PhilHealth GAMOT must be
                    intended solely for the patient’s active or current medical condition.
                    Prescriptions issued upon request or designated as standby medications
                    for non-active conditions, and/or those not related to the patient’s
                    current diagnosis, shall not be eligible for claims under the GAMOT
                    Program.

                 5. Primary care physicians shall prescribe therapeutically sound and cost-
                    effective medicines consistent with clinical guidelines and the Philippine
                    National Formulary.

                 6. Primary care physicians shall evaluate potential drug-drug interactions
                    when prescribing multiple medications, especially in cases of
                    polypharmacy, to ensure patient safety, prevent adverse drug events,
                    and avoid inappropriate polypharmacy.

                 7. GAMOT facilities and its pharmacists, as essential providers of
                    pharmaceutical care, shall rationally dispense drugs ensuring the safe,
                    effective, and appropriate provision of medicines and health services
                    (including patient counseling, if necessary) tailored to the patients’
                    needs.

Meet the Interaction Checker, in Dosis. Add a patient's medicines, and it shows how they affect each other, and the kidneys.
Type a brand or a generic name, tap a favorite, or paste the whole medication list at once.
Then set the kidney function, from normal down to dialysis. If you used a Calculo calculator first, the eGFR is already filled in.
Every pair is graded: contraindicated, avoid, adjust or monitor, or minor. Each one says what happens, and what to do.
For example, clarithromycin raises atorvastatin levels. The monograph says not to exceed twenty milligrams of atorvastatin, and to watch for muscle problems.
Kidney checks look further. Ibuprofen with losartan and furosemide is flagged as a triple whammy, and each drug's kidney dosing is shown for the level you chose.
No result does not mean no interaction, so check the full prescribing information. Copy the link to share the list, print it, or send the medicines to the Med Scheduler.
You will find it in Dosis, the Drug Index. Your favorites and recent medicines stay on your own device.

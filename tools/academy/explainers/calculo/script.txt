Meet Calculo, the clinical calculators of Renal Care Matters. More than two hundred and fifty calculators across every specialty, in one place, and free.
Search by name, or browse twenty-three categories, from kidney function and drug dosing to cardiology, critical care, pediatrics and neurology.
Each calculator is one clean card. Enter the values, choose your units, and the result appears at once.
Not sure what to enter? Tap Sample data to try realistic values, then Clear sample to put your own back.
Enter the patient's age, weight and creatinine once, and related calculators fill them in for you, saved only on your device.
Every result shows its formula, sources and limitations, so you can check the math and the evidence behind it.
Open the Calculo tab on the left to install the app, and download every calculator once. Then they all work with no signal, on the ward, in the basement, or in the dialysis unit.
Calculators support your clinical judgment; they never replace it. Find Calculo at Renal Care Matters dot com.

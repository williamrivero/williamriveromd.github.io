Meet the Drug Index, and Dosis, its app. It is the free drug reference of Renal Care Matters, built for clinicians in the Philippines.
It lists every drug product registered with the FDA Philippines. That is more than thirty-seven thousand products, under about twenty-three hundred generic names. Over six thousand food supplement and medical nutrition brands are in too.
Search by brand, generic, drug class, indication, manufacturer, or registration number. Or browse by class, therapeutic area, or kidney dosing.
Each monograph carries the official U.S. or European prescribing information, word for word, with the Philippine brands and registrations beside it.
Reviewed kidney dosing is color coded by kidney function, down to dialysis: no change, adjust, avoid, or not studied. Medicines that can injure the kidney are flagged too.
Two tools work from the same data. The Interaction Checker grades every pair of medicines, and the Med Scheduler builds one daily timetable around meals and dialysis.
Tap the teal Dosis tab on the left, install the app, and download the complete index once. Then every page works with no signal, even deep in the hospital. The labels are refreshed every Monday.
Dosis supports your clinical judgment, it does not replace it, so always check the full prescribing information. Find Dosis at Renal Care Matters dot com.

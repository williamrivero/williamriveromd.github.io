# Module 11 · Answer Key and Rationales

| Item | Answer | Rationale | Review and source |
|---:|:---:|---|---|
| 1 | B | Product labeling and instructions determine whether the device is intended for single use. A single-use dialyzer is discarded under the facility process after use. | Objective 1. [Manufacturer example · Dialyzer instructions for use](https://freseniusmedicalcare.com/en-us/support/product-support-documents/instructions-for-use/) |
| 2 | D | An authorized dialyzer reuse program is patient-specific. A similar prescription or shared station does not establish patient identity. | Objective 1. [U.S. reference · 42 CFR §494.50, dialyzer reuse](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/chapter-IV/subchapter-G/part-494/subpart-B/section-494.50) |
| 3 | A | A multiple-use designation is one prerequisite. Local policy, applicable requirements, and a validated program govern whether reuse occurs. | Objective 1. [U.S. reference · 42 CFR §494.50, dialyzer reuse](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/chapter-IV/subchapter-G/part-494/subpart-B/section-494.50) |
| 4 | C | The result addresses a specific chemical safety check. Identity, integrity and all other required criteria need separate verification. | Objective 1. [CDC · Dialysis infection-control assessment tool, reuse section](https://www.cdc.gov/infection-control/media/pdfs/icar-dialysis-508.pdf) |
| 5 | B | A stock shortage does not change product labeling. The supervisor can arrange an authorized replacement or alternate plan. | Objective 1. [Manufacturer example · Dialyzer instructions for use](https://freseniusmedicalcare.com/en-us/support/product-support-documents/instructions-for-use/) |
| 6 | D | Traceability connects the specific device and patient with processing history and outcome. The unit defines the required identifiers and checks. | Objective 2. [CDC · Dialysis infection-control assessment tool, reuse section](https://www.cdc.gov/infection-control/media/pdfs/icar-dialysis-508.pdf) |
| 7 | A | A device test and a verified patient match address different risks. Acceptable test results cannot resolve an unreadable identifier. | Objective 2. [CDC · Dialysis infection-control assessment tool, reuse section](https://www.cdc.gov/infection-control/media/pdfs/icar-dialysis-508.pdf) |
| 8 | C | A hold prevents use while the concern is reviewed. The applicable policy defines further evaluation, rejection or authorized disposition. | Objective 3. [CDC · Dialysis infection-control assessment tool, reuse section](https://www.cdc.gov/infection-control/media/pdfs/icar-dialysis-508.pdf) |
| 9 | B | Prevent use and preserve the record so the authorized team can investigate. A technician should never infer or recreate an uncertain identity. | Objective 3. [U.S. reference · 42 CFR §494.50, dialyzer reuse](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/chapter-IV/subchapter-G/part-494/subpart-B/section-494.50) |
| 10 | D | The note identifies the item, problem, hold status, notification and pending disposition. Record patient identifiers only through the approved record process. | Objective 2. [CDC · Dialysis infection-control assessment tool, reuse section](https://www.cdc.gov/infection-control/media/pdfs/icar-dialysis-508.pdf) |
| 11 | A | Operation and maintenance must follow the applicable model instructions and authorized facility procedures. | Objective 4. [U.S. reference · 42 CFR §494.60, equipment maintenance](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/chapter-IV/subchapter-G/part-494/subpart-B/section-494.60) |
| 12 | C | Preventive maintenance and pre-use verification address different tasks and timing. Each needs its own documentation under the facility program. | Objective 4. [U.S. reference · 42 CFR §494.60, equipment maintenance](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/chapter-IV/subchapter-G/part-494/subpart-B/section-494.60) |
| 13 | B | Prevent patient exposure to equipment with an unresolved failure. Capture the asset and fault details so qualified staff can investigate. | Objective 4. [Philippine DOH · Dialysis facility standards](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/10/50687) |
| 14 | D | The return-to-service process verifies completion of required technical and clinical readiness steps. A tentative verbal update does not establish release. | Objective 4. [U.S. reference · 42 CFR §494.60, equipment maintenance](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-42/chapter-IV/subchapter-G/part-494/subpart-B/section-494.60) |
| 15 | A | Communicate the equipment identity, observed problem, action taken and outstanding responsibility. This keeps the next team from releasing equipment prematurely. | Objective 4. [Philippine DOH · Dialysis facility standards](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/10/50687) |

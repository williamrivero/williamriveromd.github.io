# Module 11 · Self-Assessment

Choose one best answer per question. Score one point per correct response. Suggested mastery is 12 of 15. Review missed safety decisions about device labeling, patient-to-dialyzer verification or safe return to service even if you reach the suggested score.

1. **What does the label ‘single use only’ mean for the dialyzer?**  
   A. Use it on the same patient for an unlimited number of sessions.  
   B. It is intended for one use. Follow facility disposal procedure afterward.  
   C. Reprocess it if no blood is visible.  
   D. It can be reused when the inventory is low.  

2. **When reuse is authorized, for whom may the previously used dialyzer be prepared?**

   B. Any patient with the same prescribed dialyzer size  
   C. Any patient who has agreed to reuse  
   D. Only the same patient, with identity verified under the program  

3. **The label permits multiple use, but the facility runs a single-use program. What does the model’s label establish?**

   C. The unit can be cleaned for reuse without a validated process.  
   D. The facility must adopt reuse for that model.  

4. **What does an acceptable documented residual disinfectant check indicate?**  
   A. That the dialyzer’s patient identity has been verified  
   B. That all prior reprocessing records can be discarded  
   C. That the residual disinfectant requirement was met for this recorded check  
   D. That the membrane integrity result is unnecessary  

5. **Stock is low and a coworker suggests reprocessing a dialyzer already used once even though the label specifies single use. What should you do?**  
   A. Reuse it after a visual inspection.  
   B. Keep it out of reuse and notify the supervisor of the supply issue.  
   C. Reuse it if the patient is the same.  
   D. Cover the label so it can enter the reprocessing room.  

6. **What makes a reprocessed dialyzer traceable?**  
   A. A visible color tag by itself  
   B. The machine’s station number  
   C. The reprocessing date without an item number  
   D. A legible link among the dialyzer identity, verified patient and relevant record of processing and disposition  

7. **A dialyzer has a recorded acceptable integrity result, but its patient identifier is unreadable. Which requirement remains unmet?**  
   A. Patient-to-device match  
   B. Choice of cleaning solution color  
   C. Pressure-monitor tubing check  
   D. Machine calibration date  

8. **An authorized reviewer has put a dialyzer on hold because its record is incomplete. What does hold mean?**  
   A. It may be used if a replacement is unavailable.  
   B. It is permanently discarded by whoever first sees it.  
   C. It is segregated from ready stock pending the authorized disposition decision.  
   D. It is ready after a verbal assurance from any coworker.  

9. **At preparation, a reprocessed dialyzer label and the chart disagree. Recorded technical checks are acceptable. What should you do?**  
   A. Use it if the treatment model matches.  
   B. Set it aside in the designated hold area, notify the authorized staff and document the unresolved mismatch.  
   C. Select the most likely patient from the shift list.  
   D. Relabel it using a recollection of the last treatment.  

10. **Which entry best documents the mismatch for follow-up in the authorized log?**

   B. Staff notified. Location uncertain.  
   C. Reassigned the dialyzer based on the chair schedule.  
   D. 07:20 · Dialyzer asset D-31 held · Patient identifier unreadable · Verified match unavailable · Charge nurse informed and disposition pending.  

11. **Which document identifies the approved way to operate a specific model of dialysis machine?**  
   A. The current manufacturer instructions for that model, used with the facility procedure  
   B. A shift roster  
   C. The previous patient’s record

13. **A machine shows the same safety fault twice during required checks. What should you do within your role?**

   B. Prevent clinical use, label or segregate it per policy, and notify the responsible nurse and technical team.  
   C. Repair the fault using settings for another machine.  
   D. Use the machine until the technical team arrives.  

14. **You receive verbal word that a held machine is probably repaired. What should you verify before making it available?**  
   A. That the next patient is willing to use it  
   B. That a different machine has passed its checks  
   C. That the fault tag looks old

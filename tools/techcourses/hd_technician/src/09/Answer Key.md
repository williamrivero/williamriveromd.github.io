# Module 09 · Answer Key and Rationales

| Item | Answer | Rationale | Review and source |
|---:|:---:|---|---|
| 1 | A | Use two person-specific identifiers and match them to the order and care record. The station and schedule identify a location and time. | Objective 1. [Joint Commission · Two patient identifiers](https://www.jointcommission.org/en-us/knowledge-library/support-center/standards-interpretation/standards-faqs/000001463) |
| 2 | C | The current order is the source of the prescribed treatment. Verify any discrepancy through the authorized clinician. | Objective 1. [Philippine DOH · Dialysis facility standards](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/10/50687) |
| 3 | B | Confirm scale, units, patient identity and measurement conditions as assigned. The verified current weight is a data point for clinical review, not a new prescription. | Objective 1. [UK Kidney Association · Fluid assessment and management](https://guidelines.ukkidney.org/haemodialysis/rationale-for-clinical-practice-guidelines/fluid-in-haemodialysis/) |
| 4 | D | The nurse or authorized clinician reviews the unexpected result and confirms the treatment plan. A technician reports the measurement and avoids independent changes. | Objective 1. [UK Kidney Association · Fluid assessment and management](https://guidelines.ukkidney.org/haemodialysis/rationale-for-clinical-practice-guidelines/fluid-in-haemodialysis/) |
| 5 | A | Medication preparation, administration and changes depend on a verified order, legal scope and facility authorization. | Objective 1. [Philippine DOH · Dialysis facility standards](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/10/50687) |
| 6 | C | Intradialytic observations are made during treatment, such as assigned vital signs, symptoms and equipment or access observations. | Objective 2. [NIDDK · Hemodialysis](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/kidney-disease/kidney-failure/hemodialysis) |
| 7 | B | Timing gives the value clinical context and supports comparison across the treatment record. | Objective 2. [Philippine DOH · Dialysis facility standards](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/10/50687) |
| 8 | D | Machine alarms respond to monitored device conditions. They cannot replace patient observation or timely reporting of symptoms. | Objective 2. [UK Kidney Association · Fluid assessment and management](https://guidelines.ukkidney.org/haemodialysis/rationale-for-clinical-practice-guidelines/fluid-in-haemodialysis/) |
| 9 | A | Symptoms with a substantial blood pressure change warrant prompt nursing assessment. Relay facts and follow authorized instructions. | Objective 2. [UK Kidney Association · Fluid assessment and management](https://guidelines.ukkidney.org/haemodialysis/rationale-for-clinical-practice-guidelines/fluid-in-haemodialysis/) |
| 10 | C | A mismatched identifier makes the specimen identity uncertain. Reconcile it through policy before sending or relying on it. | Objective 2. [WHO · Guidelines on drawing blood](https://www.who.int/publications-detail-redirect/9789241599221) |
| 11 | B | The pre- and post-treatment weights are measurements. The target is a clinical goal and should remain identifiable as such. | Objective 3. [UK Kidney Association · Fluid assessment and management](https://guidelines.ukkidney.org/haemodialysis/rationale-for-clinical-practice-guidelines/fluid-in-haemodialysis/) |
| 12 | D | The difference between planned and delivered treatment is relevant to clinical review, continuity and documentation. | Objective 4. [Philippine DOH · Dialysis facility standards](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/10/50687) |
| 13 | A | The station is released only after the approved cleaning and disinfection requirements are completed. | Objective 3. [CDC · Dialysis station routine disinfection](https://www.cdc.gov/dialysis-safety/media/pdfs/env-checklist-508.pdf) |
| 14 | C | A new symptom requires nursing assessment. Patient safety takes priority and routine turnover waits until the station is vacated safely. | Objective 3. [CDC · Dialysis station routine disinfection](https://www.cdc.gov/dialysis-safety/media/pdfs/env-checklist-508.pdf) |
| 15 | B | State what happened, when, who responded and what remains outstanding. The receiving clinician can confirm the pending action. | Objective 4. [AHRQ · SBAR](https://www.ahrq.gov/teamstepps-program/curriculum/communication/tools/sbar.html) |

# Module 09 · Self-Assessment

Choose one best answer per question. Score one point per correct response. Suggested mastery is 12 of 15. Review missed safety decisions about patient identity, treatment discrepancies, symptom escalation or turnover even if you reach the suggested score.

1. **Which is an appropriate pair for verifying identity before dialysis when approved by the facility?**  
   A. Full name and date of birth  
   B. Assigned chair and arrival time  
   C. Treatment shift and machine number  
   D. Surname and access arm  

2. **Which source defines the treatment parameters to be verified before the session?**

   B. The previous patient’s settings  
   C. The current authorized patient-specific order  
   D. An estimate based on the patient’s expected weight

   C. It proves the patient has gained only fluid.

   B. Increase fluid removal to match the weight change.  
   C. Enter the expected weight so setup can continue.

5. **A medication listed on a previous record is absent from today’s order. Someone asks you to prepare it as usual. What should you do?**  
   A. Ask the authorized nurse to verify the order and handle it within facility policy.  
   B. Prepare it and ask the nurse to sign later.  
   C. Use the previous session’s dose if the patient agrees.  
   D. Ask another trainee to prepare it so the session can start.  

6. **What is the term for observations made while the dialysis treatment is running?**  
   A. Pre-dialysis observations  
   B. Post-dialysis observations  
   C. Intradialytic observations  
   D. Turnover checks  

7. **Why record the actual time of an assigned vital sign and the treatment phase?**  
   A. So the time can replace the patient identifier  
   B. So the team can relate the value to symptoms, interventions and treatment progression

8. **The machine has not alarmed. The patient reports dizziness and appears pale. Which interpretation is most appropriate?**  
   A. The patient is stable because no alarm has sounded.  
   B. The next scheduled vital sign is always soon enough.

9. **A patient becomes lightheaded and an assigned blood pressure reading is markedly lower than the initial value. What should you do?**

   B. Independently change the prescription based on the reading.

   D. Record only the initial blood pressure because it was higher.  

10. **You are authorized to assist with specimen checks. A specimen label has the correct name but a different record number. What should you do?**

11. **Which measurements should be distinguished when documenting a session?**  
   A. Arrival time and machine number only  
   B. Measured pre- and post-treatment weights and the prescribed target weight

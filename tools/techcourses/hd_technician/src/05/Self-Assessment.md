# Module 05 · Self-Assessment

Choose one best answer per question. Score one point per correct response. Suggested mastery is 12 of 15. Review missed safety decisions about contact time, sharps or blood exposure even if you reach the suggested score.

1. **To whom do Standard Precautions apply in the dialysis unit?**  
   A. Patients with pending bloodborne virus tests  
   B. Every patient, with precautions matched to the task  
   C. Patients whose charts list a transmissible infection  
   D. Patients with central venous catheters  

2. **What should you do after removing used gloves?**  
   A. Put on fresh gloves immediately  
   B. Clean the gloves before discarding them  
   C. Wait to clean your hands until the next patient arrives  
   D. Perform hand hygiene  

3. **A task may splash blood toward your face. Which PPE addresses that specific risk?**  
   A. Eye protection with a mask, or an appropriate face shield, according to the task and facility policy  
   B. A fluid-resistant gown alone  
   C. Single gloves alone  
   D. A surgical cap and shoe covers  

4. **An observer records 20 hand hygiene opportunities but sees the action performed 15 times. What can this observation support?**  
   A. Proof that the unit has no bloodstream infections  
   B. Proof that the five omissions caused infections  
   C. Targeted feedback on missed opportunities and a follow-up observation  
   D. An automatic diagnosis of infection in five patients  

5. **You handled used tubing with gloves and are now asked to get clean gauze from a common supply cart. What do you do?**  
   A. Use the same gloves if no blood is visible  
   B. Remove the used gloves, perform hand hygiene and retrieve supplies using the clean-supply procedure  
   C. Add a second pair of gloves over the used pair  
   D. Push the cart into the used-equipment area  

6. **A patient is scheduled under the unit’s hepatitis B isolation protocol, but the station assignment includes equipment also used for other patients. What should you do?**  
   A. Proceed if the equipment appears clean  
   B. Move the patient to any available station  
   C. Ask the patient whether shared equipment is acceptable  
   D. Pause the affected setup and alert the charge nurse so the approved precautions can be verified  

7. **Which source sets the required wet contact time for an approved surface disinfectant?**  
   A. The machine’s remaining treatment time  
   B. The pace of station turnover  
   C. The disinfectant’s instructions for use, applied through the facility procedure  
   D. The approximate time it takes the chair to look dry  

8. **A cleaned chair looks dry, but the surface dried before the required disinfectant contact time was reached. How should you interpret its status?**  
   A. Disinfection is incomplete until the required contact time has been achieved under the approved procedure  
   B. It is ready because visible wetness is gone  
   C. It is ready if the patient wears a jacket  
   D. Contact time applies only if the chair was visibly soiled  

9. **The prior patient is still at the station after treatment and requires assistance before leaving. What does this mean for routine station disinfection?**  
   A. Disinfect around the patient to save time  
   B. Release the chair as soon as the blood circuit is disconnected  
   C. Place clean supplies on the used tray while waiting  
   D. Wait until the patient can safely leave, then complete routine station disinfection  

10. **A used blood pressure cuff is about to be moved to a clean station, and the receiving chair is still within the required wet contact time. What should you do?**  
   A. Move the cuff, but leave the chair to dry  
   B. Keep the cuff in the used workflow for proper cleaning and disinfection, and keep the chair on hold until turnover is complete  
   C. Cover the cuff and chair with clean towels and release them  
   D. Use the cuff if the receiving patient arrives before the timer ends  

11. **A used cuff was briefly placed on a clean supply cart before you noticed. What is the best response?**  
   A. Stop use of the affected cart area and supplies, separate the cuff, and notify the responsible staff for assessment and remediation  
   B. Return the cuff to the used area and keep using the cart  
   C. Wipe the cuff and return it to the same shelf  
   D. Move shared supplies to another station before informing anyone  

12. **Where should a used needle be discarded?**  
   A. A bag for infectious waste  
   B. A container for clean packaging  
   C. A designated puncture-resistant sharps container at the point of use  
   D. A tray for disposal at the end of the shift  

13. **The sharps container has reached its marked fill line. What does this indicate?**  
   A. More sharps can be inserted until they are visible at the opening  
   B. It can accept small needles but no longer accepts large needles  
   C. The contents should be pressed down to make space  
   D. It is due for closure and replacement using the facility procedure  

14. **A used sharp punctures your skin during turnover. What should you do promptly?**  
   A. Wash the puncture with soap and water, immediately report it, and seek evaluation through the facility’s exposure pathway  
   B. Finish turnover and report after the shift  
   C. Cover it and seek evaluation only if symptoms develop  
   D. Wait for the source patient’s results before reporting  

15. **While a trainee removes used supplies, blood splashes into their eye. What is the appropriate immediate response?**  
   A. Wipe the closed eyelid and finish the task  
   B. Flush the eye with clean water or saline, then immediately report the exposure and obtain evaluation  
   C. Wait for visual irritation before seeking help  
   D. Clean the eye with the station disinfectant

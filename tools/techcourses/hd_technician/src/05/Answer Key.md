# Module 05 · Answer Key and Rationales

| Item | Answer | Rationale | Review and source |
|---:|:---:|---|---|
| 1 | B | Use Standard Precautions for every patient. Select protective measures according to the anticipated exposure during the task. | Objective 1. [CDC · Infection prevention in chronic hemodialysis](https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/preview/mmwrhtml/rr5005a1.htm) |
| 2 | D | Gloves can have small defects and hands can become contaminated during removal. Hand hygiene is needed after removal. | Objective 1. [CDC · Core interventions for dialysis bloodstream infection prevention](https://www.cdc.gov/dialysis-safety/hcp/clinical-safety/) |
| 3 | A | Face and eye protection help prevent a splash from reaching mucous membranes. Use gloves and protective clothing when the task also exposes hands or clothing. | Objective 1. [CDC · Infection prevention in chronic hemodialysis](https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/preview/mmwrhtml/rr5005a1.htm) |
| 4 | C | A process audit shows which safety steps were performed during observed opportunities. Use the findings for feedback and improvement. | Objective 4. [CDC · Core interventions for dialysis bloodstream infection prevention](https://www.cdc.gov/dialysis-safety/hcp/clinical-safety/) |
| 5 | B | Hands and gloves can carry contamination to clean stock. Remove the gloves and perform hand hygiene before entering the clean workflow. | Objective 1. [CDC · Infection prevention in chronic hemodialysis](https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/preview/mmwrhtml/rr5005a1.htm) |
| 6 | D | Dialysis-specific precautions for hepatitis B can include assigned equipment and other controls. The charge nurse verifies the correct arrangement under the facility protocol. | Objective 4. [CDC · Infection prevention in chronic hemodialysis](https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/preview/mmwrhtml/rr5005a1.htm) |
| 7 | C | The product instructions specify how long the surface must remain wet. Follow the facility-approved process for that product. | Objective 2. [CDC · Dialysis station disinfection checklist](https://www.cdc.gov/dialysis-safety/media/pdfs/env-checklist-508.pdf) |
| 8 | A | A dry appearance alone does not establish that the product had the required wet contact time. Complete the approved procedure before release. | Objective 2. [CDC · Dialysis station disinfection checklist](https://www.cdc.gov/dialysis-safety/media/pdfs/env-checklist-508.pdf) |
| 9 | D | Routine disinfection begins after the station is vacated. Patient safety takes priority when the person cannot yet move safely. | Objective 2. [CDC · Dialysis station disinfection checklist](https://www.cdc.gov/dialysis-safety/media/pdfs/env-checklist-508.pdf) |
| 10 | B | The cuff and receiving chair each need their own completed process. Release each for reuse only after the facility’s applicable cleaning and disinfection requirements are met. | Objective 2. [CDC · Dialysis station disinfection checklist](https://www.cdc.gov/dialysis-safety/media/pdfs/env-checklist-508.pdf) |
| 11 | A | Treat the contact as a clean-workflow breach. Prevent further use of the affected items and follow the facility’s escalation and remediation process. | Objective 4. [CDC · Infection prevention in chronic hemodialysis](https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/preview/mmwrhtml/rr5005a1.htm) |
| 12 | C | Point-of-use disposal in a designated sharps container reduces handling and protects clinical and waste personnel. | Objective 3. [Philippine DOH · Health Care Waste Management Manual](https://ntp.doh.gov.ph/download/health-care-waste-management-manual/) |
| 13 | D | The fill line identifies when safe capacity has been reached. Close and replace the container through the approved process to limit protrusions and needlestick injuries. | Objective 3. [Philippine DOH · Health Care Waste Management Manual](https://ntp.doh.gov.ph/download/health-care-waste-management-manual/) |
| 14 | A | Prompt first aid and reporting support timely assessment for bloodborne infections and any indicated follow-up. | Objective 3. [CDC/NIOSH · Bloodborne infectious disease and exposure response](https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/topics/bbp/default.html) |
| 15 | B | Irrigation is the appropriate first aid for an eye exposure. Prompt reporting and clinical evaluation are needed even without symptoms. | Objective 3. [CDC/NIOSH · Bloodborne infectious disease and exposure response](https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/topics/bbp/default.html) |

# Module 04 · Self-Assessment

Choose one best answer per question. Score one point per correct response. Suggested mastery is 12 of 15. Review missed safety items on prescription verification, dialysate checks or specimen discrepancies even if you reach the suggested score.

1. **Which force primarily drives the diffusion of urea across a dialyzer membrane?**  
   A. A transmembrane pressure difference  
   B. A difference in blood and dialysate temperatures  
   C. A concentration difference across the membrane  
   D. A difference between pre- and post-treatment body weight  

2. **Which term describes net water movement across the membrane driven by a pressure difference?**  
   A. Ultrafiltration  
   B. Diffusion  
   C. Solute clearance  
   D. Countercurrent flow  

3. **A dissolved solute crosses the membrane as it is carried along with moving water. Which process describes that solute movement?**  
   A. Diffusion  
   B. Convection  
   C. Recirculation  
   D. Adsorption  

4. **At one point in a teaching dialyzer, dialysate bicarbonate concentration is higher than blood bicarbonate concentration. In which direction does bicarbonate tend to diffuse at that point?**  
   A. Toward the dialysate  
   B. Along the blood channel only  
   C. Along the dialysate channel only  
   D. Toward the blood  

5. **Why does countercurrent flow help sustain diffusion in a conventional dialyzer?**

   B. It maintains a concentration difference for waste solutes along more of the membrane.  
   C. It removes the need for dialysate at the blood outlet.  
   D. It determines the patient's target weight.  

6. **In a simulated session, the net ultrafiltration goal is zero while blood and fresh dialysate still flow. What can still happen?**  
   A. Net fluid removal at the same rate as a positive ultrafiltration goal  
   B. Solute transfer only if blood and dialysate mix directly  
   C. Urea removal by diffusion  
   D. No urea removal until the ultrafiltration goal is raised

   A. Treated dialysis water and the matched acid and bicarbonate concentrates  
   B. Treated dialysis water and saline only

8. **In the conceptual expression Kt/V, what does t represent?**  
   A. Dialysate temperature  
   B. Target post-treatment weight  
   C. The sample collection time after dialysis  
   D. Treatment duration

   A. The programmed and actual ultrafiltration totals

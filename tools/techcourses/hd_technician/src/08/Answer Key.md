# Module 08 · Answer Key and Rationales

| Item | Answer | Rationale | Review and source |
|---:|:---:|---|---|
| 1 | B | An AV fistula connects the patient’s artery and vein directly to create access for hemodialysis. | Objective 1. [NIDDK · Hemodialysis and vascular access](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/kidney-disease/kidney-failure/hemodialysis) |
| 2 | C | A graft uses an implanted conduit, typically synthetic, to bridge the artery and vein. A fistula joins the vessels directly. | Objective 1. [NIDDK · Hemodialysis and vascular access](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/kidney-disease/kidney-failure/hemodialysis) |
| 3 | A | A CVC is a tube placed into a large central vein to withdraw and return blood for hemodialysis. | Objective 1. [NIDDK · Hemodialysis and vascular access](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/kidney-disease/kidney-failure/hemodialysis) |
| 4 | D | Bloodstream infection risk varies by access type. Central venous catheters carry the highest relative risk, and infection precautions apply to every type. | Objective 1. [CDC · Dialysis access and infections](https://www.cdc.gov/dialysis-safety/about/index.html) |
| 5 | B | An external exit site and catheter lumens are consistent with a CVC model. Verify a patient’s actual access using the chart and the responsible clinician. | Objective 1. [NIDDK · Hemodialysis and vascular access](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/kidney-disease/kidney-failure/hemodialysis) |
| 6 | C | A thrill is a palpable vibration from blood flow. A bruit is the sound heard during authorized assessment. | Objective 2. [National Kidney Foundation · Hemodialysis access](https://www.kidney.org/kidney-topics/hemodialysis-access) |
| 7 | A | Avoid routine compression of the access arm and confirm the appropriate alternate measurement site with the clinical team. | Objective 2. [National Kidney Foundation · Hemodialysis access](https://www.kidney.org/kidney-topics/hemodialysis-access) |
| 8 | B | Keeping the access and connections visible supports early recognition of bleeding or dislodgement. Offer warmth without blocking observation. | Objective 2. [National Kidney Foundation · Hemodialysis access](https://www.kidney.org/kidney-topics/hemodialysis-access) |
| 9 | D | A wet or loose catheter dressing needs prompt review. The trainee should avoid unassigned dressing or line manipulation. | Objective 2. [CDC · Dialysis access and infections](https://www.cdc.gov/dialysis-safety/about/index.html) |
| 10 | A | An absent or newly changed thrill can reflect impaired flow and is a warning sign. Clinical assessment determines the cause and readiness for use. | Objective 3. [KDOQI · Vascular access guideline, Table 13.2](https://www.kidney.org/sites/default/files/2026-07/2019-kdoqi-guideline-vascular-access.pdf) |
| 11 | C | New local inflammation or drainage may indicate infection. Report objective findings, including any fever or chills reported by the patient. | Objective 3. [CDC · Dialysis access and infections](https://www.cdc.gov/dialysis-safety/about/index.html) |
| 12 | D | A change in thrill needs assessment before use. The supervising clinician decides the next step. | Objective 4. [KDOQI · Vascular access guideline, Table 13.2](https://www.kidney.org/sites/default/files/2026-07/2019-kdoqi-guideline-vascular-access.pdf) |
| 13 | B | Swelling and pain can indicate infiltration or another access problem. The nurse must assess promptly, and trainees should avoid unassigned manipulation. | Objective 4. [KDOQI · Vascular access guideline, Table 13.2](https://www.kidney.org/sites/default/files/2026-07/2019-kdoqi-guideline-vascular-access.pdf) |
| 14 | A | Heavy access bleeding can become life threatening. Summon help immediately and support the authorized response. | Objective 4. [National Kidney Foundation · Hemodialysis access](https://www.kidney.org/kidney-topics/hemodialysis-access) |
| 15 | C | A useful handoff distinguishes the patient report from a clinical diagnosis and states timing, the hold, and who needs to assess next. | Objective 4. [Philippine DOH · Dialysis facility standards](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/10/50687) |

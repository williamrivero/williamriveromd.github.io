# Module 12 · Answer Key and Rationales

| Item | Answer | Rationale | Review and source |
|---:|:---:|---|---|
| 1 | C | Two person-specific identifiers tie the patient to the correct record and treatment order. Location and schedule do not establish identity. | Objective 1. [Joint Commission · Two patient identifiers](https://www.jointcommission.org/en-us/knowledge-library/support-center/standards-interpretation/standards-faqs/000001463) |
| 2 | A | A usable log links results to the sampling location, time and required response. It lets staff verify whether the affected system is cleared. | Objective 1. [CDC · Dialysis water use and quality](https://www.cdc.gov/dialysis-safety/hcp/recommendations-resources/water-use-in-dialysis.html) |
| 3 | D | Cleanliness, equipment readiness and water quality are separate checks. Pending water verification prevents authorized release of affected use. | Objective 1. [CDC · Dialysis water use and quality](https://www.cdc.gov/dialysis-safety/hcp/recommendations-resources/water-use-in-dialysis.html) |
| 4 | B | Both discrepancies need resolution through the appropriate pathways. Neither can be cleared using information from the other. | Objective 1. [Joint Commission · Two patient identifiers](https://www.jointcommission.org/en-us/knowledge-library/support-center/standards-interpretation/standards-faqs/000001463) |
| 5 | A | Machine and infection-control checks are independent. Complete the required disinfection process before introducing the patient or clean supplies. | Objective 1. [CDC · Dialysis station routine disinfection](https://www.cdc.gov/dialysis-safety/media/pdfs/env-checklist-508.pdf) |
| 6 | D | A check-back uses confirmation between team members to verify that the instruction was understood. | Objective 2. [AHRQ · TeamSTEPPS tools](https://www.ahrq.gov/teamstepps-program/resources/modules/index.html) |
| 7 | C | A concise handoff names the affected station, identifies each observed exception, states the hold and requests action. | Objective 2. [AHRQ · TeamSTEPPS tools](https://www.ahrq.gov/teamstepps-program/resources/modules/index.html) |
| 8 | B | A repeat is followed by the sender’s confirmation or correction. Continue following the hold while clarifying the instruction. | Objective 2. [AHRQ · TeamSTEPPS tools](https://www.ahrq.gov/teamstepps-program/resources/modules/index.html) |
| 9 | A | Urgent symptoms require immediate escalation. State the location and observations, then assist within your assigned emergency role. | Objective 2. [National Kidney Foundation · Hemodialysis treatment day checklist](https://www.kidney.org/sites/default/files/2026-09/441-0518_2608-patflyer_checklist_hemodialysis-v2.pdf) |
| 10 | D | A handoff is useful when responsibility is explicit and follow-through is reported. Each unresolved hold remains active until appropriately cleared. | Objective 2. [AHRQ · TeamSTEPPS tools](https://www.ahrq.gov/teamstepps-program/resources/modules/index.html) |
| 11 | C | A near miss reveals a hazard or process weakness even when intervention prevented patient exposure. | Objective 3. [AHRQ · Event reporting, investigation and analysis](https://www.ahrq.gov/patient-safety/settings/hospital/candor/modules/notes4.html) |
| 12 | A | This measures the intended behavior with a defined denominator and can be compared across audits. | Objective 3. [AHRQ · Event reporting, investigation and analysis](https://www.ahrq.gov/patient-safety/settings/hospital/candor/modules/notes4.html) |
| 13 | B | Written knowledge and observed performance are distinct. Capstone sign-off requires evidence that the learner can complete required critical steps. | Objective 4. [Module 12 study guide · Capstone sign-off](Study%20Guide.md) |
| 14 | D | Observation records exposure. Authorization for supervised or independent performance requires the applicable competency and facility process. | Objective 4. [Philippine DOH · Dialysis facility standards](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/10/50687) |
| 15 | C | Objective chronology supports review of how the issue reached the station, how it was caught and which process could be tested for improvement. | Objective 3.

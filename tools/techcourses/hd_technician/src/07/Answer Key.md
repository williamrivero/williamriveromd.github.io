# Module 07 · Answer Key and Rationales

| Item | Answer | Rationale | Review and source |
|---:|:---:|---|---|
| 1 | B | Activated carbon adsorbs or reduces disinfectant residues. The facility uses prescribed monitoring to detect breakthrough. | Objective 1. [CDC · Water systems in health-care facilities](https://www.cdc.gov/infection-control/hcp/environmental-control/water.html) |
| 2 | D | Permeate is the product stream that passes through the RO membrane. The reject stream carries concentrated contaminants away. | Objective 1. [CDC · Water systems in health-care facilities](https://www.cdc.gov/infection-control/hcp/environmental-control/water.html) |
| 3 | A | Dialysis uses substantial water volumes and the membrane provides an exchange surface. Dialysis-specific chemical and microbiological controls apply to the water used to prepare dialysate. | Objective 1. [CDC · Water use in dialysis](https://www.cdc.gov/dialysis-safety/hcp/recommendations-resources/water-use-in-dialysis.html) |
| 4 | C | The distribution system delivers treated product water to points of use. Its design and maintenance help control contamination. | Objective 1. [CDC · Water systems in health-care facilities](https://www.cdc.gov/infection-control/hcp/environmental-control/water.html) |
| 5 | B | Product water travels from the RO product side through the facility’s distribution system to the station. Keep treated-water feeds distinct from drains and concentrate connections. | Objective 1. [CDC · Dialysis wall boxes and drains](https://www.cdc.gov/dialysis-safety/hcp/infection-control/wall-boxes-drains.html) |
| 6 | D | Culture looks for organisms recoverable under the specified culture conditions. Endotoxin testing measures a bacterial product and answers a different question. | Objective 2. [CDC · Water use in dialysis](https://www.cdc.gov/dialysis-safety/hcp/recommendations-resources/water-use-in-dialysis.html) |
| 7 | A | pH measures acidity or alkalinity. Conductivity measures a property related to the dissolved ions that carry electrical current. | Objective 3. [Fresenius Medical Care · Model-specific machine operator’s manual, example](https://www.freseniusmedicalcare.com/content/dam/fmcna/live/support/documents/operator%27s-manuals---hemodialysis-%28hd%29/2008t-operator%27s-manuals/490122_Rev_AB.pdf) |
| 8 | C | Conductivity describes the combined electrical behavior of dissolved ions. It cannot identify each electrolyte or establish microbiological quality. | Objective 3. [Fresenius Medical Care · Model-specific machine operator’s manual, example](https://www.freseniusmedicalcare.com/content/dam/fmcna/live/support/documents/operator%27s-manuals---hemodialysis-%28hd%29/2008t-operator%27s-manuals/490122_Rev_AB.pdf) |
| 9 | B | Total chlorine helps assess disinfectant breakthrough. Endotoxin monitoring evaluates a different contaminant and remains necessary under the applicable plan. | Objective 2. [CDC · Water use in dialysis](https://www.cdc.gov/dialysis-safety/hcp/recommendations-resources/water-use-in-dialysis.html) |
| 10 | D | Check the actual concentrate identity, compatibility and prescribed formulation. A compatible-looking connector or conductivity reading does not authorize a substitution. | Objective 3. [Fresenius Medical Care · Model-specific machine operator’s manual, example](https://www.freseniusmedicalcare.com/content/dam/fmcna/live/support/documents/operator%27s-manuals---hemodialysis-%28hd%29/2008t-operator%27s-manuals/490122_Rev_AB.pdf) |
| 11 | A | A result is interpretable only in relation to the correct sampling point, collection method and timing. Resolve missing or conflicting instructions before collecting. | Objective 2. [CDC · Water use in dialysis](https://www.cdc.gov/dialysis-safety/hcp/recommendations-resources/water-use-in-dialysis.html) |
| 12 | C | The out-of-range result identifies an unresolved water-quality concern. Use the facility’s procedure to contain the affected supply and obtain authorized clearance. | Objective 4. [CDC · Water use in dialysis](https://www.cdc.gov/dialysis-safety/hcp/recommendations-resources/water-use-in-dialysis.html) |
| 13 | B | Prevent exposure and promptly escalate. The authorized team determines repeat testing, corrective action, and clearance under site procedure. | Objective 4. [Philippine DOH · Dialysis facility standards](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/10/50687) |
| 14 | D | Capture the observation, time, exact result and units, affected use, notifications and follow-up status. Preserve the original result and link any subsequent checks. | Objective 4. [CDC · Water use in dialysis](https://www.cdc.gov/dialysis-safety/hcp/recommendations-resources/water-use-in-dialysis.html) |
| 15 | A | A normal repeat is part of the investigation. Return to use follows the facility’s clearance and documentation requirements. | Objective 4. [Philippine DOH · Dialysis facility standards](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/10/50687) |

# Module 07 · Self-Assessment

Choose one best answer per question. Score one point per correct response. Suggested mastery is 12 of 15. Review missed safety decisions about water safety, concentrate identity or release after a hold even if you reach the suggested score.

   A. Distribution loop  
   B. Activated carbon stage  
   C. Dialysate pH meter

   C. Acid concentrate

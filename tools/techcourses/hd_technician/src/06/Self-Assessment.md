# Module 06 · Self-Assessment

Choose one best answer per question. Score one point per correct response. Suggested mastery is 12 of 15. Review missed safety decisions about readiness checks, equipment faults or escalation even if you reach the suggested score.

   A. Venous pressure monitor  
   B. Air detector

   D. Blood pump

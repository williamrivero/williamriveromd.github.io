# Module 10 · Answer Key and Rationales

| Item | Answer | Rationale | Review and source |
|---:|:---:|---|---|
| 1 | B | Intradialytic hypotension is low blood pressure during dialysis. A symptomatic fall needs prompt clinical review under the facility’s procedure. | Objective 1. [UK Kidney Association · Fluid assessment and management](https://guidelines.ukkidney.org/haemodialysis/rationale-for-clinical-practice-guidelines/fluid-in-haemodialysis/) |
| 2 | D | Heavy access bleeding can be life-threatening. Initiate the unit’s emergency escalation immediately. | Objective 1. [National Kidney Foundation · Hemodialysis access](https://www.kidney.org/kidney-topics/hemodialysis-access) |
| 3 | A | A machine monitors defined equipment conditions. New chest pain and breathlessness require immediate clinical attention whether or not the machine signals an alarm. | Objective 1. [National Kidney Foundation · Hemodialysis treatment day checklist](https://www.kidney.org/sites/default/files/2026-09/441-0518_2608-patflyer_checklist_hemodialysis-v2.pdf) |
| 4 | C | Report the symptoms, pressure, timing and trend. The supervising clinician assesses the cause and directs treatment. | Objective 1. [UK Kidney Association · Fluid assessment and management](https://guidelines.ukkidney.org/haemodialysis/rationale-for-clinical-practice-guidelines/fluid-in-haemodialysis/) |
| 5 | B | Chills and new exit-site drainage can accompany an access infection. Report both promptly without assigning a diagnosis. | Objective 1. [National Kidney Foundation · Hemodialysis access](https://www.kidney.org/kidney-topics/hemodialysis-access) |
| 6 | D | Dislodgement can cause rapid blood loss. Call for help based on the observed hazard and assist under the emergency procedure within your training. | Objective 2. [National Kidney Foundation · Hemodialysis treatment day checklist](https://www.kidney.org/sites/default/files/2026-09/441-0518_2608-patflyer_checklist_hemodialysis-v2.pdf) |
| 7 | A | A call-out shares critical information quickly with the team at the point of care. Include the location and immediate concern. | Objective 2. [AHRQ · TeamSTEPPS call-out](https://www.ahrq.gov/teamstepps-program/curriculum/communication/tools/callout.html) |
| 8 | C | The receiver repeats the instruction, and the sender confirms or corrects it. This closes the communication loop. | Objective 3. [AHRQ · TeamSTEPPS tools](https://www.ahrq.gov/teamstepps-program/resources/modules/index.html) |
| 9 | B | A concise call-out states the urgency, location and observed problem so the team can act quickly. | Objective 2. [AHRQ · TeamSTEPPS call-out](https://www.ahrq.gov/teamstepps-program/curriculum/communication/tools/callout.html) |
| 10 | D | Acknowledge and complete the assigned task promptly. Closing the loop lets the team know it has been completed. | Objective 3. [AHRQ · TeamSTEPPS tools](https://www.ahrq.gov/teamstepps-program/resources/modules/index.html) |
| 11 | A | Record the chronology, symptoms, escalation, assigned actions and follow-up objectively after urgent care needs are addressed. | Objective 3. [Philippine DOH · Dialysis facility standards](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/10/50687) |
| 12 | C | Debrief uses observed events to identify what worked and what the team can practice or improve before the next drill. | Objective 3. [AHRQ · TeamSTEPPS tools](https://www.ahrq.gov/teamstepps-program/resources/modules/index.html) |
| 13 | B | The scope of a disruption informs both clinical priorities and technical containment. Report affected stations and whether treatments are active. | Objective 4. [CDC · Dialysis water use and supply disruptions](https://www.cdc.gov/dialysis-safety/hcp/recommendations-resources/water-use-in-dialysis.html) |
| 14 | D | Notify clinical leadership quickly. Conditions across machines may differ, so the trainee reports status and carries out assigned contingency actions. | Objective 4. [Philippine DOH · Dialysis facility standards](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/10/50687) |
| 15 | A | Utility restoration is an input to recovery. Required water and equipment checks and authorized release must occur before use. | Objective 4. [CDC · Healthcare water system recovery](https://www.cdc.gov/infection-control/hcp/reopen-health-facilities/water-system-repair.html) |

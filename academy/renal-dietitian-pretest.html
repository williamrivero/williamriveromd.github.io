How the pre-test works

  • Who it is for: transferees and experienced hires starting this role in a new unit. New to the role? Skip the pre-test and take the modules in order.
  • What it asks: up to 5 questions for each of the 13 testable modules, drawn at random from that module’s own question bank. You can test every module in one sitting or choose some now and the rest later; each module’s result is saved as soon as you finish it.
  • Placing out: score 80% or better on a module and it becomes optional reading for you. Below 80%, the module stays on your study path. Answers and rationales are shown only after each module is scored, and a module can be retaken 24 hours after the last try.
  • Always required: 3 modules in this track cannot be placed out of: unit orientation, infection prevention, emergencies, water and machine safety, and the capstone with its checklist sign-off. Experience elsewhere does not tell anyone how this unit does these things.
  • The certificate still needs every module quiz. For a module you placed out of, open it and go straight to its self-assessment at the end; you do not have to read it first.
  • Privacy: your answers and results stay in this browser on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere. Your head nurse or preceptor decides how your onboarding goes; print your result to show them.

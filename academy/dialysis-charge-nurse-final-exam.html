Before you begin

How this examination works

This examination covers the whole Dialysis Charge Nurse series. Each sitting draws 40 questions at random from a bank of 270, in random order, with the answer choices shuffled. Choose the single best answer for each question, then select Check answers.

When to take itAfter you have finished every module and reviewed the questions you missed.
How to take itIn one sitting, without notes. Allow about one minute per question.
Proposed pass mark32 of 40 best answers (80 percent). Your trainer may set a different mark.
If you fall shortEach explanation names its module. Reread those modules, then select New set of questions.

Abbreviations in this examination. Advanced Cardiovascular Life Support (ACLS); Administrative Order (AO); cardiopulmonary resuscitation (CPR); Department of Health (DOH); International Society for Peritoneal Dialysis (ISPD); Kidney Disease Outcomes Quality Initiative (KDOQI); Situation-Background-Assessment-Recommendation (SBAR).

What a pass meansA pass shows that you know the material. It does not show that you can do the work. Competence is demonstrated on your unit’s own equipment and signed off by a preceptor.

Program design

Using the track to prepare a new unit manager

The track supports orientation; it does not replace it. A practical plan is two modules a week over ten weeks, each paired with the supervised practice at the learner’s own unit, then the capstone. Head nurses, the medical director, the water and biomedical technicians, and accounting staff should join the exercises that touch their work.

Figure 1 · Suggested sequence

  1. Weeks 1 to 3Modules 1 to 6. Decision-rights map, evidence index, roster, capacity, infection, and water logs.
  2. Weeks 4 to 6Modules 7 to 12. Emergency drill, recall exercise, run chart, claim trace, budget, and outage plan.
  3. Week 7Module 13 or 14 for your setting.
  4. Weeks 8 to 9Modules 15 to 18. Coaching, month-end close, payroll cutoff, and remittance reconciliation.
  5. Week 10Module 19, the capstone exercise and portfolio defense.

Keep signed assessments in the facility’s approved system, and use fictional or de-identified data in every exercise.

Completion

Proposed completion criteria

  • Self-assessments: 4 of 5 on two different sets for each module, with every missed question reviewed.
  • Every Critical step on every supervised-practice checklist demonstrated to a preceptor.
  • Capstone portfolio accepted by the review panel.

Completion is an Academy learning record. It is not continuing professional development (CPD) credit, Department of Health (DOH) or Philippine Health Insurance Corporation (PhilHealth) eligibility, or a management appointment.

Written examination. The end-of-course examination draws 40 questions at random from the question banks of all modules. A proposed pass mark is 80 percent, with missed questions reviewed against their modules before a second sitting. For a supervised sitting, the learner takes it on a unit device with the preceptor present and the preceptor records the score.

Printable

Skills checklists for every module

Print this page for a full set. Each checklist starts on a new sheet. Steps marked Critical must all be satisfactory before sign-off; a missed critical step is repeated with the preceptor. Replace any step that does not match your equipment or approved procedure.

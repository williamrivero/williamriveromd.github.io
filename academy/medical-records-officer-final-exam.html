Before you begin

How this examination works

This examination covers the whole Medical Records Officer and Custodian series. Each sitting draws 40 questions at random from a bank of 210, in random order, with the answer choices shuffled. Choose the single best answer for each question, then select Check answers.

When to take itAfter you have finished every module and reviewed the questions you missed.
How to take itIn one sitting, without notes. Allow about one minute per question.
Proposed pass mark32 of 40 best answers (80 percent). Your trainer may set a different mark.
If you fall shortEach explanation names its module. Reread those modules, then select New set of questions.
What a pass meansA pass shows that you know the material. It does not show that you can do the work. Competence is demonstrated on your unit’s own equipment and signed off by a preceptor.

How to get your certificate of completion

  1. Pass all 14 module quizzes. Each module quiz is passed at 80%, or at the pass mark the module states. The Certificate checklist above the examination shows which modules are done; each line links to that module's quiz.
  2. Use the same device and browser for the modules and this examination. Progress is saved in your browser, not in an account. On iPhone and iPad, the Home Screen app and Safari keep separate progress.
  3. Pass this timed examination (80%; where these instructions say so, every safety-critical question must also be correct). You have one minute per question from the moment you tap Start the examination; only your first check of a set counts. If you pass before finishing the modules, your pass is saved: finish the module quizzes, then come back here.
  4. Type your name the way you want it printed and tap Make my certificate. You can tick Add a preceptor sign-off line if your unit will sign the printed copy.
  5. Save or print your completion packet: two pages, the certificate and a record of your module results. Use Print or save as PDF to keep both pages together.
  6. Be online when you make it, so the certificate gets its QR code. Anyone can scan the code, or enter it at renalcarematters.com/verify, to confirm it.

Not finished yet? Use Print a progress record in the checklist to show a preceptor how far you have gone; it is clearly marked "not a certificate". The certificate is a self-study certificate of completion, not CPD units: competence is demonstrated on your own unit and signed off by a preceptor. To make the QR code work, your name, track, date and score are saved with the certificate code; to have them removed, use Report a correction on any guide.

Abbreviations

AKI
Acute kidney injury.
CI
Confidence interval — The range in which the true rate plausibly lies.
DBNMS
Data Breach Notification Management System — The NPC’s online channel for breach notifications.
DC
Department circular — A numbered DOH issuance that disseminates or clarifies policy.
DOH
Department of Health — Philippines.
DPO
Data protection officer — The person who advises on privacy and handles breach assessment.
EHR
Electronic health record — A computerized record of a patient’s care.
EMR
Electronic medical record — A computerized record used within one facility.
HD
Hemodialysis.
HR
Hazard ratio.
ICD-10
International Classification of Diseases, 10th Revision — The diagnosis code set PhilHealth uses, with Philippine modifications.
JAO
Joint administrative order — An order issued jointly by two or more agencies.
LTO
License to Operate — The DOH license a facility needs to run.
MPI
Master patient index — The list linking each person to one chart number.
MRO
Medical records officer — The person designated to keep a facility’s records.
NAP
National Archives of the Philippines — Approves disposal schedules and authority for public records.
NPC
National Privacy Commission — The Philippine body that enforces the Data Privacy Act.
PD
Peritoneal dialysis.
PDD
PhilHealth Dialysis Database — PhilHealth’s eligibility database for the hemodialysis package.
PhilHealth
Philippine Health Insurance Corporation.
PIN
PhilHealth Identification Number — The member number PhilHealth assigns.
PRDR
Philippine Renal Disease Registry — The national registry of patients on kidney replacement therapy.
PSN
Philippine Society of Nephrology.
PTH
Parathyroid hormone.
PWD
Person with disability — Eligible for a PWD identification card and related benefits.
REDCOP
Renal Disease Control Program — The DOH program the registry supports.
SOP
Standard operating procedure — The unit’s approved written steps for a task.
WHO
World Health Organization.
YAKAP
Yaman ng Kalusugan Program — PhilHealth’s primary-care benefit.

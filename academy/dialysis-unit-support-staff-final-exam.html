Nonclinical support pathway · orderlies and environmental service aides

This subtrack prepares orderlies, environmental service (EVS) aides, utility workers and contracted cleaners for nonclinical support work in an adult hemodialysis unit. It does not authorize nursing care, machine operation, needle or catheter work, medicines, dialyzer reprocessing, water-treatment work or technical release of a station. Your employer’s written task list, your supervisor and the nurse on duty decide what you may do. All people and cases are fictional.

End-of-course knowledge check

15 questions, one from each module

Each sitting draws one question at random from each of the 15 modules (a pool of 45 questions), in a shuffled order with shuffled options. Answer all of them, then select Check answers. The pass mark is 12 of 15. A reason appears under every option.

A pass marks your Academy self-study as complete. It is not employer-verified competence and does not authorize any task. If you do not pass, review the modules for the questions you missed, then select New set of questions for a parallel version.

Before you begin

How this examination works

This examination covers the whole Philippine Health Insurance Corporation (PhilHealth) Liaison, Claims Officer and Clerk series. Each sitting draws 40 questions at random from a bank of 165, in random order, with the answer choices shuffled. Choose the single best answer for each question, then select Check answers.

When to take itAfter you have finished every module and reviewed the questions you missed.
How to take itIn one sitting, without notes. Allow about one minute per question.
Proposed pass mark32 of 40 best answers (80 percent). Your trainer may set a different mark.
If you fall shortEach explanation names its module. Reread those modules, then select New set of questions.
What a pass meansA pass shows that you know the material. It does not show that you can do the work. Competence is demonstrated on your unit’s own equipment and signed off by a preceptor.

Abbreviations

AKI
Acute kidney injury.
CF1
Claim Form 1 — Member and patient information for a claim.
CF2
Claim Form 2 — Provider and service information, including the diagnosis and procedure codes and hemodialysis consumption.
CF4
Claim Form 4 — Clinical information for the claim, where required.
CF5
Claim Form 5 — Inpatient clinical data for diagnosis-related group shadow billing; mandatory for inpatient discharges from August 1, 2026.
CKD
Chronic kidney disease.
DPO
Data Protection Officer — The person who leads the facility’s privacy program and incident response.
DRG
Diagnosis-related group — A payment group assigned from a hospital case’s full set of diagnosis and procedure codes.
eSOA
Electronic statement of account — The bill for a claim, sent to PhilHealth as structured XML data.
HD
Hemodialysis.
HDF
Hemodiafiltration — A form of dialysis that adds large-volume convection to diffusion.
HIV
Human immunodeficiency virus.
LHIO
Local Health Insurance Office — PhilHealth’s local office for members and facilities.
NPC
National Privacy Commission — The government agency that enforces the Data Privacy Act.
PD
Peritoneal dialysis.
PDD
PhilHealth Dialysis Database — The registry of patients with chronic kidney disease stage 5 on dialysis; registration is required for the dialysis benefits.
PDF
Portable Document Format — A file that looks like a printed page; PhilHealth no longer accepts it as the statement of account.
PhilHealth
Philippine Health Insurance Corporation.
PIN
PhilHealth Identification Number — The member’s permanent PhilHealth number.
PM
Preventive maintenance — Scheduled service done before something breaks.
PRO
PhilHealth Regional Office — Where a facility gets written guidance on PhilHealth rules.
RVS
Relative Value Scale — PhilHealth’s procedure codes; 90935 is hemodialysis.
SOP
Standard operating procedure — The unit’s approved written steps for a task.
XML
Extensible Markup Language — A structured file format computers can check field by field.

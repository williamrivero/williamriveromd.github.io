For patients and care partners

This course helps you understand your care and prepare questions. It does not give individual fluid limits, food limits, blood pressure targets, medicine doses or machine settings: those come from your own care team. Practical home dialysis training happens with your treatment service. No website, quiz or chat is watched in real time; in an emergency, get help now. Every person in the stories is fictional.

Start here

Understand your care, one short lesson at a time

This course is for people living with kidney disease and for the people who support them: a spouse, a son or daughter, a friend, a paid helper. Start with the shared foundations, then choose the pathway that fits your treatment or the person you support. Each module takes about 15 minutes: five minutes of reading, five of practice, and five explaining it back in your own words.

Every module follows the same path. It opens with an everyday picture, shows where the picture stops being true, explains how the body really works, and only then gives the practical rule. A realistic Filipino situation shows the rule in action, and a short “What the research shows” box says honestly how strong the evidence is. You leave each module with questions to bring to your next visit.

⚠️

What this course is, and is not

This course is education, not treatment. It does not diagnose, choose a treatment, or set any personal number. Finishing it gives no job qualification, nursing or technician credential, Philippine Society of Nephrology (PSN) recognition, Philippine Health Insurance Corporation (PhilHealth) accreditation, Department of Health (DOH) licensure or continuing professional development (CPD) credit, and it never authorizes anyone to perform dialysis or any procedure. Practical home dialysis training and readiness are decided by your treatment service. Translations are not yet available. Draft for clinical review: not yet signed off by the clinical editor.

Find my learning path

Choose the path that fits you

Choosing a path selects lessons, not treatment. It does not ask for your diagnosis, laboratory values or any identifier, and you can change it any time. Care partners add the pathway of the person they support. You can read any module, in any order, whenever you need it.

How each module works

The same steps every time

1. The everyday pictureA familiar image, then where it stops being true.
2. Three short lessonsHow the body works, then what to do.
3. A real situationA fictional Filipino story with what happened next.
4. What the research showsThe evidence in plain words, with how sure we are.
5. Questions to ask your teamBring them to your next visit.
6. Practice and teach backA worksheet, then explain it in your own words.
7. Three quick questionsWith explanations, as many tries as you like.

Every module page can be read aloud with the Listen button, highlighted and annotated on your own device, and saved for use with no signal in the Schola app. Ask for help with reading, hearing, eyesight or language; an educator can go through any module with you and check your answers out loud.

Checking what you learned

Learning, not testing

Each module ends with three questions and explanations. When you have finished your pathway, the final knowledge check builds a set from the modules you studied: the first question of every shared-foundation module, every safety question from your pathway, and more questions up to 20 (it shows the real number when your path has more or fewer). It passes at 80 percent with every safety question correct; a missed safety question shows you the right answer and the reason. There is no time limit, and an educator may read the questions with you.

Finishing gives you a printable self-reported learning summary, never a certificate or a qualification. Hands-on skills, such as peritoneal dialysis exchanges, are taught and checked only by your treatment team, on their own records.

For care partners

Helping without taking over

A care partner is someone the patient chooses. The patient decides what you help with and what you may know; being family does not by itself give access to records. Start with the shared foundations, add the pathway of the person you support, then the three care-partner modules: A01 on supporting choices, A02 on organizing the practical load, and A03 on looking after yourself. Helping with daily life never authorizes clinical procedures: any home dialysis task needs training and assessment by the home team.

What the course is built on

Sources and review

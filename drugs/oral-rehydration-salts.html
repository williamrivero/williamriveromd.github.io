Clinical evidence and practical considerations

Preparation and clinical context

Oral rehydration solutions are designed around coupled solute and water absorption. Altered electrolyte and osmolar formulations have different applications and should not be treated as identical preparations. [Oral rehydration review]

The Philippine registry contains multiple products and presentations. Use the water volume specified for the actual sachet; the generic heading alone does not establish a dilution recipe or an appropriate intake for a fluid-restricted patient. [PH FDA registry]

Mechanism of action

Uses intestinal sodium–glucose cotransport to promote water absorption and replace gastrointestinal fluid and electrolyte losses.

Class: Oral glucose–electrolyte rehydration preparation

Clinical evidence and practical considerations

Formulation and kidney context

The registry heading groups product records; it does not supply an individualized infusion rate or prove equivalence with another balanced solution. Check the complete bag composition and approved insert before use, particularly when fluid or electrolyte handling is impaired. [PH FDA registry]

Clinical evidence

SMART compared saline with a balanced-fluid strategy using lactated Ringer’s or Plasma-Lyte A. The balanced group had fewer major adverse kidney events at 30 days, a composite endpoint; this was not a trial of every marketed balanced formulation. [SMART trial]

A later meta-analysis found uncertainty around mortality and renal-replacement effects. Balanced-fluid evidence should not be presented as proof of kidney protection for every brand or every patient. [Balanced fluid meta-analysis]

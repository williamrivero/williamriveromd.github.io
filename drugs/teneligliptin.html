Dosing: Altered kidney function

Teneligliptin is a dipeptidyl peptidase-4 (DPP-4) inhibitor cleared by both liver (CYP3A4, flavin monooxygenases) and kidney (15% to 22% unchanged in urine). Kidney impairment raises exposure only modestly (at most about 1.7-fold), and the Thai label, based on the Japanese originator, sets no kidney dose adjustment; hemodialysis removes about 16% of a dose. It has no US FDA label.

Kidney function measure: The label reports kidney pharmacokinetics by severity without numeric thresholds or dose changes; bands shown on creatinine clearance (CrCl).

Adverse reactions

Common

  • Hypoglycemia (2.6%)
  • Constipation (0.9%)

Serious

  • Hypoglycemia (with sulfonylurea or insulin)
  • Intestinal obstruction (0.1%)
  • Acute pancreatitis
  • Hepatic dysfunction
  • Interstitial pneumonia
  • Bullous pemphigoid
  • QT prolongation
renalcarematters.com, reviewed

Contraindications

  • Severe ketosis, diabetic coma or precoma, type 1 diabetes
  • Severe infection, around surgery, serious trauma
  • Hypersensitivity

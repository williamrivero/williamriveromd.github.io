Contraindications

  • Absolute: no suitable IV veins; barbiturate hypersensitivity; variegate or acute intermittent porphyria 4
  • Relative: severe cardiovascular disease; hypotension/shock; excessive premedication; Addison disease; hepatic/renal dysfunction; myxedema; increased blood urea; severe anemia; asthma/status asthmaticus; myasthenia gravis 4
renalcarematters.com, reviewed

Dosing: Adult

  • Historical US-label anesthesia induction: 50–75 mg IV at 20–40-second intervals according to response; qualified IV-anesthesia personnel only 4

Adverse reactions

Serious

  • Respiratory/myocardial depression, arrhythmias, laryngospasm, anaphylactic reactions; rare immune hemolytic anemia with renal failure 4
renalcarematters.com, reviewed

Monitoring parameters

  • Maintain airway patency; closely observe respiration; oxygen, intubation and resuscitation equipment must be available. Avoid intra-arterial injection/extravasation 4
renalcarematters.com, reviewed

Mechanism of action

Produces CNS depression and hypnosis without analgesia.

Class: IV thiobarbiturate anesthetic

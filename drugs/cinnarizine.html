Dosing: Adult

  • Motion sickness (adults): 30 mg 2 hours before travel, then 15 mg every 8 hours during the journey
  • Motion sickness (children 5 to 12 years): Half the adult dose

CKD · dialysis · CRRT

Dosing: Altered kidney function

Cinnarizine is extensively metabolized by the liver (CYP2D6); metabolites are excreted about one-third in urine and two-thirds in feces. The Irish label has no kidney studies and advises using it with care in kidney insufficiency, without a dose change. It has no US FDA label.

Kidney function measure: The SmPC does not grade kidney impairment; bands shown on creatinine clearance (CrCl).

Adverse reactions

Common

  • Somnolence (9.9%)
  • Nausea (3.0%)
  • Weight gain (1.5%)

Serious

  • Extrapyramidal disorder, parkinsonism, dyskinesia
  • Cholestatic jaundice
  • Subacute cutaneous lupus erythematosus, lichen planus

    Show full dosing details
    eGFR ≥60
    No adjustment. Chronic kidney disease (CKD), heart failure and cardiovascular indications: 10 mg once daily. Glycemic control: start 5 mg once daily.
    eGFR 30–59
    CKD, heart failure and cardiovascular indications: 10 mg once daily. Glycemic control: not recommended if eGFR <45 (likely ineffective).
    eGFR 15–29
    eGFR 25–29: may start 10 mg once daily for CKD, heart failure or cardiovascular indications. eGFR <25: do not start; patients already on it may continue 10 mg once daily (label). Not for glycemic control. Guidelines (KDIGO 2024, ADA-KDIGO 2022) support starting SGLT2 inhibitors at eGFR ≥20 (off-label for dapagliflozin at 20–24) and continuing below 20 until intolerance or kidney replacement therapy.
    eGFR <15 (not on dialysis)
    Do not start. May continue 10 mg once daily if started earlier (label: continue if eGFR falls below 25). Not for glycemic control.
    Hemodialysis (intermittent)
    Not studied; trials did not enroll patients on dialysis. Trial patients who started dialysis were not required to stop (label). Effect of hemodialysis on exposure is unknown. Guidelines advise continuing until kidney replacement therapy begins (ADA-KDIGO); not effective for glucose lowering on dialysis. Specialist input.
    Peritoneal dialysis
    Not studied (label). Insufficient data: specialist input.
    CRRT
    No data in label. Insufficient data: specialist input; usually held in acute illness.
    • Dialyzable? Unknown: removal by hemodialysis has not been studied (label).
    • Monitoring Assess kidney function and volume status before starting; correct volume depletion. Expect an early small eGFR dip. Withhold at least 3 days before surgery or prolonged fasting (label); watch for ketoacidosis.

    From the Dapagliflozin monograph (renalcarematters.com, reviewed).

    25 mg once daily (label).
    CRRT
    No data in label.
    • Dialyzable? Modestly: about 13.5% removed over a 3–4 hour hemodialysis session (label).
    • Monitoring Assess kidney function before starting and periodically (label). Pancreatitis, hypoglycemia with sulfonylureas or insulin.

    From the Sitagliptin monograph (renalcarematters.com, reviewed).

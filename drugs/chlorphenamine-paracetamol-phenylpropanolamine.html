1988 Convention Table I (norephedrine); FDA-registered oral preparations are exempt from all DDB regulatory requirements except registration and licensing of the importer, exporter and manufacturer; prescription and dispensing follow FDA rules (BR 1 s.2014 Sec. 4(5)). Parenteral preparations are not covered by this exemption (Sec. 4(2)(c)(i)); prescribing a drug preparation containing a controlled chemical otherwise requires a PDEA S-2 license (Sec. 6(3)).; BR 1 s.2014 Sec. 6(14)-(15): preparations with norephedrine/phenylpropanolamine 25 mg or below that are classified over-the-counter may be advertised to the public and distributed as samples; the S-2 license fee schedule (Sec. 6) likewise excludes them. The precursor exemption for oral FDA-registered preparations applies to combinations as well (BR 1 s.2014 Sec. 4(5)).

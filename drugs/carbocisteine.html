Contraindications

  • Active peptic ulcer
  • Hypersensitivity
renalcarematters.com, reviewed

Dosing: Adult

  • Excess viscous mucus (adjunct; including chronic obstructive airways disease): 2,250 mg/day in divided doses to start (two 375 mg capsules three times daily); reduce to 1,500 mg/day in divided doses after response
renalcarematters.com, reviewed
CKD · dialysis · CRRT

Dosing: Altered kidney function

Carbocisteine is a mucolytic. The Irish label gives no kidney or dialysis dosing and no pharmacokinetic data on kidney impairment, so no evidence-based adjustment can be stated. Its main safety issue is gastrointestinal bleeding in people with ulcer history. It has no US FDA label.

Kidney function measure: The SmPC does not address kidney impairment; bands are shown on creatinine clearance (CrCl) for consistency.

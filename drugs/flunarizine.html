Contraindications

  • History of depression
  • Parkinson disease or other extrapyramidal disorders
  • Hypersensitivity

Dosing: Adult

  • Migraine prophylaxis (adults under 65): 10 mg once daily in the evening; stop if no improvement after 3 months

CKD · dialysis · CRRT

Dosing: Altered kidney function

Flunarizine is highly protein-bound (over 99%), metabolized by the liver and excreted very slowly, with negligible urinary excretion; its terminal half-life is about 19 days. The Canadian product monograph does not address kidney impairment or dialysis. It has no US FDA label.

Kidney function measure: The product monograph does not address kidney impairment; bands shown on creatinine clearance (CrCl).

Adverse reactions

Common

  • Somnolence, sedation or fatigue (about 20%)
  • Weight gain or increased appetite (about 15%)

Serious

  • Depression
  • Extrapyramidal symptoms, parkinsonism
  • Galactorrhea

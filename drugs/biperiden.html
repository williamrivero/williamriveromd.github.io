Clinical evidence and limitations

Study regimens are descriptions of research, not prescribing recommendations.

StudyDesign and populationTreatment and durationMain findingsLimitations
Magnus 1980 4Single-blind active-comparator clinical study.Biperiden versus benzhexol (trihexyphenidyl) for neuroleptic-induced parkinsonism.Both groups responded favorably; no significant difference in individual symptoms between treatments.Older study with limited abstract detail; no placebo comparison, contemporary comparative-effectiveness conclusion or treatment of every antipsychotic-associated movement disorder established.
Bakker 2021 5Randomized placebo-controlled three-way crossover; 12 healthy older adults.Two biperiden challenge regimens versus placebo.The higher challenge impaired sustained attention, verbal memory and working-memory performance; effects were dose/concentration dependent and temporary.A pharmacology experiment, not a dementia-incidence study or therapeutic trial in Parkinson disease. It nevertheless directly demonstrates potential cognitive harm.
renalcarematters.com, reviewed

Dosing: Older adult

  • Healthy older adults experienced dose-related cognitive impairment in the challenge study; a therapeutic benefit must not be inferred from use as a research model 5.
renalcarematters.com, reviewed

Adverse reactions

Common

  • Controlled challenge data show temporary impairment of attention and memory; the small study does not quantify the frequency during routine long-term treatment 5.
renalcarematters.com, reviewed

Mechanism of action

Antagonizes muscarinic acetylcholine receptors; an M1-antagonist challenge has produced temporary cognitive impairment in controlled human studies 5.

Class: Antimuscarinic antiparkinsonian agent

renalcarematters.com, reviewed

    Show full dosing details
    eGFR ≥60
    Usual dosing. Assess kidney function before starting and periodically (label).
    eGFR 30–59
    eGFR 45–59: usual dose; consider dose reduction if at high risk of lactic acidosis. eGFR 30–44: label advises not to start; if continuing, reduce to a maximum of 1,000 mg/day (KDIGO 2022; ADA-KDIGO 2022). Recheck eGFR every 3 to 6 months.
    eGFR 15–29
    Contraindicated (label). Stop if eGFR falls below 30.
    eGFR <15 (not on dialysis)
    Contraindicated (label).
    Hemodialysis (intermittent)
    Contraindicated (eGFR <30 per label).
    Peritoneal dialysis
    Contraindicated (eGFR <30 per label).
    CRRT
    Not appropriate; contraindicated in severe kidney impairment (label).
    • Dialyzable? Yes: dialyzable with clearance up to 170 mL/min under good hemodynamic conditions; hemodialysis may help remove drug in suspected overdose (label).
    • Monitoring eGFR before starting and at least periodically; more often in older adults. Stop before or at the time of iodinated contrast in at-risk patients per label. Hold temporarily during acute illness with dehydration, sepsis, hypoxia or acute heart failure, during procedures with restricted food or fluid intake, and for acute kidney injury; restart when eating and kidney function is stable (label 5.1).

    From the Metformin monograph (renalcarematters.com, reviewed).

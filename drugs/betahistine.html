Contraindications

  • Pheochromocytoma
  • Hypersensitivity
renalcarematters.com, reviewed

Dosing: Adult

  • Ménière's syndrome (vertigo, tinnitus, hearing loss): 8 to 16 mg three times daily with meals; usual maintenance 24 to 48 mg/day
renalcarematters.com, reviewed
CKD · dialysis · CRRT

Dosing: Altered kidney function

Betahistine is converted almost completely to the inactive metabolite 2-pyridylacetic acid (2-PAA), and about 85% of a dose is recovered in urine. The European label has no kidney trials but, based on post-marketing experience, states that no dose adjustment appears necessary in kidney impairment. Dialysis has not been studied. It has no US FDA label.

Kidney function measure: SmPC does not grade kidney impairment; bands are shown on creatinine clearance (CrCl).

  • Camphor

    Use: Uses For the temporary relief of pain and itching associated with: insect bites minor cuts minor burns sunburn scrapes minor skin irritations rashes due to poison ivy, poison oak, or poison sumac US FDA label

  • Eucalyptus oil
  • Menthol

    Use: Uses For temporary relief of minor aches and pains of muscles and joints associated with: arthritis simple backache sprains strains bruises US FDA label

    Use: Uses For the temporary relief of minor aches and pains of muscles and joints associated with: simple backache arthritis strains bruises sprains US FDA label

Clinical evidence and practical considerations

Historical US label: kidney considerations

The 2019 label states that daclatasvir itself requires no dose adjustment at any degree of renal impairment. It separately directs clinicians to the companion sofosbuvir and ribavirin labels; this single-agent statement does not establish the suitability of the whole regimen. Because protein binding exceeds 99%, dialysis is unlikely to substantially lower daclatasvir concentrations. [DAKLINZA historical FDA label (2019)]

Adverse reactions

Serious

  • HBV reactivation, including liver failure and death; serious symptomatic bradycardia with amiodarone plus a sofosbuvir-containing regimen 4
renalcarematters.com, reviewed

Monitoring parameters

  • Before treatment, test HBsAg and anti-HBc; monitor coinfected patients for HBV reactivation during and after HCV treatment (historical US label) 4
renalcarematters.com, reviewed

Mechanism of action

Inhibits HCV nonstructural protein 5A (NS5A).

Class: Direct-acting hepatitis C antiviral; NS5A inhibitor

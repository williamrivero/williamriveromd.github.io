Use: Labeled indications

Full indications and usage US FDA label

Uses • on chest and throat, helps temporarily relieve cough due to common cold • on joints and muscles, temporarily relives minor aches and pains

Dosing: Adult

Dosage and administration (US label) US FDA label

Directions: Adults and Children 2 years and over: • rub a thick layer on chest and throat or rub on sore aching muscles • keep clothing loose around chest/throat to help vapors reach the nose/mouth • repeat up to 3 times per 24 hour or as directed by doctor Children under 2 years: do not use unless directed by doctor.

Warnings/Precautions

Warnings and precautions (US label) US FDA label

Warnings For external use only; avoid contact with eyes. Do not use

  • by mouth
  • in nostrils
  • with tight bandages
  • on wounds or damaged skin Ask doctor before use if you have:
  • cough that occurs with too much phlegm (mucus)
  • persistent or chronic cough such as occurs with asthma, emphysema, or smoking When using this product, do not:
  • heat
  • microwave
  • add to hot water or any container where heating water. May cause splattering and results in burns. Keep out of the reach of children. Stop use and ask a doctor if:
  • cough lasts more than 7 days, comes back, or occurs with fever, rash, or persistent headache. These could be signs of a serious conditions.
  • muscle aches and pain persist more than 7 days or come back

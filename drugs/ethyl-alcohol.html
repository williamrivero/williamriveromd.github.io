Full indications and usage US FDA label

Hand Sanitizer to help reduce bacteria that potentially can cause disease. For use when soap and water are not available.

Dosing: Adult

Directions: For adults and children 2 years and older. Supervise children when they use this products. Rub thoroughly into hands for at least 30 seconds. Allow to dry.

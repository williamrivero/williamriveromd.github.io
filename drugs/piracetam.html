Dosing: Adult

  • Cortical myoclonus: Start 7.2 g/day, increase by 4.8 g/day every 3 to 4 days to a maximum of 24 g/day in 2 or 3 doses; taper by 1.2 g every 2 days when stopping
  • Psycho-organic syndromes or vertigo: 2.4 to 4.8 g/day in 2 or 3 doses

Dosing: Altered kidney function

About 90% of piracetam is excreted unchanged in urine, so the dose falls in steps with creatinine clearance (CrCl): two-thirds at 50–79, one-third at 30–49 and one-sixth (once daily) below 30 mL/min. It is contraindicated in end-stage kidney disease, and hemodialysis removes 50% to 60%. It also inhibits platelet aggregation. It has no US FDA label.

Kidney function measure: European core safety profile uses creatinine clearance (CrCl, mL/min; Cockcroft–Gault, ×0.85 for women).

CrCl ≥60 mL/min
CrCl >80: usual dose in 2 to 4 divided doses (e.g., 2.4 to 4.8 g/day; up to 24 g/day for cortical myoclonus). CrCl 60–79: two-thirds of the usual dose in 2 or 3 divided doses (CSP).
CrCl 30–59 mL/min
CrCl 50–59: two-thirds of the usual daily dose in 2 or 3 divided doses. CrCl 30–49: one-third of the usual daily dose in 2 divided doses (CSP).
CrCl 15–29 mL/min
CrCl <30: one-sixth of the usual daily dose as a single daily dose (CSP).
CrCl <15 mL/min (not on dialysis)
CrCl <30: one-sixth of the usual daily dose once daily. End-stage kidney disease: contraindicated (CSP). Equating CrCl <15 with end-stage disease is our judgment; we keep it red because piracetam is renally cleared, of marginal benefit and affects platelets in a population prone to uremic bleeding.
Hemodialysis (intermittent)
Contraindicated in end-stage kidney disease (CSP). Hemodialysis removes 50% to 60% (dialyzer extraction), useful in overdose.
Peritoneal dialysis
Contraindicated in end-stage kidney disease (CSP).
CRRT
Not appropriate; contraindicated in end-stage kidney disease and no data in acute kidney injury.
  • Dialyzable? Yes: hemodialysis extraction efficiency 50% to 60% (CSP 4.9).
  • Monitoring Creatinine clearance regularly in older adults and long-term use. Bleeding risk (platelet inhibition): caution with anticoagulants, antiplatelets, ulcer, recent surgery or hemorrhagic stroke. Do not stop abruptly in myoclonus (CSP 4.4).

Dosing: Pediatric

Approved ages
Dyslexia from 8 years; sickle cell crises from 3 years (limited data 1 to 3 years) (CSP).
Usual dose
Dyslexia (8 years and older): about 3.2 g/day in 2 doses. Sickle cell: 160 mg/kg/day orally (prophylaxis); 300 mg/kg/day intravenously (crisis) (CSP).

Kidney dosing in children: see Dosing: Altered kidney function.

Adverse reactions

Common

  • Nervousness
  • Hyperkinesia
  • Weight gain

Serious

  • Bleeding (hemorrhagic disorder)
  • Anaphylactoid reactions, angioedema
  • Aggravated epilepsy, hallucinations
renalcarematters.com, reviewed

Contraindications

  • End-stage kidney disease
  • Cerebral hemorrhage
  • Huntington chorea
  • Hypersensitivity to piracetam or other pyrrolidone derivatives

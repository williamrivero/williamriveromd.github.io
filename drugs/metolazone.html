Contraindications

  • Anuria; hepatic coma/precoma; metolazone hypersensitivity 2
renalcarematters.com, reviewed

Dosing: Adult

  • US-label cardiac/renal edema: 5–20 mg orally once daily; individualize and titrate to minimum effective maintenance dose 2
  • US-label mild/moderate essential hypertension: 2.5–5 mg orally once daily 2
renalcarematters.com, reviewed

These are US-label ranges for the cited formulation, not verified Philippine Metoz instructions. Zaroxolyn-type and Mykrox-type formulations must not be interchanged dose-for-dose 2.

CKD · dialysis · CRRT

Dosing: Altered kidney function

The cited US label reports diuretic activity at low GFR, but warns of accumulation in severe renal impairment. Anuria is a contraindication. Discontinue if azotemia and oliguria worsen. No numerical CKD adjustment is specified here, and US formulation instructions do not establish the Philippine Metoz regimen 2.

Kidney function measure: No CKD dosing bands are inferred from historical study CrCl values.

Clinical evidence and prescribing limits

US-label guidance and research evidence are distinguished below. The US label is not the Philippine Metoz package insert.

StudyDesign and populationTreatment and durationMain findingsLimitations
3T randomized trial 360 hospitalized adults with loop-resistant acute heart failure; 48-hour comparison.Metolazone, IV chlorothiazide or tolvaptan added to IV loop diuretics.The 3T trial randomized 60 hospitalized patients with acute heart failure and resistance to high-dose IV loop diuretics to oral metolazone, IV chlorothiazide or tolvaptan, alongside IV loop therapy. At 48 hours, weight loss was 4.6 ± 2.7 kg with metolazone, 5.8 ± 2.7 kg with chlorothiazide and 4.1 ± 3.3 kg with tolvaptan; neither comparison against metolazone was statistically significant. 3 Excluded eGFR <15 mL/min/1.73 m² and patients requiring renal replacement therapy or ultrafiltration. Short, monitored inpatient study; no long-term equivalence or dialysis-regimen conclusion 3.
DAPA-RESIST 461 patients hospitalized with loop-resistant heart failure.Dapagliflozin versus metolazone for three days.61 patients hospitalized with loop-resistant heart failure were randomized to dapagliflozin or metolazone for three days. At 96 hours, mean weight loss was 3.0 versus 3.6 kg (difference 0.65 kg; 95% CI −0.12 to 1.41; P=0.11). Metolazone recipients had larger sodium/potassium decreases and urea/creatinine increases, while serious adverse events were similar. This small short-term comparison does not define dialysis dosing or demonstrate a mortality benefit. 4Small, short-term comparison; no mortality benefit or dialysis dosing established.
Historical severe-CKD study (1972) 514 nonedematous patients with stable chronic renal failure; CrCl 1.2–12 mL/min.Experimental metolazone exposure.Urine flow and sodium excretion increased; potassium excretion also rose slightly 5.Very small historical study. How to read this evidence

  • Zaroxolyn-type and Mykrox-type formulations must not be interchanged at equivalent doses. The US formulation described here does not establish bioequivalence or dosing instructions for Dega Metoz. 2
  • Accumulation may occur in severe renal impairment. Discontinue if azotemia and oliguria worsen. The overdose section states dialysis is unlikely to be effective; it supplies no chronic HD, PD or CRRT regimen. 2

Mechanism of action

Inhibits renal tubular sodium reabsorption, predominantly at the cortical diluting site.

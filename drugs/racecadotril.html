Dosing: Adult

  • Acute diarrhea in adults (with rehydration): 100 mg once, then 100 mg three times daily before main meals; stop after two normal stools; maximum 7 days

CKD · dialysis · CRRT

Dosing: Altered kidney function

Racecadotril is an enkephalinase inhibitor for acute diarrhea, converted to the active metabolite thiorphan; racecadotril is eliminated mainly in urine as metabolites (81.4% of the dose; SmPC 5.2). The Irish label has limited data in kidney impairment and advises caution without a dose change; courses are limited to 7 days. Combining it with ACE inhibitors raises the risk of angioedema. It has no US FDA label.

Kidney function measure: The SmPC does not grade kidney impairment; bands shown on creatinine clearance (CrCl).

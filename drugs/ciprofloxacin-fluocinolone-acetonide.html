    CrCl ≥60 mL/min
    Usual dosing (label: CrCl >50).
    CrCl 30–59 mL/min
    Oral: CrCl >50, usual dosing; CrCl 30–50, 250 to 500 mg every 12 hours (label). IV: CrCl >30, usual dose (IV label).
    CrCl 15–29 mL/min
    Oral: CrCl 5–29, 250 to 500 mg every 18 hours (label); severe infection: a 750 mg unit dose may be given at these intervals with careful monitoring. IV: CrCl 5–29, 200 to 400 mg every 18 to 24 hours (IV label).
    CrCl <15 mL/min (not on dialysis)
    Oral: CrCl 5–29, 250 to 500 mg every 18 hours (label). IV: CrCl 5–29, 200 to 400 mg every 18 to 24 hours (IV label). Below 5 not on dialysis: not addressed; specialist input.
    Hemodialysis (intermittent)
    Oral: 250 to 500 mg every 24 hours, after dialysis (label). IV: dialysis not addressed in the IV label; specialist input.
    Peritoneal dialysis
    Oral: 250 to 500 mg every 24 hours (the label groups hemodialysis and peritoneal dialysis). IV: dialysis not addressed in the IV label; specialist input. For PD-associated peritonitis (ISPD 2022): intraperitoneal continuous maintenance 50 mg/L (no intermittent IP data); systemic oral 500–750 mg daily (750 mg twice daily for CCPD) (ISPD Table 6).
    CRRT
    Not in label. Published CRRT regimens: 200–400 mg IV every 8–12 hours by effluent rate (Li 2020); specialist input.
    • Dialyzable? Minimal: less than 10% removed by hemodialysis or peritoneal dialysis (label).
    • Monitoring Tendon rupture, QT prolongation, dysglycemia, central nervous system effects; crystalluria (keep hydrated).

    From the Ciprofloxacin monograph (renalcarematters.com, reviewed).

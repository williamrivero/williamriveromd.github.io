Contraindications

  • CrCl 59 mL/min or less
  • History of NSAID-related gastrointestinal bleeding, perforation or ulcer; active peptic ulcer
  • Severe heart failure
  • Severe hepatic impairment (Child-Pugh 10–15)
  • Bleeding disorders; inflammatory bowel disease
  • Severe dehydration
  • Third trimester of pregnancy; breastfeeding
  • NSAID hypersensitivity (asthma, urticaria)
renalcarematters.com, reviewed

Dosing: Adult

  • Acute mild to moderate pain (adults): 25 mg every 8 hours; maximum 75 mg/day; shortest duration
  • Older adults, mild–moderate hepatic impairment, CrCl 60–89: Start at 50 mg/day total
  • Acute moderate to severe pain, injection (IV or IM): 50 mg every 8 to 12 hours (may repeat after 6 hours if needed); maximum 150 mg/day; no more than 2 days. CrCl 60–89: 50 mg/day total; CrCl ≤59: contraindicated

Dosing: Altered kidney function

Dexketoprofen is a nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drug (NSAID) excreted mainly in urine as glucuronide. NSAIDs can cause fluid retention, hyperkalemia and acute kidney injury. The Irish label starts at 50 mg/day in mild kidney impairment (creatinine clearance [CrCl] 60–89 mL/min) and contraindicates it at CrCl 59 mL/min or below. It has no US FDA label.

Kidney function measure: Irish summary of product characteristics (SmPC) uses creatinine clearance (CrCl, mL/min); thresholds 89 and 59.

Kidney injury risk

Reduced kidney blood flow
  • Highest risk with volume depletion, heart failure, liver disease, older age, and use with diuretics, ACE inhibitors or ARBs.

Class warning for NSAIDs (US NSAID labels); this drug has no US label of its own.

Renal Toxicity and Hyperkalemia Renal Toxicity Long-term administration of NSAIDs has resulted in renal papillary necrosis and other renal injury.US label: Naproxen (Zameer Pharmaceuticals LLC), Warnings and precautions

This drug has no US label of its own; the warning is the class statement in the US labels of this drug class.

Adverse reactions

Common

  • Nausea, vomiting
  • Abdominal pain, diarrhea, dyspepsia

Serious

  • Peptic ulcer, gastrointestinal bleeding or perforation
  • Acute kidney failure, nephritis, nephrotic syndrome
  • Anaphylaxis
  • Stevens-Johnson syndrome, toxic epidermal necrolysis
  • Neutropenia, thrombocytopenia

Contraindications

  • US spinal-label reference: Bier block; septicemia; severe hemorrhage, severe hypotension or shock; clinically significant arrhythmias such as complete heart block; amide-local-anesthetic/component hypersensitivity; local infection at lumbar-puncture site 2
renalcarematters.com, reviewed

Dosing: Adult

  • Product-specific spinal anesthesia: No single numerical regimen is assigned across these Philippine products. The US comparator is 7.5 mg/mL with 82.5 mg/mL dextrose, whereas these registry records generally describe 5 mg/mL preparations; US milliliter schedules are not transferred 2,3

Adverse reactions

Common

Serious

  • Systemic CNS/cardiovascular toxicity, respiratory arrest, methemoglobinemia 2

Mechanism of action

Bupivacaine blocks nerve-impulse conduction; dextrose changes solution baricity.

Class: Hyperbaric spinal local anesthetic; amide type

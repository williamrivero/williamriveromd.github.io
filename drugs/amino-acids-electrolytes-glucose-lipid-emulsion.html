Kidney notes: SmofKabiven Central

SmofKabiven Central (Fresenius Kabi AB) is a three-chamber bag of amino acids with electrolytes, glucose and SMOFlipid (soybean oil, medium-chain triglycerides, olive oil and fish oil) for central-vein parenteral nutrition. The Philippine registration is the 1970 ml bag. It is a standard formula containing potassium, phosphate and magnesium.

Dose and rate limits

  • Infusion rate should not exceed 2.0 ml/kg body weight per hour; maximum daily dose 35 ml/kg body weight per day (SmPC 4.2).

Kidney notes

  • Contraindicated in "severe renal insufficiency without access to hemofiltration or dialysis" and with pathologically elevated serum levels of any of the included electrolytes (SmPC 4.3).
  • In patients with renal insufficiency, phosphate and potassium intake should be carefully controlled to prevent hyperphosphatemia and hyperkalemia (SmPC 4.4).
  • Give with caution in impaired lipid metabolism, which may occur in renal failure, and to patients with a tendency towards electrolyte retention; correct fluid and electrolyte disturbances before starting (SmPC 4.4).
  • The lipid emulsion (SMOFlipid) contains fish oil; see ESPEN Recommendation 27 on omega-3-enriched PN in kidney disease.

How it fits the ESPEN 2021 kidney nutrition guideline

  • ESPEN 2021 Recommendation 18 (Grade of recommendation 0 – Consensus (82.6% agreement)): protein intakes that may be prescribed are 0.6–0.8 g/kg body weight (BW)/d for a hospitalized patient with CKD without acute/critical illness; 1.2 g/kg BW/d with CKD and KF on conventional intermittent chronic KRT without acute/critical illness; 0.8–1.0 g/kg BW/d with AKI or AKI on CKD without acute/critical illness; start with 1 g/kg BW/day and gradually increase up to 1.3 g/kg BW/d if tolerated with acute/critical illness not on KRT; 1.3–1.5 g/kg/d for critically ill patients on conventional intermittent KRT; and 1.5 g/kg/d up to 1.7 g/kg/d for critically ill patients on continuous KRT (CKRT) or prolonged intermittent KRT (PIKRT). "If available, the pre-hospitalization body weight or usual body weight may be preferred over the ideal BW. Actual BW should not be considered for a protein prescription." (AKI, acute kidney injury.)
  • ESPEN 2021 Recommendation 25: "The choice of the most appropriate EN or PN formula should be made based on the calorie and protein ratio to provide the most accurate dosing in clinical practice." Grade of recommendation B – Strong consensus (91.3% agreement). (PN, parenteral nutrition.)
  • ESPEN 2021 Recommendation 24: "No disease-specific enteral nor parenteral formula oriented for patients with reduced kidney function should be routinely utilized in every patient with AKI, AKI on CKD, or CKD with KF in comparison to conventional formulas. Instead, their use is to be individualized (see recommendation 26)." Grade of recommendation B – Consensus (87.5% agreement).
  • ESPEN 2021 Recommendation 26: "In selected patients with electrolyte and fluid imbalances, concentrated “renal” EN or PN formulas with lower electrolyte content may be preferred over standard formulas." Grade of recommendation GPP – Strong consensus (95.7% agreement). (GPP, good practice point.)
  • ESPEN 2021 Recommendation 31: "Electrolytes abnormalities are common in patients with AKI, AKI on CKD, or CKD with KF receiving KRT and shall be closely monitored." Grade of recommendation A – Strong consensus (100% agreement).
  • ESPEN 2021 Recommendation 29: "Serum glucose levels shall be maintained between 140–180 mg/dl in hospitalized patients with AKI, AKI on CKD, or CKD with KF." Grade of recommendation A – Strong consensus (95.5% agreement). Recommendation 30: "Tight glucose control (80–110 mg/dl) shall not be pursued because of the increased risk of hypoglycemia." Grade of recommendation A – Strong consensus (100% agreement).
  • ESPEN 2021 Recommendation 14: "For patients undergoing KRT, the total energy provision by additional calories given in the form of citrate, lactate, and glucose from dialysis/hemofiltration solutions should be included in the calculations to determine the total daily energy provision to avoid overfeeding." Grade of recommendation B – Strong consensus (100% agreement). (KRT, kidney replacement therapy.)
  • ESPEN 2021 Recommendation 4: "Intradialytic parenteral nutrition (IDPN) shall be applied in malnourished non-critically ill hospitalized patients with CKD and KF on hemodialysis, or the same patients if at risk of malnutrition that fail to respond or do not tolerate ONS or EN." Grade of recommendation A – Strong consensus (91.7% agreement). (CKD, chronic kidney disease; KF, kidney failure; ONS, oral nutritional supplements; EN, enteral nutrition.)
  • ESPEN 2021 Recommendation 27: "There is not enough evidence to support the routine use of omega-3 polyunsaturated fatty acids (PUFA) supplements or PN solutions enriched with omega-3 PUFA in hospitalized patients with AKI, AKI on CKD or CKD with KF." Grade of recommendation GPP – Strong consensus (95.8% agreement).

Bottom line

A standard central three-chamber bag with a mixed-oil lipid. In severe renal insufficiency it needs dialysis or hemofiltration access, and its 60 mmol potassium and 25 mmol phosphate per 1970 ml bag must be counted. Choose by calorie-to-protein ratio, not by brand, and monitor electrolytes closely.

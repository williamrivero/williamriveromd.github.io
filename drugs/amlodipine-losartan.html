Contraindications

  • CrCl below 30 mL/min or dialysis
  • Hepatic impairment
  • Bilateral renal artery stenosis or stenosis to a single kidney
  • Angioedema with ACE inhibitors or ARBs; hereditary or idiopathic angioedema
  • Pregnancy and breastfeeding
  • Aliskiren; potassium-sparing diuretics; lithium
  • Aortic stenosis, hypertrophic obstructive cardiomyopathy
  • Porphyria
  • Hypersensitivity

Dosing: Altered kidney function

This fixed combination replaces the two drugs in patients already controlled on the same doses. Losartan exposure is unchanged above creatinine clearance (CrCl) 10 mL/min and amlodipine is unaffected by kidney function, but angiotensin-receptor blockade can cause hyperkalemia and acute kidney injury. The South African label needs no change at CrCl 30–50 mL/min and contraindicates the combination below CrCl 30 mL/min and in dialysis; the separate drugs can still be used. It has no US FDA label.

Kidney function measure: South African professional information (SAHPRA) uses creatinine clearance (CrCl, mL/min).

Adverse reactions

Common

  • Dizziness
  • Headache
  • Peripheral edema, flushing, palpitations (uncommon)

Serious

  • Angioedema, including intestinal angioedema
  • Hyperkalemia
  • Kidney deterioration in susceptible patients
  • Hypotension in volume depletion

Registered with the Philippine FDA as a FOOD SUPPLEMENT — no approved therapeutic claims. Not a substitute for prescribed treatment.

Foods for special medical purposes (FSMP) carry a different registration: for the dietary management of patients under medical supervision. Foods for special dietary use (FSDU) are foods formulated for a particular dietary need, such as diabetes or dialysis; not medicines. Food products registered under neither heading and not as food supplements (milk supplements, adult nutrition drinks, ready-to-use supplementary foods) are marked "Other food registration".

    Renal nutrition formulas

    Registered as foods for special medical purposes (FSMP) or, where marked, foods for special dietary use (FSDU), for kidney disease or dialysis. LP marks the lower-protein version (CKD not on dialysis) and HP the higher-protein version (dialysis), as registered. Check the product label for protein, potassium, phosphorus and fluid per serving.

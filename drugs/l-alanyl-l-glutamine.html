Kidney notes: Dipeptiven

Dipeptiven (Fresenius Kabi Austria GmbH) is a concentrated N(2)-L-alanyl-L-glutamine solution, a glutamine dipeptide added to parenteral or enteral nutrition in hypercatabolic or hypermetabolic states. Philippine registration: 100 ml glass bottles.

Composition

Per presentationper 100 ml
N(2)-L-alanyl-L-glutamine (g)20
L-alanine (g)8.2
L-glutamine (g)13.46
  • Theoretical osmolarity 921 mOsmol/l; pH 5.4 to 6.0 (SmPC). These amounts match the Philippine registration (20 g/100 ml, equivalent to L-alanine 8.2 g and L-glutamine 13.46 g).

Composition from the cited manufacturer SmPC/package insert; values independently re-checked November 17, 2026.

Dose and rate limits

  • 0.3 to 0.5 g alanyl-glutamine/kg body weight per day (1.5 to 2.5 ml/kg); maximum 2.5 ml/kg/day; must not exceed 30% of total amino acid/protein supply; not more than 3 weeks (SmPC 4.2).

Kidney notes

  • Contraindicated in severe renal insufficiency (creatinine clearance below 25 ml/min), severe hepatic insufficiency, circulatory shock, hypoxia, multiple organ failure and severe metabolic acidosis (SmPC 4.3).
  • Monitor serum electrolytes, serum osmolality, fluid balance, acid-base balance, creatinine clearance, urea and liver function tests (SmPC 4.4).

How it fits the ESPEN 2021 kidney nutrition guideline

  • ESPEN 2021 Recommendation 28: "In critically ill patients with AKI, AKI on CKD, or CKD with KF, additional high dose parenteral glutamine shall not be administered." Grade of recommendation A – Strong consensus (100% agreement).

Bottom line

Do not use in critically ill patients with AKI (acute kidney injury), AKI on CKD (chronic kidney disease) or CKD with kidney failure (ESPEN Rec 28, Grade A); outside critical illness, the SmPC contraindicates it when creatinine clearance is below 25 ml/min.

Use

  • Stable angina pectoris, as add-on therapy when symptoms are not controlled by, or the patient cannot tolerate, first-line antianginal drugs (beta-blockers, calcium channel blockers, nitrates)
  • Its benefit on long-term mortality and morbidity in ischemic heart disease and heart failure has not been confirmed in adequately designed randomized trials

Dosing: Altered kidney function

Trimetazidine is cleared mainly by the kidneys, so exposure rises as kidney function falls. Since the 2012 European Medicines Agency (EMA) review it is contraindicated in severe renal impairment (creatinine clearance [CrCl] <30 mL/min), which includes end-stage kidney disease (ESKD) and dialysis, and the dose is halved at CrCl 30–60 mL/min. It is not sold in the US, so there is no US FDA label.

Kidney function measure: European summary of product characteristics (SmPC) uses creatinine clearance (CrCl, mL/min; Cockcroft–Gault).

CrCl ≥60 mL/min
Usual dose: 35 mg modified-release (MR) twice daily with meals, or 20 mg immediate-release three times daily. Add-on therapy for stable angina only; reassess at 3 months and stop if no response (EMA 2012).
CrCl 30–59 mL/min
Moderate impairment (SmPC: CrCl 30–60): 35 mg MR once daily in the morning with breakfast, or 20 mg immediate-release twice daily. Older adults: dose by CrCl, titrate carefully (EMA 2012).
CrCl 15–29 mL/min
Contraindicated: severe renal impairment (CrCl <30 mL/min) (EMA 2012).
CrCl <15 mL/min (not on dialysis)
Contraindicated: severe renal impairment (CrCl <30 mL/min) (EMA 2012).
Hemodialysis (intermittent)
Contraindicated (severe renal impairment, CrCl <30 mL/min; EMA 2012). Stop it in patients starting dialysis.
Peritoneal dialysis
Contraindicated (severe renal impairment, CrCl <30 mL/min; EMA 2012).
CRRT
Contraindicated (severe renal impairment; EMA 2012).
  • Dialyzable? Not established; irrelevant in practice because the drug is contraindicated in kidney failure.
  • Monitoring Check CrCl before starting and periodically. Watch for parkinsonian symptoms, tremor, restless legs, gait instability and falls; stop trimetazidine permanently if they appear (EMA 2012). Also contraindicated in Parkinson disease and other movement disorders.

Monitoring parameters

  • Check CrCl before starting and periodically. Watch for parkinsonian symptoms, tremor, restless legs, gait instability and falls; stop trimetazidine permanently if they appear (EMA 2012). Also contraindicated in Parkinson disease and other movement disorders. Kidney

Mechanism of action

Trimetazidine competitively inhibits the terminal enzyme of mitochondrial fatty acid beta-oxidation, shifting heart and muscle energy production from free fatty acids toward glucose oxidation, which uses less oxygen per unit of ATP. Its anti-ischemic effect occurs without changes in heart rate or blood pressure.

Class: Metabolic anti-ischemic agent (partial inhibitor of fatty acid oxidation)

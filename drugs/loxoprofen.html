Clinical evidence and practical considerations

Clinical evidence and kidney considerations

The Japanese PMDA-hosted April 2026 oral 60 mg OHA tablet insert lists severe renal impairment among its contraindications, alongside severe hepatic impairment, severe cardiac failure and active peptic ulcer. This product-specific Japanese reference should not be converted into an unverified Philippine dosing schedule. [Japanese oral loxoprofen insert]

A published case described a 76-year-old woman with biopsy findings suggesting interstitial nephritis and minimal-change disease. Symptoms resolved after withdrawal of oral NSAIDs, then nephrotic-range proteinuria recurred after a loxoprofen patch and improved again after patch withdrawal. [Loxoprofen kidney case report]

This single case cannot estimate incidence or prove comparative safety, but it demonstrates why topical exposure cannot be assumed to eliminate kidney risk. No HD, PD or CRRT regimen is established by these sources. [Loxoprofen kidney case report]

Dosing: Adult

  • Muscle tone improvement (neck-shoulder syndrome, low back pain) and spastic paralysis: 150 mg/day in 3 divided doses after meals; adjust for age and symptoms

CKD · dialysis · CRRT

Dosing: Altered kidney function

Eperisone is a centrally acting muscle relaxant with a short half-life (about 1.7 hours). The Japanese package insert contains no kidney-impairment guidance or kidney pharmacokinetic data; it advises dose reduction and caution in older adults. It has no US FDA label.

Kidney function measure: The Japanese package insert does not address kidney impairment; bands shown on creatinine clearance (CrCl).

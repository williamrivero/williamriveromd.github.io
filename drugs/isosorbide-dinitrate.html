Contraindications

  • Hypersensitivity; concomitant PDE5 inhibitors or riociguat 2.
renalcarematters.com, reviewed

Dosing: Adult

  • US oral immediate-release reference: Start 5–20 mg two or three times daily; maintenance 10–40 mg two or three times daily, with at least a 14-hour daily dose-free interval 2.
renalcarematters.com, reviewed

Oral immediate-release reference only. Sublingual, sustained-release and IV products require separate instructions. Dinitrate and mononitrate are not interchangeable dosing headings 2.

Adverse reactions

Common

Serious

Monitoring parameters

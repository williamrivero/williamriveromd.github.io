CKD · dialysis · CRRT

Dosing: Altered kidney function

Kidney dosing follows the levocetirizine component. Levocetirizine is contraindicated in end-stage kidney disease and in patients on hemodialysis, so this combination should not be used in dialysis patients. Adult tablets carry 5 mg levocetirizine with montelukast 10 mg (chewable tablets 2.5 or 5 mg levocetirizine); the 2.5 mg regimens the label gives for CrCl 10 to 80 mL/min cannot be given with them, so use single-ingredient levocetirizine at the label dose and montelukast separately. Montelukast itself needs no dose adjustment in renal insufficiency (Singulair label).

Kidney function measure: Label uses creatinine clearance (CrCl, mL/min; Cockcroft–Gault).

Dosing: Pediatric

Approved ages
Children 6 months and older (label).
Usual dose
Ages 6 to 11: 2.5 mg once daily in the evening (do not exceed). Ages 6 months to 5 years: 1.25 mg once daily (do not exceed). Ages 12 and older: adult dosing (label).
Maximum dose
2.5 mg/day (6 to 11 years); 1.25 mg/day (6 months to 5 years) (label).

Kidney dosing in children: see Dosing: Altered kidney function.

Contraindications

  • Hypersensitivity to sulbactam, cefoperazone or other beta-lactams

Dosing: Adult

  • Susceptible infections (adults, 1:1 product): 2 to 4 g/day IV or IM in divided doses every 12 hours; up to 8 g/day (sulbactam maximum 4 g/day)
renalcarematters.com, reviewed
CKD · dialysis · CRRT

Dosing: Altered kidney function

Sulbactam is cleared by the kidneys (about 84% in urine; half-life 6.9 to 9.7 hours in anephric patients), whereas cefoperazone is mainly excreted in bile. The label therefore caps sulbactam at 1 g every 12 hours at creatinine clearance (CrCl) 15–30 mL/min and 500 mg every 12 hours below 15, allowing extra cefoperazone alone for severe infections, and doses after dialysis. It has no US FDA label.

CrCl 15–30: maximum sulbactam 1 g every 12 hours (2 g/day of the 1:1 product). Extra cefoperazone may be given separately for severe infection (SmPC).
CrCl <15 mL/min (not on dialysis)
CrCl <15: maximum sulbactam 500 mg every 12 hours (1 g/day of the 1:1 product). Extra cefoperazone may be given separately (SmPC).
Hemodialysis (intermittent)
Dose as for CrCl <15 (sulbactam maximum 500 mg every 12 hours); give the dose after dialysis, because hemodialysis markedly alters sulbactam kinetics (SmPC).
Peritoneal dialysis
No PD-specific data; dose as for CrCl <15.
CRRT
No data in the SmPC. Specialist input.
  • Dialyzable? Sulbactam: yes (hemodialysis significantly changes its clearance). Cefoperazone: half-life only slightly reduced (SmPC).
  • Monitoring Prothrombin time and bleeding (vitamin K deficiency; fatal hemorrhage reported), especially with poor intake, malabsorption, prolonged IV feeding or anticoagulants; give vitamin K if needed. Avoid alcohol during and for 5 days after. Kidney function with aminoglycosides. In combined hepatic and kidney impairment, cefoperazone no more than 2 g/day without level monitoring (SmPC). Watch for confusion, myoclonus or seizures in kidney failure (cephalosporin neurotoxicity); consider vitamin K and check INR in dialysis patients with poor intake.

Dosing: Pediatric

Approved ages
Children, including neonates (limited data in premature infants) (SmPC).
Usual dose
40 to 80 mg/kg/day of the 1:1 combination in 2 to 4 divided doses; up to 160 mg/kg/day in serious infections; maximum sulbactam 80 mg/kg/day. First-week neonates: every 12 hours (SmPC).

Kidney dosing in children: see Dosing: Altered kidney function.

  • Anaphylaxis
  • Stevens-Johnson syndrome, toxic epidermal necrolysis

Pharmacokinetics

Key PK parameters

Half-lifeSulbactam about 1 hour; cefoperazone about 1.7 hours
Half-life in kidney failure (ESKD) kidneySulbactam 6.9 to 9.7 hours in anephric patients; cefoperazone unchanged (prolonged 2- to 4-fold in severe liver disease)
Renal excretion kidneySulbactam about 84% in urine; cefoperazone about 25% (rest biliary)
Dialyzability kidneySulbactam yes; cefoperazone slightly

1988 Convention Table I (norephedrine); FDA-registered oral preparations are exempt from all DDB regulatory requirements except registration and licensing of the importer, exporter and manufacturer; prescription and dispensing follow FDA rules (BR 1 s.2014 Sec. 4(5)).

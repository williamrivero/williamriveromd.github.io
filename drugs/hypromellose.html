Warnings/Precautions

Warnings and precautions (US label) US FDA label

Warnings For external use only. Do not use if solution changes color or becomes cloudy. When using this product do not touch tip of container to any surface to avoid contamination. replace cap after using. Stop use and ask a doctor if you experience eye pain, changes in vision, continued redness or irritation of the eye. condition worsens or persists for more than 72 hours. Keep out of reach of children. If swallowed, get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center right away.

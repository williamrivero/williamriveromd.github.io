Dosing: Adult

  • Susceptible bacterial infections (adults): 375 to 750 mg twice daily; usually 5 to 14 days, continuing 48 hours after symptoms resolve; at least 10 days for hemolytic streptococcal infections
  • Uncomplicated gonorrhea: 2.25 g (six 375 mg tablets) as a single dose with probenecid 1 g

CKD · dialysis · CRRT

Dosing: Altered kidney function

Sultamicillin is an oral prodrug of ampicillin and sulbactam, both cleared by the kidneys. The Singapore product information lowers dosing frequency when creatinine clearance (CrCl) is 30 mL/min or less, following usual ampicillin practice, but gives no specific interval or dialysis guidance. It has no US FDA label.

Kidney function measure: Singapore product information uses creatinine clearance (CrCl, mL/min); single threshold ≤30.

CrCl ≥60 mL/min
Usual: 375 to 750 mg orally twice daily (Singapore NDF).
CrCl 30–59 mL/min
No adjustment stated above CrCl 30 (Singapore NDF).
CrCl 15–29 mL/min
Label: dose less often at CrCl ≤30 (Singapore NDF). The usual 12-hourly oral schedule already matches the IV label's every-12-hours interval for CrCl 15–29 (US ampicillin-sulbactam label) — no further change (judgment).
CrCl <15 mL/min (not on dialysis)
Once daily (every 24 hours), by analogy with the IV label for CrCl 5–14 (judgment).
Hemodialysis (intermittent)
Once daily, given after dialysis on dialysis days (judgment; ampicillin is removed by hemodialysis and sulbactam probably is).
Peritoneal dialysis
No guidance in the source.
CRRT
No guidance; oral prodrug rarely appropriate in CRRT settings.
  • Dialyzable? Ampicillin yes; sulbactam probably (US ampicillin-sulbactam label, overdosage section).
  • Monitoring Penicillin allergy history; diarrhea (including Clostridioides difficile); kidney function to set dosing frequency.

Dosing: Pediatric

Approved ages
Children (weight-based) (Singapore NDF).
Usual dose
Under 30 kg: 25 to 50 mg/kg/day orally in 2 divided doses. 30 kg or more: adult dose (Singapore NDF).

Kidney dosing in children: see Dosing: Altered kidney function.

Adverse reactions

Common

  • Diarrhea (about 3%), rash (under 2%) (IV ampicillin-sulbactam label; applies to the active moieties)

Serious

  • Serious and fatal hypersensitivity (anaphylaxis)
  • Clostridioides difficile-associated diarrhea
  • Stevens-Johnson syndrome, toxic epidermal necrolysis, DRESS
  • Tubulointerstitial nephritis
  • Hemolytic anemia, thrombocytopenic purpura, agranulocytosis
  • Cholestatic hepatitis
  • Convulsions
renalcarematters.com, reviewed

Monitoring parameters

  • Penicillin allergy history; diarrhea (including Clostridioides difficile); kidney function to set dosing frequency. Kidney
  • Kidney function to set dosing frequency

Pharmacokinetics

Key PK parameters

Half-lifeAbout 1 hour for both ampicillin and sulbactam (IV label; active moieties)
Half-life in kidney failure (ESKD) kidneyAbout 5 hours at CrCl 15–29 and 9 hours at CrCl 5–14 (IV label Table 3)
Protein bindingAmpicillin about 28%; sulbactam about 38% (IV label)
Renal excretion kidneyAbout 75% to 85% of both excreted unchanged in urine within 8 hours (IV label)
Dialyzability kidneyAmpicillin yes; sulbactam probably (IV label)

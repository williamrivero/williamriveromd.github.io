Contraindications

  • Tetracycline/component hypersensitivity; age under 8 years; oral retinoids; pregnancy/lactation (Philippine insert) 3
renalcarematters.com, reviewed

Dosing: Adult

  • Acne—exact Philippine Tetralysal 300 mg capsule: 300 mg/day; usual duration 12 weeks. Capsule: 408 mg lymecycline equivalent to 300 mg tetracycline base 3
  • Other susceptible infections—adult Philippine Tetralysal: 600 mg/day; adequate fluids reduce esophageal injury 3

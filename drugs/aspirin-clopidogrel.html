Note: Fixed-dose combinations may not allow the dose changes advised for each component; check the strength and use the single agents if a component needs adjusting.

Contraindications

  • Severe kidney impairment (CrCl <30 mL/min)
  • Severe hepatic impairment
  • Active pathological bleeding
  • NSAID or aspirin hypersensitivity (asthma, rhinitis, nasal polyps)
  • Mastocytosis
  • Third trimester of pregnancy
  • Hypersensitivity
renalcarematters.com, reviewed

Dosing: Adult

  • Acute coronary syndrome or ST-elevation myocardial infarction in patients already taking both drugs: One tablet once daily (clopidogrel 75 mg/aspirin 75 or 100 mg); duration per indication (up to 12 months after NSTE-ACS or PCI)

CKD · dialysis · CRRT

Dosing: Altered kidney function

The fixed combination of clopidogrel 75 mg with aspirin 75 or 100 mg is contraindicated in Europe in severe kidney impairment (creatinine clearance [CrCl] below 30 mL/min) because of the aspirin component, and experience in mild to moderate impairment is limited. Clopidogrel's antiplatelet effect is about 25% lower at CrCl 5–15 mL/min. It has no US FDA label.

Kidney function measure: European summary of product characteristics (SmPC) uses creatinine clearance (CrCl, mL/min).

Adverse reactions

Common

  • Bleeding, bruising
  • Dyspepsia, diarrhea, abdominal pain

Serious

  • Gastrointestinal and intracranial bleeding
  • Thrombotic thrombocytopenic purpura, aplastic anemia, agranulocytosis (rare)
  • Anaphylaxis, angioedema
  • Acute kidney impairment (rare, mainly with pre-existing dysfunction)
renalcarematters.com, reviewed

Pharmacokinetics

Key PK parameters

Half-lifeClopidogrel about 6 hours (inactive metabolite 8 hours); aspirin 0.3 to 0.4 hours; salicylic acid about 2 hours
Half-life in kidney failure (ESKD) kidneyAt CrCl 5–15, clopidogrel platelet inhibition about 25% lower; bleeding time similar
Metabolism (CYP, transporters)Clopidogrel: prodrug activated partly by CYP2C19; aspirin hydrolyzed to salicylic acid
Active metabolitesClopidogrel thiol metabolite; salicylic acid
Renal excretion kidneyClopidogrel about 50% in urine, 46% in feces (120 hours); salicylate renal excretion depends on urine pH
Dialyzability kidneyNot stated

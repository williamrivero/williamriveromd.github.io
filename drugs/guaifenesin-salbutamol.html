Component information

No US or EU label exists for this exact combination, so information is shown for each component.

  • Guaifenesin

    Use: Uses helps loosen phlegm (mucus) and thin bronchial secretions to rid the bronchial passageways of bothersome mucus and make coughs more productive US FDA label

  • Salbutamol

    Use: INDICATIONS AND USAGE Albuterol Sulfate Syrup is indicated for the relief of bronchospasm in adults and children 2 years of age and older with reversible obstructive airway disease. US FDA label

Note: Fixed-dose combinations may not allow the dose changes advised for each component; check the strength and use the single agents if a component needs adjusting.

Warnings/Precautions

Warnings and precautions (US label) US FDA label

Warnings For external use only. When using this product Avoid getting into the eyes or on mucous membranes. Do not apply to wounds or damaged skin. Do not bandage tightly. Do not use if excessive irritation of the skin develops. Do not use otherwise than as directed. Stop use and ask a doctor if Condition worsens, or if symptoms persist for more than 7 days or clear up and occur again within a few days, discontinue use of this product and consult a doctor.

If pregnant or breast-feeding ask a health professional before use. Keep out of reach of children If swallowed, get medical help or contact a Poison Control Center right away.

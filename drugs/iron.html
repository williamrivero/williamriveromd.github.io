Full indications and usage US FDA label

Use: For temporary relief of exhaustion, muscle weakness, pallor. May also be used for standard homeopathic indications or as directed by your physician.

Dosing: Adult

Dosage and administration (US label) US FDA label

Directions for Use: Place drops under tongue for 30 seconds. Adults 7 drops 3 times a day. Children 2-12 years, 7 drops 3 times a day. Under 2 years, consult your doctor. Use until symptoms are relieved or as directed by your health care provider.

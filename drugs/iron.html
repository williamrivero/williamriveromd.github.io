Clinical evidence and practical considerations

CKD anemia assessment

The KDIGO 2026 anemia synopsis uses ferritin and transferrin saturation to guide iron therapy and prefers intravenous over oral iron in hemodialysis. It also emphasizes correction of treatable causes before erythropoiesis-stimulating therapy. [KDIGO anemia synopsis, 2026]

A registry heading of Iron does not identify an interchangeable medicine or define elemental iron content. Oral and intravenous preparations require their own product information; a universal dose for this heading would be misleading. [PH FDA registry]

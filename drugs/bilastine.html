Dosing: Altered kidney function

Bilastine is a non-sedating antihistamine that is not metabolized; about 28% is excreted unchanged in urine. Exposure rises about 2.3-fold in severe kidney impairment, but the European label needs no dose change in adults. It advises avoiding P-glycoprotein inhibitors (such as ketoconazole, erythromycin, cyclosporine, ritonavir, diltiazem) in moderate or severe kidney impairment. It has no US FDA label.

Kidney function measure: SmPC pharmacokinetic groups use glomerular filtration rate (GFR, mL/min/1.73 m2); no dose adjustment at any level. Bands shown on creatinine clearance (CrCl).

Dosing: Pediatric

Approved ages
2 years and older, weighing at least 15 kg (SmPC).
Usual dose
2 to 11 years (15 kg or more): 10 mg once daily (orodispersible tablet or oral solution). 12 years and older: 20 mg once daily.

Kidney dosing in children: see Dosing: Altered kidney function.

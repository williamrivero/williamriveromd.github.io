    How interactions are graded

    • Contraindicated The source says the combination must not be used.
    • Avoid Avoid the combination; use only with specialist reason and close monitoring.
    • Adjust or monitor Usually usable with a dose change, spacing, or monitoring.
    • Minor Small or uncommon effect; awareness is enough for most patients.

    Each interaction is quoted from a drug monograph reviewed by renalcarematters.com, built on US FDA labels and PubMed-verified literature. Reviewed interaction lists now cover 210 drugs (977 interactions). A pair is found when either drug's list names the other drug or its class, so no result does not mean no interaction; check the full prescribing information.

Kidney injury risk

Contrast-associated kidney injury
  • Check kidney function and hydration before the study; use the lowest dose needed.
Acute Kidney Injury: Acute injury including renal failure can occur.US label: ISOVUE-M (Bracco Diagnostics Inc), Warnings and precautions

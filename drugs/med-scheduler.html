1. The patient's day

Dialysis

By shift: morning session, after lunch; midday or afternoon session, after dinner; evening session, at bedtime. Twice-daily agents (morning session): after lunch and at bedtime. Alpha blockers: bedtime. A time in the sig always wins.

    How the schedule is built

    Meal and time-of-day rules come from each medicine's US label (shown with each placement). Dose spacing, such as levothyroxine 4 hours from phosphate binders, comes from the reviewed interaction lists. Medicines dosed after dialysis follow the reviewed kidney dosing. The scheduler meets every rule it can with the fewest dose times and lists any rule it cannot meet. Medicines with no label timing rule are placed at standard times and can be moved with the timing menu. A few placements follow local practice and are tagged Practice default: statins at bedtime, and blood-pressure medicines at one fixed time every day when the hold toggle is on. Placements tagged Evidence cite the study behind them: oral iron in the morning on an empty stomach, and calcium with meals as a phosphate binder or between meals as a calcium supplement. The sample prescriptions are fictional. Review the schedule before giving it to the patient.

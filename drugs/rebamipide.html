Contraindications

  • Hypersensitivity

Dosing: Adult

  • Gastric ulcer: 100 mg three times daily (morning, evening, bedtime)
  • Gastric mucosal lesions in acute gastritis or acute exacerbation of chronic gastritis: 100 mg three times daily
renalcarematters.com, reviewed
CKD · dialysis · CRRT

Dosing: Altered kidney function

Rebamipide is a gastric mucosal protectant; about 10% of a dose appears in urine. In kidney impairment, single-dose levels are higher and the half-life longer, but the Japanese insert reports no accumulation with repeated dosing in dialysis patients and sets no kidney dose change. It has no US FDA label.

Kidney function measure: The Japanese insert describes kidney impairment and dialysis without numeric thresholds or dose changes; bands shown on creatinine clearance (CrCl).

Adverse reactions

Common

  • Rash, pruritus
  • Constipation, bloating, diarrhea, nausea

Serious

  • Shock, anaphylaxis
  • Leukopenia, thrombocytopenia
  • Hepatic dysfunction, jaundice

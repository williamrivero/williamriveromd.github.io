Dosing: Adult

  • Acute mild to moderate pain (Xefo Rapid tablets; arthritis regimens differ): 8 to 16 mg/day in 8 mg doses; 16 mg then 8 mg after 12 hours on day 1; maximum 16 mg/day thereafter
  • Kidney or hepatic impairment: Reduce to once-daily dosing (avoid at CrCl <30, our judgment)

Dosing: Altered kidney function

Lornoxicam is an oxicam nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drug (NSAID) metabolized by CYP2C9; its pharmacokinetics change little in kidney failure, but NSAID effects on kidney blood flow still apply. The Irish label reduces dosing to once daily in kidney impairment and contraindicates it in severe impairment, defined by serum creatinine above 700 µmol/L. It has no US FDA label.

Kidney function measure: The SmPC grades kidney impairment by serum creatinine: mild 150–300 µmol/L (1.7–3.4 mg/dL), moderate 300–700 µmol/L (3.4–7.9 mg/dL), severe above 700 µmol/L (above 7.9 mg/dL). Bands are mapped to creatinine clearance (CrCl) by judgment; we advise avoiding it below CrCl 30.

CrCl 30–59 mL/min
Kidney impairment: reduce dosing frequency to once daily (SmPC). Use with caution; stop if kidney function worsens.
CrCl 15–29 mL/min
Avoid. The SmPC contraindicates only at serum creatinine above 700 µmol/L (above 7.9 mg/dL), but that threshold is outdated; NSAIDs at CrCl <30 risk acute kidney injury, hyperkalemia and fluid overload (our judgment, consistent with other EU NSAID labels).
CrCl <15 mL/min (not on dialysis)
Avoid (our judgment: CrCl <30). Severe impairment (serum creatinine above 700 µmol/L [above 7.9 mg/dL]) is contraindicated in the SmPC.

Adverse reactions

Common

  • Nausea, abdominal pain, dyspepsia, diarrhea, vomiting
  • Headache, dizziness

Serious

  • Gastrointestinal bleeding and perforation
  • Thrombocytopenia, anemia
  • Acute kidney failure in pre-existing kidney impairment
  • Hepatotoxicity, hepatic failure
  • Stevens-Johnson syndrome, toxic epidermal necrolysis
renalcarematters.com, reviewed

Contraindications

  • Severe kidney impairment (serum creatinine above 700 µmol/L)
  • Severe hepatic impairment
  • Severe heart failure
  • Active or recurrent peptic ulcer or bleeding
  • Thrombocytopenia
  • Third trimester of pregnancy

    eGFR ≥60
    No adjustment (label).
    eGFR 30–59
    No adjustment (label). Monitor for myopathy. KDIGO: atorvastatin 20 mg/day is the studied dose in CKD G3a–G5; higher doses not proven safe in CKD.
    eGFR 15–29
    No adjustment (label). Monitor for myopathy. KDIGO: atorvastatin 20 mg/day is the studied dose in CKD G3a–G5; higher doses not proven safe in CKD.
    eGFR <15 (not on dialysis)
    No adjustment (label). Monitor for myopathy. KDIGO: atorvastatin 20 mg/day is the studied dose in CKD G3a–G5; higher doses not proven safe in CKD.
    Hemodialysis (intermittent)
    No dose adjustment (not dialyzed; label). KDIGO: do not start a statin in most patients already on dialysis; continue if already taking one at dialysis initiation.
    Peritoneal dialysis
    No label adjustment; no PD data.
    CRRT
    No data in label.
    • Dialyzable? Not expected to be significantly removed by hemodialysis (label).
    • Monitoring Muscle symptoms (myopathy, rhabdomyolysis); renal impairment is a risk factor (label).

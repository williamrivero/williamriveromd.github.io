CKD · dialysis · CRRT

Dosing: Altered kidney function

Ketoanalogues of essential amino acids (Ketosteril and similar products) are a kidney-specific therapy: the Spanish label indicates them for adults with chronic kidney failure and glomerular filtration rate (GFR) 25 mL/min/1.73 m2 or less who are on a protein-restricted diet (40 g/day or less). Each tablet carries 50 mg of calcium, so serum calcium must be checked. There is no US FDA label.

Kidney function measure: Spanish summary of product characteristics (ficha técnica) uses GFR in mL/min/1.73 m2 (indication threshold 25).

  • Sore throat (lozenges, 12 years and older): Up to 6 lozenges (20 mg) a day for no more than 3 days

Dosing: Altered kidney function

Ambroxol is cleared mainly by the liver (glucuronidation and CYP3A4), but its metabolites are excreted in urine and are expected to accumulate in severe kidney impairment. European-derived labels give no numeric adjustment; they advise caution with lower doses or longer intervals. It has no US FDA label.

Kidney function measure: The SmPCs grade kidney impairment only in words ('impaired', 'severe'); bands shown on creatinine clearance (CrCl).

Dosing: Pediatric

Approved ages
Syrup: 2 years and older; contraindicated under 2 years. Lozenges: not under 12 years (SmPC).
Usual dose
2 to 5 years: 7.5 mg three times daily (22.5 mg/day); 6 to 12 years: 30 to 45 mg/day in 2 or 3 divided doses; over 12 years: adult dose (syrup SmPC).

Kidney dosing in children: see Dosing: Altered kidney function.

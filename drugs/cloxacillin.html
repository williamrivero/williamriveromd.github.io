Contraindications

  • Allergy to penicillins or cephalosporins
renalcarematters.com, reviewed

Dosing: Adult

  • Staphylococcal and other susceptible infections (adults, oral): 250 to 500 mg every 6 hours, 1 to 2 hours before meals, for at least 5 days; maximum 6,000 mg/day
  • Streptococcus pyogenes infections: Treat for at least 10 days
renalcarematters.com, reviewed
CKD · dialysis · CRRT

Dosing: Altered kidney function

Cloxacillin is a short-acting antistaphylococcal penicillin, 94% protein-bound, with 37% of an oral dose excreted in urine. The Canadian product monograph gives no kidney dose adjustment (half-life about 25 minutes, 49 minutes in uremia) but warns that high levels in kidney failure can cause central nervous system toxicity. It has no current US FDA label. This page covers oral dosing only; intravenous dosing is not covered, and high-dose intravenous use in end-stage kidney disease needs dose reduction with specialist input.

Kidney function measure: The Canadian product monograph does not grade kidney impairment; bands shown on creatinine clearance (CrCl).

Adverse reactions

Common

  • Nausea, vomiting, epigastric discomfort, flatulence, loose stools

Serious

  • Anaphylaxis
  • Severe skin reactions (acute generalized exanthematous pustulosis, DRESS, Stevens-Johnson syndrome, toxic epidermal necrolysis)
  • Neurotoxicity in kidney failure with high levels
  • Leukopenia (rare)
renalcarematters.com, reviewed

Monitoring parameters

  • Kidney, liver and blood counts periodically in long courses; neurotoxicity (myoclonus, seizures) at high levels in kidney failure; severe skin reactions (PM). Kidney
  • Kidney, liver and blood counts in long courses

Pharmacokinetics

Key PK parameters

BioavailabilityRapid but incomplete; food delays absorption
Time to peakAbout 0.9 hour (500 mg capsule, fasting)
Half-lifeAbout 25 minutes (infusion study); about 1.4 hours in an oral comparative bioavailability study (1.36 and 1.45 hours)
Half-life in kidney failure (ESKD) kidney49 minutes in uremic patients
Protein binding94%
Renal excretion kidney37% of an oral dose (62% of an intravenous dose) in urine
Dialyzability kidneyNot stated

Contraindications

  • Hypersensitivity to teicoplanin
renalcarematters.com, reviewed

Dosing: Adult

  • Complicated skin and soft tissue infection, pneumonia, complicated urinary tract infection: Loading 6 mg/kg IV or IM every 12 hours for 3 doses, then 6 mg/kg once daily
  • Bone and joint infection: Loading 12 mg/kg IV every 12 hours for 3 to 5 doses, then 12 mg/kg once daily
  • Infective endocarditis: Loading 12 mg/kg IV every 12 hours for 3 to 5 doses, then 12 mg/kg once daily; usually at least 21 days
  • Clostridioides difficile diarrhea (oral): 100 to 200 mg orally twice daily for 7 to 14 days
renalcarematters.com, reviewed
CKD · dialysis · CRRT

Dosing: Altered kidney function

Teicoplanin is a glycopeptide excreted about 80% unchanged in urine, with a very long terminal half-life (100 to 170 hours). The European label gives the full loading dose, then from day 4 halves the maintenance dose at creatinine clearance (CrCl) 30–80 mL/min and gives one-third at CrCl below 30 mL/min and on hemodialysis, guided by trough levels. Hemodialysis does not remove it. It has no US FDA label.

Kidney function measure: European summary of product characteristics (SmPC) uses creatinine clearance (CrCl, mL/min); thresholds 80 and 30.

CrCl ≥60 mL/min
CrCl >80: usual loading and maintenance doses (e.g., 6 mg/kg every 12 hours for 3 doses, then 6 mg/kg once daily; 12 mg/kg for bone, joint and endocarditis). CrCl 60–80: no change until day 4, then half the maintenance dose (full dose every 2 days or half dose daily) (SmPC).
CrCl 30–59 mL/min
Usual loading doses; from day 4, half the maintenance dose: the full dose every 2 days or half the dose once daily (SmPC: CrCl 30–80). Keep trough at least 15 mg/L (FPIA) or 10 mg/L (HPLC).
CrCl 15–29 mL/min
Usual loading doses; from day 4, one-third of the maintenance dose: the full dose every 3 days or one-third once daily (SmPC: CrCl <30). Guide by trough levels.
CrCl <15 mL/min (not on dialysis)
Usual loading doses; from day 4, one-third of the maintenance dose (full dose every 3 days or one-third daily) (SmPC). Guide by trough levels.
Hemodialysis (intermittent)
As for CrCl <30: usual loading, then one-third of the maintenance dose from day 4 (every third day or one-third daily). Not removed by hemodialysis; no supplement needed (SmPC).
Peritoneal dialysis
CAPD peritonitis: single intravenous loading dose of 6 mg/kg, then 20 mg/L in the dialysate: week 1 in every bag; week 2 in alternate bags; week 3 in the overnight bag only (SmPC). Removed only slowly by peritoneal dialysis.
CRRT
No CRRT dosing in the SmPC. Use trough-guided dosing; specialist input.
  • Dialyzable? No: not removed by hemodialysis; removed only slowly by peritoneal dialysis (SmPC 4.2, 4.9).
  • Monitoring Trough concentrations at steady state after loading and at least weekly during maintenance (trough targets by FPIA: at least 15 mg/L for complicated skin and soft-tissue infection, pneumonia and complicated urinary tract infection; above 20 mg/L for bone and joint infection; 30–40 mg/L after loading and above 30 mg/L during maintenance for endocarditis). In kidney impairment, set the day-4 reduction by trough rather than by CrCl alone. Kidney function closely in CKD, after high loading doses and with nephrotoxic drugs. Hearing tests with ototoxic or nephrotoxic co-medication. Blood counts periodically (SmPC 4.4).

Dosing: Pediatric

Approved ages
All ages, from neonates (SmPC).
Usual dose
Neonates and infants up to 2 months: 16 mg/kg intravenously on day 1, then 8 mg/kg once daily (infusion only). 2 months to 12 years: 10 mg/kg every 12 hours for 3 doses, then 6 to 10 mg/kg once daily. Over 12 years: adult doses (SmPC).

Pediatric half-life about 40 hours in neonates and 58 hours at a mean age of 8 years.

Kidney dosing in children: see Dosing: Altered kidney function.

Adverse reactions

Common

  • Rash, erythema, pruritus
  • Pain, fever

Serious

  • Nephrotoxicity and acute kidney failure (about 2% on standard loading; 11% to 27% with high loading regimens)
  • Ototoxicity: deafness, tinnitus, vestibular disorder
  • Agranulocytosis, neutropenia, thrombocytopenia
  • Anaphylaxis, red man syndrome
  • Stevens-Johnson syndrome, toxic epidermal necrolysis, DRESS, acute generalized exanthematous pustulosis
  • Seizures
renalcarematters.com, reviewed

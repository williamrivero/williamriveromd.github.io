Dosing: Altered kidney function

Citicoline is broken down in the gut wall and liver to choline and cytidine; under 3% of a dose appears in urine and feces, and about 12% leaves as exhaled carbon dioxide. The Spanish label gives no kidney dose adjustment and no dialysis data. It has no US FDA label (sold in the US only as a supplement).

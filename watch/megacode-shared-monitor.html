The shared monitor display: a 1-minute explainer

How the Megacode Practice Lab turns a second screen into a bedside monitor: rhythms, capnography, the defibrillator and rhythm playlists. Free on the Renal Care Academy.

Meet the shared monitor display. It turns any second screen into a bedside monitor for your megacode practice. Open the controls on your laptop, and the display on a tablet beside the manikin. Your controls stay private. The team sees only the monitor. Choose a rhythm, and typical values fill in. Change any number you like, then press Update display. Nothing changes on the monitor until you do. The yellow trace is capnography. During CPR, end-tidal carbon dioxide reflects how well compressions are working, and a sudden rise can mean the heart is beating again. Turn on the defibrillator, and the team can sync, charge and shock. Set the rhythm after the next shock, and the monitor changes on its own. Build a playlist of rhythms, and run the whole scenario with one tap. It is a training display, not a medical device. Now, go practice!

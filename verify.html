Scan the QR code on a certificate of completion, or type its code below.

A Renal Care Academy certificate of completion shows that the learner finished a free self-study course and passed its online end-of-course examination. It is not CPD-accredited and is not a license or a competency sign-off: competence is demonstrated on the learner's own unit and signed off by a preceptor. The examination is taken online without supervision, so this page confirms that the certificate was issued by renalcarematters.com, not who sat the examination.

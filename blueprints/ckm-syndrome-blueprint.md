## Section Blueprint

### §1 — What Is CKM Syndrome? `#what-is-ckm`
- CKM = Cardiovascular-Kidney-Metabolic Syndrome; AHA + ASN formal definition 2023
- Four components: heart disease, kidney disease, type 2 diabetes/metabolic dysfunction, obesity
- Why it matters: biological feedback loops mean each condition worsens the others simultaneously
- Why siloed care fails: treating only kidneys or only heart misses shared pathways
- **Amber callout:** "CKM is not a new disease — it is a new understanding of why so many Filipinos with kidney disease also have uncontrolled diabetes and heart failure."

### §2 — The 5 Stages `#stages`
Stage cards (color-coded, 0–4):
| Stage | Who Fits | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | No risk factors — healthy | Annual screening |
| 1 | Obesity/prediabetes only; no CKD, no CVD | Lifestyle; prevent progression |
| 2 | CKD Stage 1–4 OR DM OR HTN (no CVD yet) | RAAS + SGLT2i; calculate CV risk |
| 3 | CKD + metabolic + PREVENT ≥15% 10-yr CVD risk | Triple therapy; cardiology referral |
| 4 | Established CVD (ASCVD/HF/AF) with CKD/metabolic | Guideline-directed HF/CVD + renal dosing |

"Most Filipino dialysis patients are Stage 4. Most Stage 3 patients don't know it yet."
**Action box:** "Ask your doctor: What is my CKM stage?"

### §3 — Why They Travel Together `#why-together`
Three mechanism panels (simple language):
1. Insulin resistance → glomerular hyperfiltration → proteinuria → CKD (car-engine analogy)
2. Impaired kidneys → fluid retention + uremic toxins → hypertension → LVH → HF
3. Visceral fat → adipokines → systemic inflammation → damages glomeruli AND coronary endothelium simultaneously
**Clinical pearl:** "A CKD patient with type 2 diabetes is 10× more likely to die of a heart attack than of kidney failure itself."

### §4 — Obesity and the Kidneys `#obesity-kidneys`
- Obesity-related glomerulopathy: perinephric fat compression + hyperfiltration
- Even 5–10% weight loss reduces proteinuria (FLOW trial data)
- **Filipino-specific:** Central obesity causes organ damage at BMI 23–25 (Asian BMI threshold)
- Waist circumference cutoffs: Men ≥90 cm / Women ≥80 cm = high cardiorenal risk (DOH PH)
- Asian BMI overweight: ≥23; obese: ≥27.5

### §5 — The Filipino CKM Risk Profile `#filipino-risk`
Stat callouts:
- "1 in 3 Filipino adults has hypertension" (DOH 2021)
- "1 in 6 has diabetes or prediabetes"
- "Most CKD diagnosed at Stage 3–4 — after 50% kidney function lost"
- NKTI 2024: kidney disease top 5 cause of death; 80,000+ on dialysis → projected 150,000 by 2030
- Filipino dietary amplifiers: high-Na (patis/bagoong/instant noodles), high-refined carb (3× white rice/day), low-fiber
- BHC gap: urinalysis + FBS available at RHU level but rarely integrated with waist + BP into unified risk score

### §6 — Your CKM Treatment Team `#treatment-team`
Team diagram: patient at center, 5 roles:
1. Nephrologist — eGFR/proteinuria, RAAS/SGLT2i, ESRD planning
2. Cardiologist — HF/ASCVD/AF management, fluid targets
3. Endocrinologist — glucose, GLP-1/insulin titration, metabolic targets
4. Nutritionist-Dietitian — simultaneous CKM diet (low-K + low-P + low-Na + low-refined carb)
5. Primary Care — referral coordination, family screening
**Filipino reality note:** "In many Philippine settings, your nephrologist functions as all five. Bring this guide and ask: 'Am I being managed for all components of CKM?'"

### §7 — Triple Therapy: The New Standard `#triple-therapy`
Three drug-class cards:
| Class | Examples | Mechanism | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| RAAS blockade | Losartan, Enalapril | ↓intraglomerular pressure, proteinuria, BP | KDIGO 2024 Grade 1A foundation |
| SGLT2 inhibitor | Empagliflozin, Dapagliflozin | Kidney + heart protection; ↓HF hospitalization | KDIGO 2024 Grade 1A add-on |
| Finerenone | Kerendia® | ↓CKD progression + HF in DKD; anti-fibrotic | KDIGO 2024 Grade 1B with DM+CKD |
| GLP-1 agonist | Semaglutide (Ozempic®) | FLOW 2024: 24% ↓kidney events, 29% ↓CV death | KDIGO 2024 Grade 2A with DM+CVD |

PH availability: Empagliflozin (Jardiance® + generics entering), Dapagliflozin (Forxiga®), Finerenone (Kerendia® — specialty pharmacy), Semaglutide (Ozempic® — currently out-of-pocket)
**Key patient message:** "If you have CKD + DM + HTN, ask: 'Should I be on all three? What is stopping me?'"

Contraindication callouts:
- eGFR <20: SGLT2i usually stopped
- K+ >5.5: finerenone dose reduction
- eGFR <25: semaglutide requires specialist review

### §8 — Red Flags `#red-flags`
CKM-specific warnings:
- Weight gain >2 kg in 2 days → fluid retention, early HF decompensation
- Chest tightness on mild exertion → new cardiac event
- Ankle swelling + ↓urine output same day → combined cardiorenal decompensation
- BG >16 mmol/L with nausea on SGLT2i → euglycemic DKA risk
- Dizziness on standing after new BP med → orthostatic hypotension (CKD Stage 4–5)
- Palpitations + irregular heartbeat → atrial fibrillation (CKD = 2–4× increased AF risk)

### §9 — FAQ `#faq` (8 questions)
1. CKD + DM + HTN = CKM? → Yes, Stage 2–3 minimum
2. CKM vs Metabolic Syndrome? → Metabolic Syndrome is Stage 1 of CKM; CKM adds CVD + CKD
3. Can I have CKM without being obese? → Yes — Stage 2–4 doesn't require obesity
4. My cardiologist says kidneys fine; nephrologist says heart fine — who's right? → Both are missing the integration; ask for unified CKM risk assessment
5. Can CKM be reversed? → Stages 0–2 significantly reversible; Stages 3–4 focus on slowing + preventing events
6. Are SGLT2i/finerenone available in PH? → Yes; list PhilHealth coverage status
7. Should my children be screened? → Yes, annual BP/FBS/urinalysis from age 18 if 1st-degree relative with CKM 2–4
8. Do I need three doctors? → Ideally yes; in PH your nephrologist can coordinate all three aspects

## Clinician Section (Physician Mode)

### MD §1 — AHA-ASN Staging Criteria
Full PREVENT equation integration; eGFR + UACR as first-class staging inputs; distinction from Ronco CRS classification

### MD §2 — Pathophysiology
RAAS loop; adipokine profile (leptin resistance, adiponectin deficiency); FGF-23/Klotho axis; uremic toxin CV toxicity (indoxyl sulfate, p-cresyl sulfate, TMAO); neurohormonal activation (SNS, aldosterone, endothelin)

### MD §3 — PREVENT Risk Calculator
Replaces Pooled Cohort Equations; eGFR + UACR incorporated; ACC/AHA online tool; CKM-adjusted thresholds for statin + SGLT2i initiation

### MD §4 — Treatment Algorithm
Flowchart: Stage 0–1 → lifestyle + screening → Stage 2 → RAAS + SGLT2i ± finerenone → Stage 3 → add GLP-1, intensify CVD reduction → Stage 4 → guideline-directed HF/ASCVD + renal-adjusted dosing
Drug monitoring: eGFR q3m on SGLT2i start; K+ q2w on finerenone start

### MD §5 — Philippine Context
PSN/PSC joint statement status 2025; PhilHealth formulary coverage per drug class; NKTI CKM screening program; referral triggers for nephrology-cardiology co-management

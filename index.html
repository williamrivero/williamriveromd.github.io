Evidence Basis

Grounded in current clinical guidelines

Every recommendation in this library traces back to one of these bodies — click to see why each one matters to kidney care.

KDIGO Kidney Disease: Improving Global Outcomes

An international nonprofit that publishes the global consensus guidelines defining how kidney disease is diagnosed, staged, and managed.

ADA American Diabetes Association

Publishes annual Standards of Care setting the benchmark for managing diabetes, the leading driver of chronic kidney disease worldwide.

ACC/AHA American College of Cardiology / American Heart Association

Jointly issue cardiovascular guidelines essential to kidney care given how closely heart and kidney disease progress together.

KDOQI Kidney Disease Outcomes Quality Initiative

The National Kidney Foundation's initiative producing US-focused, practice-level guidelines for evaluating and treating kidney disease.

Why I Built This
William Gregory M. Rivero, MD
FPCP · DPSN · Nephrology, Internal Medicine & Clinical Nutrition

I'm a nephrologist in daily practice, and I kept meeting patients who left the clinic frightened and under-informed — not because they weren't smart, but because no one had the time to explain. This library is my attempt to give that time back: honest answers, in plain language, in the language you think in. New guides go up regularly. If you spot an error or want to reach out, I'd genuinely like to hear from you.

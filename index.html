About

A physician who listens, thinks, and cares

Dr. William Gregory M. Rivero is a specialist in Internal Medicine, Nephrology, and Clinical Nutrition with over a decade of experience caring for patients with complex chronic conditions across Metro Manila, Pampanga, and Bulacan.

His practice integrates evidence-based medicine with functional and integrative approaches — recognizing that kidney disease, diabetes, hypertension, and metabolic disorders are deeply interconnected and require a whole-person strategy.

Dr. Rivero is committed to clear communication and patient education, ensuring that every patient and family understands their condition, their medicines, and their path forward.

Specialties

Areas of expertise

Comprehensive care across three interconnected disciplines, grounded in physiology and delivered with compassion.

🦔

Nephrology

Diagnosis and management of kidney diseases with close attention to slowing progression and preserving quality of life.

  • Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD)
  • Diabetic Nephropathy
  • Hypertensive Nephropathy
  • Proteinuria & Glomerular Disease
  • Dialysis Management
  • Post-Transplant Care
❤️

Internal Medicine

Comprehensive adult medicine with a focus on cardiometabolic and multi-organ conditions.

  • Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus
  • Hypertension
  • Atrial Fibrillation
  • Dyslipidemia
  • Gout & Hyperuricemia
  • Infectious Disease Management
🌿

Clinical Nutrition

Therapeutic nutrition tailored to kidney disease, diabetes, and cardiometabolic health — practical, culturally relevant, and sustainable.

  • Renal Diet Planning
  • Diabetic Meal Planning
  • Weight Management
  • Low-Purine Diets
  • Integrative Supplementation
  • Nutritional Rehabilitation

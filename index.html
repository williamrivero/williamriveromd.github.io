How the counter ticks. No registry feed exists for live CKD or dialysis numbers. Each row uses the latest published baseline (ISN/KDIGO 2023 for prevalence; NKTI/Philippine Renal Disease Registry 2024 for dialysis census) and extrapolates forward at the observed annual growth rate, ticking once per second. So the climb you see is the projected pace — the underlying counts are real, the second-by-second motion is arithmetic. Sobering, not a gimmick: this is roughly how many people this library is trying to reach.

Why This Library

Free, physician-written, and built for real life here

No content mills. No paywalls. No ads. Just clear, honest kidney education — kept current.

Written by one doctor, personally.

Every guide is written by Dr. Rivero, a practising nephrologist — not outsourced, not spun out by a content farm. If it's on this site, he wrote it and stands behind it.

Traceable to the guidelines.

Recommendations are anchored in current KDIGO, ADA, and ACC/AHA guidance — and every guide shows a visible "Last Reviewed" date, so you always know how fresh it is.

Built for the Philippines.

The medicines on your prescription, the food at the palengke, PhilHealth and PWD benefits, dengue and lepto season, El Niño heat and typhoons — this library is written for the system and the life you actually live, not a Western pantry.

Free, in four languages.

English, Tagalog, Cebuano, and Kapampangan — because language should never stand between a family and understanding.

A Note From The Author

Why I built this

I'm a nephrologist in daily practice, and I kept meeting patients who left the clinic frightened and under-informed — not because they weren't smart, but because no one had the time to explain. This library is my attempt to give that time back: honest answers, in plain language, in the language you think in. New guides go up regularly. If you spot an error or want to reach out, I'd genuinely like to hear from you.

— William Gregory M. Rivero, MD, FPCP, DPSN
